## Decision

Solenoid is a visual computation graph: calculations are built by wiring cards (nodes) on a canvas rather than typing formulas into cells. It scales from a quick scratchpad for a few scalar operations up to the table work people do in Excel.

## Why

The founding product identity. Every other decision exists to make this a great computational tool at both ends of that range.
