## Decision

Solenoid is a visual computation graph: calculations are built by wiring cards (nodes) on a canvas rather than typing formulas into spreadsheet cells. The domain is data tables and the everyday computation people do in Excel.

## Why

The founding product identity, stated across CLAUDE.md and DESIGN.md. Every other decision exists to make this a real, usable alternative to a spreadsheet.
