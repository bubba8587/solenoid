## Decision

Every value keeps its type and (where dimensional) its unit as it flows through the graph. The type/unit is a property of the value, and mismatches surface as errors rather than plausible-but-wrong numbers.

## Why

The correctness differentiator over Excel (the flagship unit-control feature). Excel silently coerces; Solenoid refuses to, which is what makes its answers trustworthy.
