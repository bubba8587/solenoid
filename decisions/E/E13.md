## Decision

The DATE(year, month, day) builder node is titled "DATE (Build)". The parenthetical tells it apart from Date Input rather than hinting at its use, so it is allowed under [[D22]] oneNamePerCard, and it fits [[D23]] capsClaimsFunction because DATE is the callable token and the parenthetical is Title Case.

## Why

The author's call, restoring an earlier explicit name that a [[D22]] sweep had trimmed to "DATE": on a canvas beside a Date Input card, a bare "DATE" and "Date Input" would differ only by letter case.

## Consequences

**Reopen if:** Date Input is renamed.
