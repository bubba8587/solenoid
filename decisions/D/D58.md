## Decision

Reference capture resolution `REF = 1` (CSS `zoom` on the clone), so the clone lays out at true 1×. The crisp escape hatch is `live` mode (`window.__hcLive`), which re-rasterizes at the exact CTM per frame.

## Why

REF>1 supersamples for zoom-in crispness BUT rounds text line-boxes differently than the live DOM: a measured ~0.9px text drift. At REF=1 zooming past 100% softens (an upscaled capture), the accepted trade for faithful text and alignment.
