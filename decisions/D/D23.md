## Decision

**MUST:** a catalog leaf label or a `NODE_OPS` op label written in all capitals (dotted ones such as `STDEV.S` included, and each token of an "X / Y" enumeration counted on its own) is one of two things:
- a formula-callable name, present in `formulaFunctionNames()` (the Excel and Solenoid dispatch set); or
- a refused frame verb, that is, a `FRAME_SURFACE_NAMES` key: a real Excel function whose typed form redirects to this very node, which is how the frame nodes for Excel's GROUPBY and PIVOTBY carry those spellings.

Every other label is Title Case. Two allowlisted exceptions: the acronym proper nouns PCA, BMI, TDEE, SVG and KPI, and the Solenoid-only op ISNULL (a sibling of the callable IS checks), stay all-caps though they don't dispatch; and the flagship `Convert` leaf stays Title Case though `CONVERT` dispatches.

## Why

The label is a card title ([[D22]] oneNamePerCard), so an all-caps `IS.TEST` or `REGEX` reads as a function the user can type when they can't, and a Title Case `Transpose` hides one they can. Letter case is the signal.
