## Decision

**MUST:** every `registerInternal` name has an `EXCEL_IMPL_META` entry declaring its `returns` and `arity`, plus `rank` and `listArgs` where they apply. Routing, rank checks and list handling are derived from that entry and declared nowhere else ([[D4]] noManualList).

## Why

A registration with no declared contract leaves its routing to be guessed or kept by hand in a second list, which is the drift [[C8]] declareOnce exists to stop.

## Consequences

- *Exception:* the positional lookups (`XLOOKUP`, `XMATCH`, `INDEX`) declare meta but not `listArgs`. They are routed by `RANGE_POSITIONAL`, which skips the error scan, and the flag would reroute them. **Removed by:** unifying the two routing declarations.
