## Decision

**MUST:** every element-wise surface (the operators, unary minus, percent and function broadcasting) goes through `mapCells`. The broadcast rules live in one table (`archive/17-matrix-formulas.md` Part 2), transcribed row for row into `broadcastRules.test.ts`, so changing either one without the other fails.

## Why

Two broadcasters are how the same expression ends up answering differently depending on where it's typed, which is the drift the parity program exists to close. Tying the table to the test is [[D7]] oneMetricImpl's pattern, applied to semantics instead of a metric.
