## Decision

`parseDate` refuses relative phrases (today / next friday / in 3 days / a bare weekday with no year) unless called with `{ relative: true }`; the ONLY caller that does is the Date Input node, and only under Settings ▸ Data ▸ Relative dates (default off). It re-resolves on every pass and fires a warning Alert when the resolved DAY moves between calculations (edge on the serial). A text with a four-digit year is never relative ("Monday, 16 March 2026" is absolute).

## Why

Excel purity and "a stored date is a fixed calendar day": DATEVALUE, Cast, Frame/Table columns and every other date surface stay deterministic, so a document never changes its answers because the day changed unless the user asked for it (2026-08-23).

## Consequences

- **Where:** `dateSerial.ts` (`isRelativeDateText`, `ParseDateOptions`), `control.ts` DateInputNode, `settingsStore.ts` relativeDates; pinned in `relativeDates.test.ts`.
- **Reopen if:** the author wants DATEVALUE (or a frame column) to follow the setting; then the opt-in moves from the node to the parser call sites, the alert story with it.
