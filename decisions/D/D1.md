## Decision

One non-persisted forceDemoVault(on) resolver override (in demoVault.ts) pins getVaultRoot and getCsvFolder to the demo vault. The marketing pages turn it on during render (before scene children mount) and off on unmount, without touching or persisting the user's settings.

## Why

The scenes (C2) must read the demo vault (C1) regardless of the visitor's saved setting; a persisted setting flip would leak into their real app. One switch keeps the vault root and data folder in lockstep.
