## Decision

Discrete zoom steps (a wheel notch, the zoom pill, keys, fit) land on a 10% lattice (`ZOOM_SNAP`); a continuous gesture, the pinch, never snaps mid-drag, and the next discrete step rounds back onto the lattice.

## Why

A mid-drag snap moves the canvas in 10% jumps under the fingers (author rulings 2026-08-26 and 27); a lattice for discrete steps keeps the readout tidy and the steps reproducible. Two touch contacts are the only gate ([[C92]] pinchUnvetoable), so a touchscreen laptop pinches smoothly too.

## Consequences

- The wheel carries unsnapped zoom across events so a trackpad glide of tiny deltas still climbs to the next step.
- **Reopen if:** the lattice step changes with zoom level (a log lattice), which is a curve change, not a gating one.
