## Decision

A composite in a heavy run mode, meaning any mode with a Solve button (Manual or Query, Goal Seek, Monte Carlo, Scenarios, Data Table, By-Row, Simulation), reads blank and stale until the user clicks Solve or Refresh. It never solves on load, paste, create, or a switch into a heavy mode. `isHeavyMode()` draws the line; the plain `single` mode stays fully live.

## Why

The author's call: people expect a heavy composite to sit blank so they can watch it compute when they press Solve. Solving on load would also stall opening a document.

## Consequences

- **Reopen if:** the author wants a mode to compute on load again.
