## Decision

Semantics live in shared helpers (the broadcasters, `forAggregate`), never re-derived per surface. The one sanctioned formula-vs-node divergence: reduction contexts skip nulls (formula AND and OR, the Aggregate family, Excel range semantics), while element-wise contexts use Kleene logic and propagate nulls (BooleanOp, IF, operators, SQL WHERE).

## Why

[[C17]] shareImpl applied to semantics: a helper re-derived per surface answers differently per surface. The one sanctioned split follows Excel's own range semantics for reductions and three-valued logic for element-wise contexts ([[C24]] arraySemantics).

## Consequences

Any other node-vs-formula disagreement is a bug against this decision.
