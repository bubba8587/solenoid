## Decision

**MUST:** an eliminated Excel name (currentExcelParity) resolves to a `#NAME?` redirect naming the
current function, is dropped from autocomplete and highlighting, and gets no range
routing. The blocklist is DERIVED from `LEGACY_ALIASES`, not hand-pruned.

## Why

currentExcelParity: parity means current Excel on every surface. A blocked spelling that was still shaped or routed could compute, or fail with an argument error, before answering its redirect; answering first keeps the gate total.
