## Decision

A Convert whose from- and to-units measure different things answers `#N/A` for the whole output, not a per-cell `#UNIT!`: a bad dropdown pick is the node's condition, not a value's.

## Why

`#N/A` is what IFNA/ISNA catch and what Excel's CONVERT answers; a per-cell `#UNIT!` would be consistent with the algebra's dimension clashes but uncatchable by the functions users reach for, and it would blame the data for the pick.

## Consequences

- Every other dimension clash stays `#UNIT!` at the cell ([[C25]] firstClassUnits).
- **Reopen if:** Convert grows per-cell units (a mixed-unit list), when the clash becomes the cell's.
