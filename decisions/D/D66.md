## Decision

The engine has two precision modes chosen on the card, not one granularity with a display snap: Days (whole working days, FS lag 0 means the next working day, Finish − Start + 1 = Duration) and Minutes (Project's 08:00–17:00 intervals, same-afternoon FS starts). Finish is inclusive on its cell in both; a Minutes-mode midnight finish displays on the previous day.

## Why

A display-only snap shows two tasks on one day and reads as an overlap; snapping inside a single engine breaks MSPDI parity on hour durations. Two honest modes are cheaper than one dishonest one.

## Consequences

- Mode is part of the plan's meaning, so it persists with the document and rides the figure payload.
- **Reopen if:** a third precision (shifts, seconds) is asked for; it is a mode, not a snap.
