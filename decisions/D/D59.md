## Decision

Each paint's captured nodes pack into one atlas region; the whole paint is snapshotted with ONE canvas read-back, and per-node textures come from bitmap→bitmap crops (`createImageBitmap(atlas, x, y, w, h)`, no canvas read-back).

## Why

Snapshotting each node's raster with its own `createImageBitmap(canvas, region)` is a canvas read-back PER NODE (~16 per paint), the expensive pattern on mobile GPUs. The shelf packer exists solely to collapse them.
