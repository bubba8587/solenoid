## Decision

**MUST:** logicals are a real type with three-valued logic (a null operand yields the
Kleene answer, not `false`), and `logical ↔ number` is the one cross-family bridge
(`noAutoCross`).

## Why

Excel treats TRUE/FALSE as 1/0, so logical↔number is the one bridge; but a null operand is unknown, not false, and answering false would invent a result (arraySemantics).
