## Decision

`DEFAULT_ZOOM_SETTLE_MS = 420`: the HTML-canvas layer holds its gesture state that long after the last zoom event before restoring the live DOM at the new scale. `window.__zoomSettle = <ms>` overrides live (read at timer-set time).

## Why

A scale change REPAINTS the visible DOM at the new raster scale while a translate only re-composites, and wheel zoom is notchy with no held-pointer signal: a short timer would exit and re-enter the gesture PER NOTCH and pay that repaint repeatedly mid-zoom.

## Consequences

- A LONGER settle is NOT the lever for choppy zoom: ruled out by a deployed 3000 ms A/B (dev-notes archive).
