## Decision

**MUST:** every size read in the movement stack (align, autofit, Tidy, expand push, standoffs, splice, focus and fly-to) goes through `measuredBox()` in `nodeSize.ts`. It tries React Flow's measure first, then the live rendered size, then the ResizeObserver mirror, and last a non-zero default that knows whether the node is collapsed. No feature reads `offsetWidth || node.width || 100` on its own.

## Why

The separate reads disagreed exactly where it mattered. Before first paint, an unpainted member read as zero, which shrank the bounding box to its corner. On a collapsed group the stored size is the taller expanded one, and OutlinePanel, which tried the sources in the opposite order, once centered the camera on a collapsed group's expanded box.

## Consequences

- A source sweep enforces it: a direct `offsetWidth` or `offsetHeight` read in a movement-stack module outside `nodeSize.ts` fails unless the line above carries `[[D64]] exception:` and its reason ([[C5]] exceptionsUnderRule). Five exceptions stand, each the same ladder with a different last step, and each goes away when `measuredBox` accepts a fallback from its caller:
  - `zoomAt.frameSize` takes a narrower surface, so callers outside the flow view can frame a node.
  - The expand push reads an expanding group at its stored size.
  - The expand push reads a collapsed card by its layout formula.
  - Group containment falls back to the stored box.
  - A docked FC's caller supplies the fallback.
- **Reopen if:** React Flow's measure becomes reliable before first paint, which would collapse the ladder to one step.
