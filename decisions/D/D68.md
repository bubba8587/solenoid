## Decision

When an imported plan has a field the model cannot express (working-time exceptions, finish-no-earlier-than (FNET) and must-finish-on constraints, level-of-effort (LOE) tasks, unusual weekend patterns, rubber links), the reader lists that field by name in `unsupported` and still returns a plan. Such a field is never silently dropped and never becomes a new column.

## Why

A named divergence gets triaged; a hidden one is a wrong schedule that looks right. This is the border that the reopen clause of [[C70]] oneScheduleRule points to.

## Consequences

- The divergence corpus (`docs/v2.0/25-gantt.md` § 3.2) is the list of what a reader must name.
- **Reopen if:** a named field turns out to be common enough to earn a column. That is a change to C70, not to an importer.
