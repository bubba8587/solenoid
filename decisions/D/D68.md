## Decision

An imported field the model cannot express (working exceptions, FNET, must-finish-on, LOE tasks, odd weekend patterns, rubber links) is listed by name in `unsupported`, and the reader still returns a plan. It is never silently dropped and never becomes a new column.

## Why

[[C70]] oneScheduleRule's own reopen clause names this border. A named divergence gets triaged; a hidden one is a wrong schedule that looks right.

## Consequences

- The divergence corpus (25-gantt.md § 3.2) is the list of what a reader must name.
- **Reopen if:** a named field turns out to be common enough to earn a column, which is a C70 change, not an importer one.
