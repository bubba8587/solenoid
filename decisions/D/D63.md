## Decision

A position-locked group sits out every layout pass as a fixed obstacle. Global Tidy and Cleanup never re-place it or carry its members; the fresh arrangement is separated off it (a pinned box never moves, its partner yields); every standoff solve pins it so the correction falls entirely on the other end. Its own within-group Tidy still works: the lock is about the box's corner, not its interior.

## Why

A lock that a layout pass could move is not a lock. Making it an obstacle rather than an exemption is what keeps the rest of the layout consistent: an exempt box that the arrangement lands on would leave an overlap nothing owns.

## Consequences

- `setGroupLocked` (groupLogic.ts) is the one entry point; `withLockedGroupsPinned` feeds every solver call.
- **Reopen if:** the lock should also freeze the members' interior, or users expect a locked group to still take part in Tidy as a movable unit.
