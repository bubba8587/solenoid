## Decision

**MUST:** every own field of every catalog node is one of three things:
- captured by `extractInit` (the whitelist, the literal maps, or a bespoke extras block);
- transient by naming convention (`cached*` for derived display state, `_*` for private runtime machinery); or
- listed in the `DELIBERATELY_TRANSIENT` map (`persistenceSweep.test.ts`) with the reason it must not persist.

A field in none of these is an unmade decision, and the sweep fails, naming it.

## Why

The round-trip sweep ([[C29]] plainJsonInit) proves that whitelisted fields survive, but it can't see a field the whitelist never captured: both sides omit it identically, so the test passes while the user's setting silently resets on every reload. The first triage of the unclassified fields found exactly that bug: `asofDirection`, the direction dropdown of an as-of Join, reset to "backward" on every save, reload and paste.
