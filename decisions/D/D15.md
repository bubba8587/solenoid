## Decision

**MUST:** the untyped ladder is `any` (scalar) → `anycombo` (0-or-1-D) → `anylist` (1-D) → `anytable` (2-D) → `anydata` (rank ≤ 2) → `trueany` (adopts anything). The two strict rank-bearing rungs, `anylist` and `anytable`, keep their rank and adopt only the element family. The elastic rungs, `any`, `anycombo` and `anydata`, adopt the wired type as is (`adoptTypeForBase`, `RANK_BASES` in the sweep).

## Why

A wildcard that forgot its rank would let a list-shaped port take a matrix, and any untyped hop would become a way around [[D13]] widenNeverNarrow.

## Consequences

- `trueany` is the hollow ring. It adopts the wired type, never drops cables and is never saved; it is worked out again after load.
- INDEX takes its element family from its container, including a Frame column when `frameShapeResolver` can know it ahead of time. A Cube cell stays `trueany`, because a Cube is the one container whose cells can differ within a column.
- **Where:** `sockets.ts`, `trueAnyAdopt.ts`, `specs/socket-lattice.md`, `docs/socket-reference.md`.
- **Reopen if:** none foreseen.
