## Decision

**MUST:** failures flow as a tagged `SolError`. `ISERROR` ⟺ `IFERROR`; the `#N/A` test is
centralized as `isNaError`. A bare `NaN` is not an error.

## Why

Two notions of error make ISERROR and IFERROR disagree about the same cell, and a bare NaN counted as an error in one place and a number in another is a silent wrong answer ([[C24]] arraySemantics).
