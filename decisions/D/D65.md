## Decision

The schedule engine and the Gantt layout compute on Excel serials with integer day arithmetic and never construct a `Date`: serials convert once at the border (import, display) and are never compared as floats.

## Why

Every surveyed Gantt library carries the DST off-by-one-hour class of bug because it schedules in `Date`; integer serials in a unit-index space cannot have it. It is [[C44]] dateSerials applied where it matters most.

## Consequences

- Minutes mode works in whole minutes inside the day, still on the serial.
- **Reopen if:** a time-zone-aware schedule is wanted (then the border grows a zone, and the engine still does not).
