## Decision

When `obsidianVault` is blank, `getVaultRoot()` is the demo vault; when `taskNotesUrl` is blank, the TaskNotes node reads the bundled demo replies. The `useDemoVault` setting and the marketing pages' `force*` switches still turn the demo on over a real setup; nothing turns it off below one. An Obsidian seed therefore carries no snapshot input beside its live reader.

## Why

B2 says the web is look-and-try and readers must work with no filesystem; C1 gave them a demo vault behind a setting nobody new has found. Every Obsidian seed shipped a snapshot Frame/Cube Input next to its live node so it would compute somewhere, which doubled the nodes and told the reader the live one was optional. With the demo as the unconfigured default, the seeds wire the live node only, the first open of the app shows real notes and tasks, and configuring a vault or a URL replaces the demo the moment it is set.
