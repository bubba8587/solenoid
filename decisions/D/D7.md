## Decision

**MUST:** a number that gates CI is computed in one module. The human-readable report and
the ratchet test call the same function. Neither recomputes it.

## Why

A report that measures differently from the test is how a ratchet silently stops ratcheting.
The rule was a comment first and was broken twice in one session anyway: the report matched a
leaf by its host label and listed nine registered `FILL*` functions as a gap, and a gap computed
as `!inFormula` let `SCAN` drop out of a gap it was still in. See [[D8]] oneThingPerMetric.
