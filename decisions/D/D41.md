## Decision

**MUST:** a value's display format flows downstream, its unit does not, and the format survives only an op that keeps the value's meaning.

- **What carries.** The format half of an annotation (style, precision, grouping, negatives, scale, text attributes, logical show-as) passes through a transform only where that transform declares its current op preserves meaning (`formatCarry`). `makeAnnotationResolver.compute` reads `formatCarry()`, duck-typed like `passthrough()` with its per-op mapping in the node's op table, and carries the first wired, annotated input of the same element family. A transform with no declaration for an output carries nothing.
- **Examples that carry:** add and subtract (5% + 3% is a percent, a date + days is a date); mean, min, max, stdev, moving windows and EWMA; abs, round, mround and clamp; EDATE and WORKDAY keep a date.
- **Examples that don't:** multiply, divide, power and mod; count, variance and product; z-scores and correlation; every finance output (NPV, a rate, CAGR, Sharpe); a wildcard Expression; and date − date or NETWORKDAYS, which give a span, not a date. A change of family, like number to text, carries nothing.
- **Passthroughs** (Sort, Reverse, Slice, Filter, INDEX, TRANSPOSE) aren't transforms and forward the format unchanged.
- **The unit stays on the value.** The carried format has `unit: "none"`; the unit rides its `UnitCell` or breaks at the transform on its own ([[D40]] unitOnValue). So multiplying $10 keeps the $ and drops the 2-decimal format.
- **Overrides.** A nearer FC overrides what it inherits. A docked FC can also decline: its style dropdown's leading `—` pick (`inheritFormat`) carries the upstream format through and sets only the FC's unit (`FormatControllerNode.resolveAnnotation`), so an FC docked just for a unit doesn't reset the style to Auto.
- **Convert drops the format.** It sets a new unit and rescales the number, so precision chosen for the old unit no longer fits.
- **Upstream reads stay bounded.** A box reading a trailing FC (`downstreamAnnotation`) stops at a transform, because a format chosen after a transform says nothing about the value before it.
- **Frames.** A Frame's per-column format rides `FrameColumn.format` on the value, like the column's unit. The coercion wrapper's output step stamps it from the producing node's `frameFormatStore` picks, and the nearer node's pick wins. It is worked out on every compute and never saved; the store is the one saved home.

## Why

*Why (author, 2026-09-04):* "Generally, we want formatting to carry down the stream if
possible, but it's always possible to override. Units are LOCKED in contrast." *(author,
2026-09-13, formatCarryPerOp):* "if possible" means a MEANING-preserving op — a percent's
NPV is not a percent, an integer 7 ÷ 2 is not 4 — so a transform now carries per its
declared op, not through everything.

Carrying the first annotated input through any transform gave wrong displays: NPV of a 5% rate showed `123,456.00%`, integer `7 ÷ 2` showed `4`, and `date − date` showed a date.
