## Decision

Solenoid adopts DTE (tools/dte.py): decisions live as a ring tree under decisions/, artifacts carry dte:ID citations, and validate/blast/trace are available. The existing docs/rules.md (normative MUST+test) and docs/decisions.md (why/reopen-if) are NOT replaced; over time their entries are lifted into the tree and cited from it. CLAUDE.md carries the agent protocol.

## Why

A4 says break freely; DTE's blast-radius is the safety net that makes that safe, and A1 (a real, maintainable product) needs decision provenance. Adopted incrementally: coverage starts near zero and only rises.
