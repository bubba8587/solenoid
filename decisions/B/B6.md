## Decision

Where the author judged Excel lacking, Solenoid departs from it on purpose. The headline cases: every value keeps its type and (where dimensional) its unit as it flows through the graph, and mismatches surface as errors rather than plausible-but-wrong numbers.

## Why

[[A1]] needs answers people can trust. Excel silently coerces types and has no units; Solenoid refuses to, and unit control is a flagship feature. [[B5]] already requires every divergence to be documented, so the two do not contradict: [[B6]] says which divergences are deliberate.

## Alternatives considered

### Contest 2026-09-15 by Claude Opus 5: keep wins

Rubric: [[A1]].
- keep: deliberate divergences where Excel falls short (typed values, first-class units). Cost: lattice, unit algebra, documented parity:false entries.
- opposite: full Excel fidelity including silent coercion and no units. Produces plausible wrong answers, which a calculator people trust cannot ship.
- deletion: [[B5]] alone would argue for copying Excel's coercions everywhere.
- variant: divergences opt-in per document. Two semantics to build, test and explain, for no user who asked.
Verdict: keep.
