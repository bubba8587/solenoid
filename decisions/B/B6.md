## Decision

Where the author judged Excel lacking, Solenoid departs from it on purpose. The headline cases: every value keeps its type and (where dimensional) its unit as it flows through the graph, and mismatches surface as errors rather than plausible-but-wrong numbers.

## Why

A1 needs answers people can trust. Excel silently coerces types and has no units; Solenoid refuses to, and unit control is a flagship feature. B5 already requires every divergence to be documented, so the two do not contradict: B6 says which divergences are deliberate.
