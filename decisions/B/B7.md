## Decision

While Solenoid has one user, old saves, code, and names are broken freely and deleted rather than preserved: no shims, aliases, or migration maps. The save-format version field plus a forward-refusal guard are the only concession.

## Why

A single-user pre-alpha pays no compatibility tax; the clean change plus updated seeds/tests beats a preserved legacy path. Flips the day there is a real external user.

## Consequences

- Break saves, code and names freely; delete rather than preserve, and update the seed JSONs and tests in the same change (this node absorbs the noBackCompat decision).
- **Where:** the save-format `v` field and the forward-refusal guard stay: the seam migrations would attach to.
- **Reopen if:** the first real external user; this flips hard that day.
