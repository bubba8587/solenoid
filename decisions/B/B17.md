## Decision

A value on a cable is a typed, self-describing thing, and the type is part of its meaning: a socket is a family × rank; null and per-cell errors are first-class; a unit is a property of the value; a date is a real serial; a figure is a value like any other; a column, not a cell, is the unit of definition. Nothing coerces silently: a connection that would change a value's meaning is refused or made explicit.

## Why

[[A6]] divergeByDesign names Excel's silent coercion, its unit-blindness and its per-cell logic as the frustrations the product exists to fix. They are one fix, not several: each is a place where Excel lets a value mean less than it says. A calculator people trust has to carry what a value IS along with what it holds, and every family under this node is that promise applied to one kind of value.

## Consequences

- The families here: the socket lattice ([[C10]] socketLattice), arrays and errors ([[C24]] arraySemantics), units ([[C25]] firstClassUnits), dates ([[C44]] dateSerials), figures ([[C100]] chartIsAValue), the column as the definition ([[C54]] noPerCellFormulas), the one typed escape hatch ([[C66]] scriptNode), and a fill that answers null rather than guess ([[C102]] gridFillThenForecast).
- Every new node is kept type-honest and unit-honest; a node that cannot say what it emits emits `any` and adopts.
- **Reopen if:** nothing (this is [[A6]]'s core made concrete); a family may move under a sibling B node if the ring grows one.
