## Decision

Functions carry Excel's names and produce Excel's results, so a user's existing formula knowledge transfers directly. Divergences from Excel are documented (parity:false + a note), never silent.

## Why

A1 needs a great computational tool, and most users arrive knowing Excel. Parity is the on-ramp that lets them adopt Solenoid without relearning; it serves A1 rather than standing beside it. Parity is judged by name and answer. Deliberate divergences are B6's, not exceptions to this.
