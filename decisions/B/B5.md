## Decision

Functions carry Excel's names and produce Excel's results, so a user's existing formula knowledge transfers directly. Divergences from Excel are documented (parity:false + a note), never silent.

## Why

[[A1]] needs a great computational tool, and most users arrive knowing Excel. Parity is the on-ramp that lets them adopt Solenoid without relearning; it serves [[A1]] rather than standing beside it. Parity is judged by name and answer. Deliberate divergences are [[B6]]'s, not exceptions to this.

## Alternatives considered

### Contest 2026-09-15 by Claude Opus 5: keep wins

Rubric: [[A1]].
- keep: Excel parity by name and answer as the on-ramp. Cost: the parity program, divergence catalogue and legacy-name gate.
- opposite: Solenoid-native names and semantics. Every Excel user relearns; [[A1]]'s table end loses its arriving audience.
- deletion: [[A1]] does not say whose function vocabulary to use; re-argued per function.
- variant: parity by name only, answers free to differ. A familiar name returning a different answer is a trap worse than a new name.
Verdict: keep.
