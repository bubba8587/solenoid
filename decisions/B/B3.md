## Decision

The node designs are exact and are never re-drawn: anywhere a node appears outside the canvas (the landing and /obsidian pages, docs, previews) it is the real component over a real computed rete stack, not a mockup, so it cannot look or behave differently. Marketing chrome is locked to the brand gold regardless of the visitor's app accent.

## Why

A cold visitor should see the true product shape (typed cards and cables, real computed results), and the scenes can never drift from the app because they ARE the app. Locked scenes borrow the process globals for one compute; the page's one live stage keeps them.
