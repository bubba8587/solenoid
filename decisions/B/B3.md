## Decision

A node is never redrawn. Anywhere one appears outside the canvas (the landing and /obsidian pages, docs, previews, the Obsidian plugin) it is the real component over a real computed graph, not a mockup, so it can't look or behave differently from the app.

## Why

A first-time visitor should see the product as it is: typed cards and cables with real computed results. And a scene that is the app can never drift from the app, where a mockup starts drifting the day after it's drawn.

## Consequences

- How a page hosts real canvases is [[C2]] realCanvasScenes; its brand-colored chrome is [[C3]] brandGoldChrome.
