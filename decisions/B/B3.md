## Decision

The landing and /obsidian pages render the actual node components over real (locked or live) rete stacks, computed for real, rather than hand-built DOM/SVG mockups. Chrome is locked to the brand gold regardless of the visitor's app accent.

## Why

A cold visitor should see the true product shape (typed cards and cables, real computed results), and the scenes can never drift from the app because they ARE the app. Locked scenes borrow the process globals for one compute; the page's one live stage keeps them.
