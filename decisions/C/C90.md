## Decision

A drawn cable is annotation, never wiring: it has no sockets, no value, no Conduit run, no ribbon and no engine part, and it never touches `cableShapeStore`. Three author rulings fix its footprint: it stacks above every card, its heading step is 45° only, and it is reached only from Insert (no toolbar button, no hotkey).

## Why

It reuses the wired cables' drawers so it reads as part of the same visual language, and that is exactly why it must be unmistakable as something that is not data flow: nothing about it may look like it carries a value. Its footprint is the author's call, and a minor tool gets no chrome or key of its own.

## Consequences

- Own store, exclusive selection, additive persistence as `SavedGraph.drawnCables`.
- Each drawn cable carries its own shape, arrows, width, head scale and palette color; the toolbar's shape governs wired cables only.
- Every settled edit goes through `commitDrawn()` so it has undo entries like any graph edit.
- The armed tool is modal and screen-space; its geometry, gestures and keys are `specs/drawn-cables.md`.
- **Reopen if:** a drawn cable should tow with the nodes it annotates (it then needs anchors and stops being free).
