## Decision

**MUST:** an alert fires on a status edge (`statusKey`, so a range going from LOW to HIGH fires again, and Boolean mode means `=== 1`), re-arms on the calm edge, and is suppressed while `isGraphRebuilding()`. The post-load recompute runs inside the rebuild scope, so an ungated effect would replay its whole backlog on every document open, switch and rollback.

## Why

Ungated effects misfired in both directions. A Connection node's auto-refresh interval inside a closed composite kept firing full recomputes forever, and switching into a document whose alert condition was already met fired the alert as if it had just happened.
