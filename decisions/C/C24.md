## Decision

Lists/frames carry first-class `null` (missing, skipped by aggregators) AND per-cell `SolError` (propagated), plus a first-class logical type with Kleene 3-valued logic.

## Why

B6: a missing value, an error and a zero are different facts, and a calculator that conflates them answers with plausible wrong numbers. Foundational: every value rule below refines this model.

## Consequences

- **Where:** `valueKinds.ts`, `errorValue.ts`, `subsystem-invariants.md` § Error values; full spec `docs/value-semantics.md` (read "Reading an input" before writing any `data()`).
- **Reopen if:** nothing (foundational).
