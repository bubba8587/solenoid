## Decision

Lists/frames carry first-class `null` (missing, skipped by aggregators) AND per-cell `SolError` (propagated), plus a first-class logical type with Kleene 3-valued logic.

## Why

[[A6]]: a missing value, an error and a zero are different facts, and a calculator that conflates them answers with plausible wrong numbers. Foundational: every value rule below refines this model.

## Consequences

- **Reopen if:** nothing (foundational).
