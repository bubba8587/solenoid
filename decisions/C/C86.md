## Decision

Group membership changes only on an explicit gesture: dragging a node in or out, a select→group action, or a manual box resize over a node. Autofit wraps the box around the existing members and never re-derives membership. A collapsed group never gains a member. Membership is exclusive and stable: a node belongs to at most one group and keeps it while its center is inside, so an overlapping group cannot steal it.

## Why

Deriving membership from geometry after a programmatic resize silently absorbed bystanders whose center the shrunk box happened to cover (2026-06-15). A collapsed group renders as a small card with its members hidden, so a node dropped or created over it joined and then vanished on the next collapse sync (2026-07-07). Both are the same failure: a membership change the user did not ask for.

## Consequences

- The three editors (`reconcileGroupMembership`, `reconcileGroupBox`, `absorbIntoContainingGroup`) each guard the collapsed case; `autofitGroupWithHistory` omits the reconcile.
- A docked FC follows its host's membership and never reconciles on its own.
- **Reopen if:** an explicit "absorb everything under the box" action is wanted (then it is its own gesture, not a side effect).
