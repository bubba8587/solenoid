## Decision

Feature scenes render the real node components over a local rete stack. Locked scenes (SceneStage) borrow the process globals for a single compute then restore them; a page may host ONE live interactive stage (LiveGraph) that keeps the globals, so its overlays (report, table) work.

## Why

[[B3]] requires real product surfaces. Only one editor/engine/view global slot exists per page, so at most one live stage; the rest must be locked and self-contained.
