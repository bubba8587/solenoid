## Decision

As packs and subgraphs add power, keeping the UI obvious is the governing constraint:
- **Signal the exception, not the rule.** Locked is the silent default with no chrome; only an exposable or promoted port gets an accent affordance. Never stamp a lock icon on every locked wire or port.
- **Separate layout from structure.** A subgraph's arrangement can move freely while its wiring stays locked: "can move" is not "can rewire".
- **Convey the mode at the frame, not per element.** "You are viewing locked internals" is said once, as a tinted inner-canvas frame or a mode pill, reusing the existing canvas-lock and layout-pill chrome, never per-wire badges.

## Why

Blender's node graph is the cautionary tale: powerful and illegible. The feature bends to the UI, not the reverse; this is the same reason Conduit reroute nodes exist, so bundles do not turn into spaghetti.

## Consequences

**Where:** the drill-in's state strip (`compositeEditor.css`, a quiet accent cue rather than per-element chrome).
