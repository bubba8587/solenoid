## Decision

Every probability distribution lives on one Distribution card. The `op` picks the distribution (14 of them, from Normal to Negative Binomial), and the `form` argument picks what to compute: CDF, PDF, PMF, a tail, an inverse, Sample and a few others. The first input follows the form (a probability for an inverse form, a count for Sample, otherwise the x), and switching distribution swaps the parameter inputs, with cables on departing sockets pruned first ([[D10]] onePrunePath). The form carries across a switch by meaning (`formAfterSwitch`): PDF and PMF trade places across the line between continuous and discrete, and inverse variants collapse to plain Inverse. Binomial Range stays its own node.

## Why

The author's call ([[B11]] maximalMerge). The first pass merged only the DIST/INV pairs, and the same session then went the rest of the way. Binomial Range stays separate because its range form needs a second x input, a shape the first-input swap cannot describe.

## Consequences

- **Reopen if:** a distribution arrives whose parameters or input shape `DIST_SPECS` cannot describe (a second x input, a non-numeric parameter).
