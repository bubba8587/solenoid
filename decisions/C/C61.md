## Decision

Every probability distribution lives on one card. The `op` selector picks the distribution (14: normal through negative binomial), the arg-tagged `form` field picks CDF / PDF / PMF / a tail / the inverse; an inverse form swaps the first input to Probability, and a distribution switch swaps the parameter inputs (cables on departing sockets prune first, [[D10]] onePrunePath). The forms carry across switches by meaning (PDF↔PMF over the continuous/discrete line, inverse variants collapse to plain Inverse). Binomial Range stays its own node.

## Why

2026-08-09, author call ([[B11]] maximalMerge); the same session first merged only the dist/inv pairs, then went the rest of the way. Binomial Range stays separate because its range form has a two-input shape the first-input swap cannot describe.

## Consequences

- **Reopen if:** a distribution whose parameters or input shape cannot be described by `DIST_SPECS` (a second x input, a non-numeric parameter).
