## Decision

A pack node presents one face (declared input sockets with restriction metadata and defaults, declared outputs, a locked body) with two builds: a simple node (one compiled expression, optionally a variant dropdown) and a composite node, an encapsulated subgraph with a declared boundary built as a real subgraph (`CompositeNode`), not a Group variant. The simple node is the degenerate single-node case of the composite.

## Why

A Group has no sockets, no `data()`, spatial or hybrid membership and an inferred boundary: the opposite of a pack node on every axis, which has a declared contract and computes. Growing from simple to composite must not change how packs appear, how restrictions attach, or how errors surface.

## Consequences

- A simple pack node never materializes boolean-gate or clamp sub-nodes to enforce a restriction; a restriction is data.
- Members run in a private NodeEditor/DataflowEngine; internals never leak into the outer engine or cache.
- **Where:** `nodes/composite.ts`.
