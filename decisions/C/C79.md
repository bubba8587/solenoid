## Decision

Switching a pack off hides its nodes from the Add menu and its formula names from autocomplete, but every pack's constructors stay registered and its formula functions keep resolving, so a saved graph using an inactive pack's node still loads and computes (isolation level 1).

## Why

Level 1 (a clean core with most things dormant) is the outcome the product needs now, and a deactivated pack that turned saved documents into placeholders or #NAME? would be data loss ([[B12]]). The wall between pack and core is drawn so that level 2 (each pack self-contained, enforced by structure) is a tidy-up later; level 3 (third-party packs loaded at runtime) is a safety project of its own, not a code-organization one.

## Consequences

- "Code not loaded when off" is deliberately not built. A required-packs/versions record with an offer-to-enable flow on open is parked with pack distribution (`deferrals.md`) and must land before the first third-party or code pack ships.
- A pack type no build registers at all loads through Placeholder ([[C35]] unknownViaPlaceholder).
