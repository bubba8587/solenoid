## Decision

Groups are React Flow sub-flows: a member is the group's RF child (`parentId`, position relative to the group box, parents first in the node array), so RF tows members with the group and stacks them above it. The MODEL stays absolute; the relative↔absolute conversion lives only at the boundary (`toFlowPosition`/`fromFlowPosition`, `handlers.moveNode`, `onNodesChange`).

## Why

RF's own parenting gives towing and stacking for free, but everything else in the app (saves, Tidy, standoffs, the lasso, docking) reads absolute positions and would each need a conversion. One seam is cheaper than a relative model everywhere, and a moved group re-basing its members at that seam is what keeps a Tidy that translates members before their group consistent.

## Consequences

- A membership rebuild re-projects through the `groupMembershipStore` subscription; during a group drag the model's member positions follow by the group's per-frame delta, selected members excepted (RF moves the selection).
- **Reopen if:** RF drops sub-flows, or the model goes relative for some other reason.
