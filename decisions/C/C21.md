## Decision

**MUST:** a generator that a formula can reach enforces `MAX_GENERATED`, the same shared constant the node uses, and answers `#OVERFLOW!` past it.

## Why

On a node, the count is a spinner the user watches. In a formula field, one typo can ask for ten million elements with nothing on screen to stop it.
