## Decision

The far-zoom simplification class (`html.solenoid-semantic-zoom`, plain CSS does the swap) gates on the RAW CSS scale, threshold 0.3 (`SEMANTIC_ZOOM_SCALE`). Whichever surface is being zoomed owns the toggle (`syncSemanticZoomFor` runs from that surface's viewport drivers).

## Why

Folding dpr in is a measured negative result: it made the gate trigger at a DIFFERENT apparent zoom per display, and apparent size is what legibility depends on. 0.3 is a card drawn at about 30% (a ~200px card becomes ~60px): body text unreadable, the card still a clear block. Conservative (far-overview only) but actually reachable.
