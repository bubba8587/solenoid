## Decision

The gesture model keys on pointer type, never on device class. On touch, a one-finger drag on an unselected card or group pans the canvas and a selected one drags (tap, then drag); mouse and pen keep select-and-drag in one motion. A pen is a precise pointer: it never counts toward a pinch and there is no palm rejection.

## Why

The author's calls: a busy graph leaves no blank pixels to pan from, so on touch every non-actionable pixel of an unselected card is pan surface; and Solenoid is a precise editor, not an inking surface, so nobody rests a palm on it and palm rejection isn't worth the state it needs. Keying on pointer type rather than `IS_MOBILE` is what makes a tablet (desktop chrome, iPadOS desktop UA) behave exactly like a phone under a finger.

## Consequences

- Only discrete controls, sockets and the resize grip veto the touch pan; text fields do not (a tap focuses, a drag pans).
- The qualifying press is stopped at `touchstart`, not only `pointerdown`, because d3-drag binds there; the tap's click is never prevented so tap-select survives.
- Touch-select mode holds RF's multi-selection flag and kills the card pan on unselected cards.
- **Reopen if:** a drawing-style surface lands in the app; it would want contact geometry (`width`/`height`/`pressure`), not a pen-active flag.
