## Decision

**MUST:** a React component extracts a pure helper instead. `data()` assumes the
engine-driven `coerceInputs` wrapper has run.

## Why

`data()` assumes the engine-driven `coerceInputs` wrapper has run; called from a component it sees raw inputs and computes a different answer than the engine shows (reactFlowView: the model and engine are headless, the view only renders).

## Consequences

*Enforced by:* `sourceInvariants.test.ts` → "no component source calls .data(" — a
source scan over `components/`.
