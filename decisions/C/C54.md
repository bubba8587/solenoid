## Decision

One definition per column, living on the column header; no surface may accept a formula typed into a grid cell.

## Why

[[A6]]: Excel's per-cell freedom is the disease this surface cures; a column whose cells can each hold a different formula cannot be read from its header. Dead, not deferred, same class as currentExcelParity.
