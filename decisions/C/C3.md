## Decision

The .sol-landing wrapper overrides the four --accent* vars to the brand coil gold. Only chrome reading --accent retints; the scene canvases' node and socket colors ride their own vars, untouched.

## Why

B3: the pages are Solenoid's storefront, not the visitor's workbench, so they present the brand accent regardless of what accent the user picked in the app.
