## Decision

**MUST:** the two-slot autosave pair obeys three laws:
- it always writes the older slot and reads the newer one;
- a slot's `seq` is a strictly increasing counter kept for the session, never a raw clock reading;
- `seq` is the first key of every slot payload.

## Why

The pair exists so that a crash mid-write never destroys the only good copy ([[B12]] losslessSaves), which is why a write always lands on the older slot. The other two laws each close a way the rotation could silently bring back an older write. Two writes in the same millisecond would tie on a clock reading. And freshness is read with a prefix regex rather than a full parse, so a payload with any other key first reads as an empty slot, and the older write wins.
