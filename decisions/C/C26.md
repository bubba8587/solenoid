## Decision

**MUST:** a node's op is a field named `op`, picked with `OpSelect` / `OpToggle`, with its family declared in `NODE_OPS`. Its values are ops, each a formula function and an Add-menu name, and the picker sits at the top of the body and takes the accent. An argument is a parameter of the node's one function: stored under its own name (`side`, `order`, `agg`, `view`, `condition`), picked with `ArgSelect` / `SegToggle`, neutral, in its own row, and a parameter on the formula surface (`SORT(list, index, order)`, `RUNNING(op, list, [window])`, `PADTEXT(text, width, [side])`). Nothing sits in between: no `kind` switch, no `arg` flag, no argument family in `NODE_OPS`, no argument stored as `op`. Spec: `DESIGN.md` § Op pickers.

## Why

The two used to share a field name (`op`), a component (`OpSelect arg`) and a declaration (`kind`), and every seam between them drifted: argument families got the accent (Sort, Drop Blank Rows), pickerless classes declared `argument`, and display families were accented with nothing searchable (Gauge, Proportion). Pad's op field was named `dir`, so PADLEFT and PADRIGHT couldn't be searched. Making the field name the whole classification leaves nothing to keep in sync.
