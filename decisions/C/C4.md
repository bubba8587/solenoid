## Decision

When a comment explains WHY code exists (rationale), move that prose into the governing node's Why section and leave a dte:ID citation in its place. Comments that explain HOW (mechanics, gotchas, non-obvious control flow) stay. The citation is the WHY's pointer; the node is its one home.

## Why

Extends commentMinimalism (comments are last-resort, default is deletion) to the WHY that a bare comment can't hold without drifting: DTE nodes are the third home, neither code nor comment. Raised by the author 2026-09-14; DTE's own docs do not yet say this (see the DTE FEEDBACK log I4).
