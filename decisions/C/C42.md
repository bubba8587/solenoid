## Decision

Cards capture into a mip pyramid of bitmaps drawn during pan/zoom; the DOM surface is the permanent default and the idle state. HIC stays in: it survived the rete cutover and was ported to the RF surface (`HtmlCanvasLayer.tsx`, a Setting gated on `supportsHtmlInCanvas()`).

## Why

The author's call: HIC stays in. It depends on the `CanvasDrawElement` Blink flag, an external dependency, which is why the DOM surface stays the permanent default and HIC is an enhancement.

## Consequences

- The Pixi/WGSL groundwork was deleted on the author's order: exactly two render paths exist, the RF DOM surface and the HIC gesture layer; git has the code. Do not rebuild a third path ([[B10]] reactFlowView).
- Standing risk: the renderer's external flag. Mitigation: the DOM renderer is the permanent default.
- **Reopen if:** the flag stalls; then the DOM stays default, no crisis.
