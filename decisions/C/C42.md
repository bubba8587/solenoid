## Decision

During pan and zoom on a big graph, an HTML-in-Canvas layer draws captured bitmaps of the cards in place of React Flow's DOM, and at rest the DOM is back. The DOM surface is the default and the only idle state; the layer is a setting, offered only where `supportsHtmlInCanvas()` finds the WICG API. Exactly two render paths exist: the React Flow DOM surface and this layer.

## Why

The author's call: HIC stays in. It survived the move off rete and was ported to the React Flow surface. It depends on an experimental Blink flag, which is why the DOM stays the default and HIC is an enhancement, never a requirement. A third path (the deleted Pixi and WGSL groundwork) would be a second renderer to keep in step with every card for no user-visible gain ([[B10]] reactFlowView).

## Consequences

- The pipeline and every knob (the size gate, the zoom settle, the hold zoom, capture resolution, the atlas, the camera sync, clone positioning) are `specs/html-in-canvas.md`. They are tuning, changed with a measurement, not decisions.
- Standing risk: the flag could stall or change. If it does, the DOM stays default and nothing breaks.
- **Reopen if:** the API ships unflagged and the DOM path becomes the one that costs more to keep.
