## Decision

Cards capture into a mip pyramid of bitmaps drawn during pan/zoom; the DOM surface is the permanent default and the idle state. HIC is IN: it survived the rete cutover and was ported to the RF surface (`HtmlCanvasLayer.tsx`, a Setting gated on `supportsHtmlInCanvas()`).

## Why

Author ruling 2026-08-26: HIC is IN. It depends on the `CanvasDrawElement` Blink flag, an external dependency, which is why the DOM surface stays the permanent default and HIC is an enhancement.

## Consequences

- The Pixi/WGSL groundwork was DELETED 2026-08-09 (author order): exactly two render paths exist, the RF DOM surface and the HIC gesture layer; git has the code. Do not rebuild a third path (reactFlowView).
- Standing risk: the renderer's external flag. Mitigation: the DOM renderer is the permanent default.
- **Where:** `HtmlCanvasLayer.tsx`, `htmlCanvasRenderer.ts`, `rasterAtlas.ts`, `domSync.ts`, `zoomSettle.ts`; the settled perf policies are its child nodes (`python tools/dte.py tree --under C42`), with the semantic-zoom gate and GPU budget beside it under reactFlowView.
- **Reopen if:** the flag stalls; then the DOM stays default, no crisis.
