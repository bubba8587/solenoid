## Decision

Dates are real serials with no Excel-1900 model; the default display is `DD-MMM-YYYY` and ISO stays a selectable style.

## Why

B6: Excel's 1900 date system carries the leap-year bug (a 29 February 1900 that never existed). Solenoid does not reproduce a wrong calendar for compatibility.

## Consequences

- **Where:** `nodes/date.ts`.
- Guard: don't "helpfully" reintroduce 1900 compat.
