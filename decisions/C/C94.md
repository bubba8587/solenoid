## Decision

One format model decides what a Format Controller renders. The adopted socket type maps to a FAMILY, and the family gates which controls exist: a control outside the family is hidden in the popup and inert in resolution, never disabled-but-visible and never silently applied. Precision resolves by ONE shared rule across every number style, with no per-style private precision logic. The unit is orthogonal to style: every number style accepts one, and no other family takes one.

## Why

The model replaced a per-style hand-written switch with precision duplicated per case and a popup gated by scattered `isDate`/`isText` checks; each new style or family was a place for the two halves (what renders, what the popup shows) to disagree. Hidden-and-inert rather than disabled-but-visible because absence is not a fact worth showing; the one sanctioned disabled control is the unit dropdown under a lock state, because there the value HAS a unit and the control shows whose it is ([[C25]] firstClassUnits).

## Consequences

- The family table, the control truth table, the advanced tier and the complex rules are `docs/format-model.md`; `formatModel.test.ts` checks the whole `SocketDataType` union against it.
- Frames are `none` in this model: per-column unit and format are a separate representation, not a scalar annotation stretched over a table ([[D43]] unitByGranularity).
- **Reopen if:** a control needs a disabled-but-visible state for a reason other than a lock, or a family needs two precision rules.
