## Decision

The Decision Matrix's Breakdown columns are each criterion's SIGNED contribution (effective × weight / Σ|weight|), summing to the Score. Ranking runs on the ROUNDED (4dp) score so display and rank cannot disagree, and round4 flattens −0. Both decision nodes default normalize to ÷Max. Sensitivity lists every rank-1 option in Winner ("A = B") on an exact tie (Margin 0 ⟺ tie), and #VALUE!s when no Scenarios column names a criterion.

## Why

[[B6]]: each default removes a plausible wrong reading. Bare post-normalize values read backwards under a negative weight (the priciest option showed Price 1.0 while taking the largest penalty); Raw silently degenerates the moment criteria mix scales, which is the node's whole reason to exist; and with no matching Scenarios column every weight defaults to 1 and all scenarios come out identical (the renamed-criteria trap).
