## Decision

`flow/FlowSurface.tsx` is the one surface; the main canvas and the composite drill-in render it over a `SurfaceStack` and differ only through `SurfaceHooks`. Every surface-level behavior (gesture installers, lasso, menus, keyboard, layers) is wired there once; a host may only supply what settles a move, which history answers undo, and what Delete removes.

## Why

Author, 2026-08-26: the main canvas and the drill-in must be equivalent. Two surface components would drift the way any two implementations do (declareOnce).

## Consequences

- **Where:** `subsystem-invariants.md` § React Flow surface contract; the drill-in host `flow/FlowCompositeOverlay.tsx`.
- **Reopen if:** a surface genuinely needs a behavior the other must not have; then the difference is a named hook, never a host-local installer.
