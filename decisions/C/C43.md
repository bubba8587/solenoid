## Decision

`flow/FlowSurface.tsx` is the one canvas surface. The main canvas and the composite drill-in both render it over a `SurfaceStack` and differ only through `SurfaceHooks`. Every surface-level behavior (the gesture installers, the lasso, menus, keyboard handling and layers) is wired there once. A host may supply only three things: what settles a move, which history answers undo, and what Delete removes.

## Why

The author's call: the main canvas and the drill-in must behave the same. Two surface components would drift apart the way any two implementations of one thing do ([[C8]] declareOnce).

## Consequences

- **Reopen if:** a surface genuinely needs a behavior the other must not have. Even then the difference becomes a named hook, never an installer local to one host.
