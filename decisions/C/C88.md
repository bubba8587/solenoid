## Decision

Collapsing a group changes nothing about computation: members stay wired and keep computing. Hiding is a `visibility` class rule on the RF node (never `display`, never the wrapper's inline style). What the collapsed card shows is derived, not chosen: exactly the members whose output leaves the group or dead-ends, as readout rows.

## Why

A hidden member must stay laid out or its measured size and socket positions collapse to zero, which is why `visibility` and not `display`. React Flow owns the wrapper's inline visibility and writes `visible` after measuring, so an imperative element sweep silently lost ("seed loads with members exposed"); the class rule survives. The retained rows are what the group means from outside: its terminals, so a dead-end value counts as a terminal too.

## Consequences

- The retain rule in full (the Display→FC hop, the leaf-member case, no double counting) is `specs/group-collapse.md`; `groupReadouts()` and `recomputeGroupCollapse()` keep it identical (the former serves a pinned group, the latter a collapsed one).
- Any "go to this node" path first resolves the nearest visible ancestor, and frames a collapsed group by its rendered size rather than its stored expanded one.
- **Reopen if:** collapse should also pause compute, or a member's readout should be user-chosen rather than derived.
