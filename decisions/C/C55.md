## Decision

The model emits a full replacement; the strict validator gates the apply; the approval shows the old→new diff. NO edit-op layer.

## Why

aiInScope requires AI edits to take the same validated, approved path as a human's; the text form already is that path. An edit-op layer would be a second grammar and validator with partial-state semantics, pure cost at model-sized documents.

## Consequences

- **Reopen if:** documents outgrow reliable whole-doc regeneration, or the MCP port ships (its typed tools ARE the op layer).
