## Decision

**MUST:** every user-visible fact (a label, a name, an op's identity, an arity) has
exactly one declaration, and every other surface derives from it. Transcribing a value into a
second location is a defect even while the two agree.

## Why

Two copies have nothing keeping them equal, so they are already wrong; nobody has looked yet.
The IS.TEST card once read `ISBOOLEAN` while Add-menu search offered `ISLOGICAL`, so you could
read a name off a card and fail to find it in the menu, because two `OPS` arrays existed, one
in the component and one in `nodeOps.ts`.
