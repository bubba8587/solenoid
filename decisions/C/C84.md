## Decision

Tidy is an ELK layered layout, called directly, that only TRANSLATES nodes. Ports are placed symmetrically (the same offset for inputs and outputs) so two connected nodes line up; the result is anchored to the flow's leading edge plus its cross-axis center; no layout pass leaves a fixed inline size on a card. A global Tidy keeps groups as rigid units and never enters them; a group's own Tidy arranges exactly that group's members.

## Why

The three failures this closes each came from the opposite choice. rete-auto-arrange's `classic` preset put outputs at the top and inputs at the bottom, so every link staircased its target upward and the whole graph read as floating high. Anchoring by the top-left corner dropped a flattened row to the top of the old footprint, the same "nodes float up" symptom from the other side. And its applier stamped an inline height on every card, which froze it: collapse could not shrink it and a grown value clipped. Translate-only keeps cards content-driven, which is what [[C37]] observerOwnsSize already demands of everything else; symmetric ports and leading-edge anchoring are deterministic in either direction, so the Cleanup tidy→autofit cycle is a fixed point.

## Consequences

- The three knobs (direction, density, width cap), the flipped-node-as-predecessor rule and the docked-FC reservation are mechanics of `specs/auto-arrange-tidy.md`, not decisions.
- **Reopen if:** ELK is replaced, or React Flow starts owning card sizes (then the pin-drop guard becomes the wrong tool).
