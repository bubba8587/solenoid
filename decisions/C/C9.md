## Decision

**MUST:** every rule node with a MUST is cited from the test that enforces it, or its
Consequences carry an explicit `*Unenforced:*` line saying why it can't be checked. A bug
fix ships the check that would have caught it. The node never names its tests: the
enforcing list is derived from citations (`dte show`, `dte coverage`).

## Why

*Why:* the difference between a spec and a folk memory is whether anything fails when you
break it. `subsystem-invariants.md` § Pointer gestures carries an honest example of the
alternative — the native-popup pointerdown swallow, kept on drag-prevention grounds after
its originating mechanism died with the rete surface.

## Consequences

The labelling is pinned mechanically (the test cites this node). Whether a citing test
truly enforces the MUST is a reading job (backlog: citation coverage).
