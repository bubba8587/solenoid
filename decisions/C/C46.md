## Decision

When an Excel quirk would split JS from Polars (`0^0`: Excel #NUM!, everyone else 1), the quirk loses, and the deviation is documented (`parity:false`).

## Why

A manufactured engine split is worse than a documented deviation: [[B2]]'s two engines must give one answer, and [[A5]] already requires every divergence to be documented. An ordering of loyalties, not a feature.
