## Decision

Every comparison (`=`, Comparison node, lookup match, Filter text ops) is case-INsensitive (EXACT / "Match case" is the escape hatch). Every identity op (Join/Group By/Distinct keys) is case-SENSITIVE. Corollary (same line): list UNIQUE never dedupes error cells; frame Distinct dedupes them by code.

## Why

[[A5]]: Excel's `=` ignores case, so comparisons do too. Keys are different: silent case-merging destroys distinctions in the data.

## Consequences

- **Reopen if:** nothing; a per-verb case-fold OPTION would be an addition inside the rule.
