## Decision

Every comparison (`=`, the Comparison node, lookup matches, Filter's text ops) ignores case; EXACT and "Match case" are the way out. Every identity op (Join, Group By and Distinct keys) is case-sensitive. On the same line, list UNIQUE never dedupes error cells, while Frame Distinct dedupes them by code.

## Why

[[A5]]: Excel's `=` ignores case, so comparisons do too. Keys are different: merging case silently destroys distinctions in the data.

## Consequences

- **Reopen if:** nothing; a per-verb option to ignore case would be an addition inside the rule.
