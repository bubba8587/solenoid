## Decision

The app never calls `window.alert`, `window.confirm` or `window.prompt`: in-app notices (`noticeStore.ts`) and the confirm dialog (`confirmStore.ts`) are the only interruptions.

## Why

Native dialogs are unreliable in the Tauri WebView and unstyled on the web ([[B2]] webTryDesktopFull needs both to behave the same); an in-app dialog also obeys the modal keyboard gate and DESIGN.md.

## Consequences

- A new "are you sure" goes through `confirmStore`; a new message through `noticeStore`.
- **Reopen if:** a platform requires a native dialog for a permission (file access prompts are the OS's, not ours).
