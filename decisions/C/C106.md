## Decision

The app never calls `window.alert`, `window.confirm` or `window.prompt`. In-app notices (`noticeStore.ts`) and the confirm dialog (`confirmStore.ts`) are the only interruptions: a new "are you sure" goes through `confirmStore`, and a new message through `noticeStore`.

## Why

Native dialogs are unreliable in the Tauri WebView and unstyled on the web, and [[B2]] webTryDesktopFull needs both platforms to behave the same. An in-app dialog also obeys the modal keyboard gate and DESIGN.md.

## Consequences

- **Reopen if:** a platform requires a native dialog for a permission. File access prompts belong to the operating system, not to the app, and do not count.
