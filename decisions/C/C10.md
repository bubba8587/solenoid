## Decision

Element families never auto-cross (Cast required; sole bridge `logical↔number`); values flow freely up in rank (scalar→list→matrix→frame). The lattice is a family × rank product, and its legality rules are DERIVED from that product rather than enumerated per pair.

## Why

[[B6]]: a value's type is part of its meaning, so the socket lattice refuses connections that would silently coerce. Deriving legality from the product keeps every variant consistent without hand-listing pairs.

## Consequences

- **Where:** `accepts()`, machine-checked by the `socketConnect.test.ts` full sweep. Full mechanics: `docs/socket-reference.md`, `docs/subsystem-invariants.md` § Socket lattice.
- **Reopen if:** a new family or a second bridge: re-derive the lattice + sweep, never ad-hoc edit.
