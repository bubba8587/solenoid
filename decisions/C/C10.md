## Decision

Element families never cross on their own: crossing takes a Cast, and the only bridge is `logical ↔ number`. Values flow freely up in rank, scalar → list → matrix → frame → cube. The lattice is a family × rank product, and which connections are legal is derived from that product, never listed pair by pair.

## Why

[[A6]]: a value's type is part of its meaning, so the lattice refuses any connection that would coerce silently. Deriving legality from the product keeps every variant consistent without a hand-kept list of pairs.

## Consequences

- **Reopen if:** a new family or a second bridge. Re-derive the lattice and the sweep; never edit one pair by hand.
