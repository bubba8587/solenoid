## Decision

A chart is a VALUE: the `chart` socket carries a self-describing figure (kind, series, options, structured payload) that is flat and JSON-safe, so it rides a cable, persists, and renders anywhere a value renders (Display, Report embed, popup) rather than being a node-local drawing.

## Why

If the figure were the node's own render, nothing downstream could show, embed or format it, and a Report could not carry a chart the way it carries a number. A flowing figure is what makes the FC's `chart` family ([[C94]] formatFamilyGates), the Report embed and the chart-of-a-chart (Gantt from Schedule) possible with no special wiring.

## Consequences

- Three render paths, chosen by the figure, not the surface: recharts for interactive plots (one lazy chunk, [[C97]] rechartsLazyChunk); plain CSS/SVG cards for structured payloads (KPI, gauge, record views); a supersampled 2D canvas for data-heavy figures, one DOM element regardless of point count, themed from the live CSS vars at draw time.
- The payload is data, never geometry: the Gantt value carries rows, links and non-working spans as serials plus the resolved view keys, and the layout produces pixels at the measured width, because the same figure draws at four widths (chip, Display, popup, Report).
- Options ride the figure as text ([[C96]] chartOptionsAreMatplotlib).
- **Reopen if:** figures need to be interactive edits back into their data (they would then need an identity, not just a value).
