## Decision

Formulas take matrices with Excel dynamic-array semantics, and tagged Cx through the owned IM* family. Formula scope is capped at rank ≤ 2: frames and cubes never enter formulas; the verb engine is the frame/cube surface.

## Why

[[A5]]: dynamic arrays are current Excel, so matrices belong in formulas. Frames/cubes rejected on record: no Excel semantics to copy, they compete with the verb engine, and they break lazy-FrameRef economics. Pressure to widen Expression routes to composites/subgraphs instead. This absorbs the old noFramesInFormulas cap (1-D formulas), which matricesInFormulas superseded.

## Consequences

- **Containment:** Formula.js never sees a matrix or a Cx (`matrixArgs`/`cxArgs` gates, hideMatrixFromVendor); the broadcast table is `broadcastRules.test.ts` (oneBroadcast); element-wise ragged pads null, shape construction pads `#N/A` (appendLadder); a list is a ROW.
- **Reopen if:** the broadcast semantics diverge from Excel in ways users hit; a re-cap would be a new decision, not a silent revert. Nothing at the formula level reopens frames/cubes.
