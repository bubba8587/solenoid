## Decision

Formulas take matrices, with Excel's dynamic-array behavior, and complex numbers through the IM* functions. Nothing wider than a matrix enters a formula: Frames and Cubes belong to the Frame nodes.

## Why

[[A5]]: dynamic arrays are current Excel, so matrices belong in formulas. Frames and Cubes stay out for three reasons. Excel has no formula behavior for them to copy, they would compete with the Frame nodes, and handing a whole table to a formula would load data that a lazy FrameRef never reads. Requests to widen Expression are answered with Composites instead.

## Consequences

- Per-row math on a Frame is a computed column ([[C22]] rowFormulaRefs). Its formula sees one row's cells and whole columns as lists, never the Frame itself.
- Formula.js never receives a matrix or a complex number; the `matrixArgs` and `cxArgs` gates stop them (hideMatrixFromVendor).
- Broadcasting follows the table in `broadcastRules.test.ts` (oneBroadcast). Ragged element-wise math pads with null, and shape-building functions pad with `#N/A` (appendLadder). A List counts as one row.
- The old 1-D cap, noFramesInFormulas, is folded into this node.
- **Reopen if:** broadcasting diverges from Excel in ways users hit. A new cap would be a new decision, not a silent revert. Nothing at the formula level reopens Frames or Cubes.
