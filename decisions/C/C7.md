## Decision

**MUST:** a rule or decision is author-ruled if and only if the author, in a specific session, has read it and marked it themselves. In the tree that mark is `ratified_by` set on the owner's in-session word. Nothing else confers it: not a past author statement, not a recorded decision, not a quote in a node's Why, not the agent's confidence in what the author meant.

## Why

*Why (author, 2026-07-28, verbatim intent):* "99% of what is in this codebase is your assumptions and references you built for yourself unless you recorded that I told you something specific… even if I said something in the past — it's not an author-ruled rule as of right now."

[[B9]] needs the owner's actual rulings distinguishable from agents' inferences, and DTE ratification is exactly that mark ([[B8]]). This was the one author-ruled (ARR) rule in rules.md.

## Consequences

- Every node the owner has not ratified binds only as the working agent's inference or default, open to question on those terms.
- rules.md's grades carry over: an INFERRED rule keeps its forcing incident as Origin in Why; a DEFAULT rule (preventive judgment, no incident, the thinnest ice) has confidence medium and is the first candidate for an enforcing incident or deletion. The grade tracks provenance, not value.
- The authorization pass is the owner walking `python tools/dte.py tree` and ratifying node by node (or telling an agent to run `ratify`); to amend, the owner dictates the amendment first; to reject, the node is superseded or reverted in the same sitting.
