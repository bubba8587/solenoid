## Decision

Diagonal and straight cable modes share one walk-enumeration router (`routeWalk`, parametrised by compass division). Its constraints hold by construction, with no sharper-turn fallback: rigid exit and entry stubs along the socket direction, every other segment on a compass heading, every turn exactly one compass step. Selection is the globally shortest solvable walk, length first; turn count and rotation preference settle only exact ties. Spline is a single tangent-exact cubic.

## Why

The heuristic routers this replaced (`getDiagonalPath`, `getSmoothStepPath`, `withAngleLeads`) needed fallbacks that produced kinks, and any selection that gates on turn count before length turns route handoffs into visible jumps: a walk enters feasibility at exactly the length of its own wider extension, which was already competing, so length-primary selection makes the handoff seamless.

## Consequences

- Property tests machine-check the invariants and the continuity under simulated sub-pixel drags; the remaining discontinuities (near-touching collapse, equal-length swaps) are inherent and listed in `specs/cable-rendering-knobs.md`.
- The renderer's dedup epsilon stays well under a pixel or it deletes real vertices.
- **Reopen if:** cables need obstacle avoidance, which no walk family provides.
