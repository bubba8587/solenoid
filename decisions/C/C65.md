## Decision

React Flow stacks nodes by their `zIndex`, and the area-plane ladder is that data, not DOM order: standoff bars at −3 (drawn by `StandoffLayer`), groups at −2 and conduits at −1 (stamped by `nodeZIndex` in `flowModel.ts`), ordinary nodes at 0. The fixed lifts (isolate endpoints, an open group picker, the selected cable) stay explicit z-index writes.

## Why

A group's body has to sit below its members, or it catches their pointer events; React Flow's own model for that is `zIndex`, so the ladder lives there. It is the rete-era ladder under a new mechanism. The rete surface stacked by DOM order, with `simpleNodesOrder` re-appending the picked card, and that ended with the rete surface ([[B10]] reactFlowView). One side effect went with it: selecting a node no longer moves its DOM element, so the old constraint that such a move closes a native popup (settled by a probe) no longer applies. The `pointerdown` swallow on form controls survives only because it prevents a drag.

## Consequences

- **Reopen if:** cards visibly stack wrongly after selection, or a control needs the last-selected card on top, which React Flow's `zIndex` model does not give.
