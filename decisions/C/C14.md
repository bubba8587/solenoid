## Decision

VLOOKUP, HLOOKUP, LOOKUP, MATCH, the legacy CEILING sign rules and the pre-2010 stats family have no node and no formula; typing one gets a short `#NAME?` pointing at the replacement. INDEX stays, since it was never superseded.

## Why

[[A5]]'s on-ramp is the Excel users have today; carrying superseded functions doubles the surface and teaches the forms Excel itself replaced. VLOOKUP was once rebuilt because this ruling lived only in a parenthetical, which is why cross-surface rulings get their own record.

## Consequences

- **Reopen if:** a real `.xlsx` import ships: auto-rewriting classic lookups then beats erroring.
