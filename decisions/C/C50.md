## Decision

Every LAMBDA consumer (MAP, BYROW, BYCOL, REDUCE, SCAN, MAKEARRAY) binds declared parameters by name, in any order. An unknown parameter is `#VALUE!`. A body variable that is a node variable but isn't declared is flagged on the card (the capture advisory). Captured constants stay explicit sockets you can wire.

## Why

A deliberate break from Excel's positional model (`parity:false`, [[A6]]): on a canvas the wiring order is invisible, so positional binding would silently swap arguments; binding by name makes a mismatch a loud `#VALUE!`.

## Consequences

- **Rejected:** auto-binding reserved names over the wiring, which leaves dead sockets and silently ignores cables.
