## Decision

Every consumer of a wired LAMBDA (MAP, BYROW, BYCOL, REDUCE, SCAN, MAKEARRAY) binds the LAMBDA's declared parameters by name, in any order. An unknown parameter name is `#VALUE!`. A name in the body that matches one of the node's variables but isn't declared is flagged on the card as a likely missing capture. Captured constants stay explicit sockets you can wire.

## Why

This is a deliberate break from Excel's positional binding (`parity:false`, [[A6]]). On a canvas the order of the wiring is invisible, so positional binding would silently swap arguments, while binding by name turns a mismatch into a loud `#VALUE!`.

## Consequences

- *Rejected:* binding reserved names automatically over the wiring. It leaves dead sockets and silently ignores cables.
