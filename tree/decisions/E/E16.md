## Decision

Every cell of a Cube Input's editor, at any level and inside a list, can be switched in place between a value, a List, a Table (rows of values, the app's 2-D shape), a Frame and a Cube: every shape a Cube cell holds, under the names the app already gives them. A Frame is flat, its cells single values; a Cube's cells can hold any of these again. Which of the two a cell is gets declared with it, never guessed from its contents. Switching keeps what it can: a value becomes the first item or cell; a List is a Table's first row and a Frame's or Cube's first column, and back; a Frame's or Cube's rows become a Table's and back, losing the column names; a Cube becomes a Frame with its nested cells blanked, and a Frame a Cube unchanged; switching to a value keeps the first value found.

## Why

[[C28]] literalsIffEditable ties what a literal node saves to what its editor shows, and a Cube holds lists, Tables, Frames and Cubes in its cells ([[D90]] cubeTypesAtDepth), so an editor that can only type a value leaves most of what a Cube is for reachable only by pasting JSON. Keeping the old contents on a switch means a wrong pick is one more pick away from undone.

## Consequences

- Each editing cell has a small menu at its edge naming Value, List, Table, Frame and Cube, with the cell's current kind marked; it shows on hover or focus, and always on a touch screen. A Table's and a Frame's own cells are values, with no menu.
- A new list starts empty (or holding the value that was there); a new Table, Frame or Cube starts as one cell, a Frame's or Cube's column named `Column 1`.
- A Frame cell is a real Frame downstream, typed column by column; records nobody declared a Frame are a Cube, as a note's nested YAML records always read.
- A switch drops the column types declared for tables inside the cell ([[D90]] cubeTypesAtDepth).
- The Solenoid Properties plugin's cube editor gets the same menu, since it shares the editor.
- How it's built: the table-popup spec (Editing a Cube Input).
