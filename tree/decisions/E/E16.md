## Decision

A Cube Input cell can be switched in place to any shape a Cube holds: a value, a List, a Table, a Frame or a Cube. Which shape a cell is gets declared with it, never guessed from its contents, and a switch keeps what it can of what was there.

## Why

A literal node's editor has to reach everything the node saves ([[C28]] literalsIffEditable), and a Cube exists to hold lists and tables in its cells ([[D90]] cubeTypesAtDepth); an editor that could only type a value would leave most of that reachable only by pasting JSON. Keeping the old contents means a wrong pick is one more pick away from undone.

## Consequences

- Records nobody declared a Frame are a Cube, as a note's nested records always read.
