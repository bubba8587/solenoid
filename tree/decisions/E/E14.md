## Decision

The frame nodes for grouping and pivoting are named GROUPBY and PIVOTBY. The formula surface refuses these verbs (`FRAME_SURFACE_NAMES`, since Frames don't flow through formulas), but typing either name redirects to the node, so the all-caps claim holds; the test for [[D23]] capsClaimsFunction admits the map's keys. Frame verbs with no Excel function (Unpivot, Nest, Rename and the like) stay Title Case. Append and Bind Columns stay Title Case too: they match Frames by column name and pad with blanks, which is not what Excel's VSTACK and HSTACK do, so the 2-D XSTACK node carries those spellings ([[C48]] appendLadder).

## Why

The author's call, after a [[D22]] oneNamePerCard sweep had renamed PIVOTBY "Pivot" to match the Title Case verbs: the on-ramp that [[A5]] excelParity promises is the Excel name the user already knows.

## Consequences

**Reopen if:** the formula surface starts accepting Frames.
