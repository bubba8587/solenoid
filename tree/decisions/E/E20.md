## Decision

A date or a temperature reading used as an amount answers #POINT!: two of them added, one scaled, negated or raised to a power, a sum, product or share of them, or a function that takes amounts handed one (SUM(date), ABS(date)). #TYPE! stays for a value of the wrong kind (a number where a date is needed, a date compared with a number) and #UNIT! for units that clash (meters plus seconds, °C beside °F).

## Why

A position is not an amount, and saying so with its own code keeps the rule visible wherever positions appear; scheduling is full of them, and its dates must come from adding durations to dates, never dates to dates.

## Consequences

- #POINT! is Solenoid's own code; ERROR.TYPE reads it as 3, with the other codes Excel lacks.
- Each message names the fix: shift by an amount, subtract two for the gap, or average them.
- Schedule and Gantt arithmetic is written as date plus duration in its own code too, though the engine works on plain day numbers inside.
- Reopen if: another kind of position (a time of day, a map coordinate) needs its own error instead of sharing this one.
