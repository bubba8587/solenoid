## Decision

**MUST:** every place that needs to see through an untyped hop calls `isWildcardType()`. Nothing re-implements "is this socket untyped" locally.

## Why

Three slightly different ideas of "untyped" are three slightly different bugs.

## Consequences

*Unenforced:* a grep check can't separate the two kinds of wildcard comparison. Most comparisons outside `sockets.ts` only classify rendering (glyph shape, combo drawing, wire-only rows), but `passthrough.ts` `agreeTypes` holds a semantic `=== "trueany"` veto, kept there so the socket pass and the display walk can't diverge. So this stays a reading rule.
