## Decision

**MUST:** a positional lookup (INDEX, XMATCH, a MAKEARRAY read) propagates only the error in the cell it actually reads; an error elsewhere in the range doesn't turn the whole answer into an error. An aggregate over a range that contains an error still propagates it, as Excel does.

## Why

This is Excel's model ([[A5]]): a lookup answers from one cell, an aggregate answers for the whole range. Getting either direction wrong is silent. Erroring every lookup hides a good answer, and letting an aggregate skip an error hides a bad one.
