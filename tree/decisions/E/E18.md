## Decision

Beyond the formulas, functions and table verbs ([[D99]] rustIsSpec), the app's other TypeScript moves to the core only where the move buys something: it fixes a wrong answer, it leaves one copy of a rule instead of two, or it runs faster. A piece that would buy none of these stays TypeScript. Slower is acceptable when the cost is imperceptible and the move buys one of the other two.

## Why

The computation already runs on the core and the app was fast before the port; moving code that only orchestrates the editor, the view and the stores costs work and adds a trip across the WebAssembly boundary for nothing.

## Consequences

- The verdicts per group are in [[graph-core]] § Phase E.
- The text form moved although it is slower (about 6.6 ms a save against 2.3 ms on the largest seed): it keeps a save's numbers and key order exactly, and it is one copy.
- **Reopen if:** a piece left in TypeScript turns out to hold a rule the core also holds, or turns out to be slow.
