## Decision

**MUST:** any step that reads a resolved socket type and caches the answer runs after `settleWildcardTypes` on that editor. The FC dock loop is the load-path case: `dockSelf` → `adaptTypeFromConnections` resolves the host socket once, at dock time. On load, `persistence.ts` settles wildcard types before registering FC docks, and any new one-shot consumer of a projected or adopted type goes on the same side of that line.

## Why

Before the settle, a projected wildcard output (INDEX over a Frame) still reads as its upstream's raw type, and a consumer that caches that answer keeps it. A docked FC once adopted `frame` this way, showed no controls, and nothing re-adapted after the settle: a computed column's unit → INDEX → docked FC chain read `frame` after every reload until docked again by hand. The failure is a believable wrong type with no error anywhere, and the apparent fix, re-docking by hand, hides the cause.
