## Decision

Beside the work branches, a branch named bleeding-edge builds the same Solenoid on the newest release of every dependency and the newest web-platform features, to find out what they break and what they make possible before the work branches take them. It never ships and is never merged; what proves itself there moves to a work branch one change at a time.

## Why

An upgrade or a new platform feature is cheapest to judge once it has been tried against the real app: a breakage found on a side branch costs the product nothing, and a feature found working there is ready to port.
