## Decision

Solenoid's product identity is twofold. Yes, it is a visual node-graph spreadsheet app. Yes, the  syntax and behavior chase Excel where possible ([[A5]]). However, it's not just a translation of Excel to a different visual format. The other half of the product is fixing Excel's many gaps and frustrating aspects - sometimes in the ways only a node-graph can.

* A node-graph is better for a workbook with many small tables or very long and complicated formulas that Excel buries inside singular cells. Rearranging a node-graph's calculation order/flow is much easier as well.
* Data types and units are first-class and error-enforced.

## Why

This is simply another aspect of the product's core identity.
## Consequences

- Every deliberate divergence is documented where it shows (parity false, with a note), so [[A5]] and this leaf never contradict.
- Unit control (propagation through the arithmetic, Convert as the one place a unit changes) is a flagship feature, and every new node is kept unit-honest.
- The leaves on which connections are allowed, on blanks and errors in lists, on units, on dates, and on formulas living in columns rather than cells all descend from here.
