## Decision

Solenoid exists for two reasons. One is to be a visual computation tool inside the set of tools people already work across (spreadsheets, knowledge bases, documents, code, databases). The other is to fix what the owner found wrong or frustrating in Excel, on purpose and by design: when there are many tables and long, complex formulas, a node graph is the better model; values are typed, and carry their unit where they have one, so a mismatch is an error rather than a plausible wrong number; and a formula is a visible node on the canvas, never something hidden invisibly inside a cell.

## Why

These are the frustrations that made the owner build the product, so they are core, not a strategy under [[A1]]. [[A5]] keeps Excel's names and answers; this leaf says which departures from Excel are the point of Solenoid rather than gaps in parity. Excel silently coerces types, has no units, and hides its logic in cells, and a calculator people trust cannot do any of those.

## Consequences

- Every deliberate divergence is documented where it shows (parity false, with a note), so [[A5]] and this leaf never contradict.
- Unit control (propagation through the arithmetic, Convert as the one place a unit changes) is a flagship feature, and every new node is kept unit-honest.
- The socket lattice, array semantics, first-class units, date serials and no-per-cell-formulas families descend from here.
