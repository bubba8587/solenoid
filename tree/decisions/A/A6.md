## Decision

Solenoid's product identity is twofold. Yes, it is a visual node-graph spreadsheet app. Yes, the syntax and behavior chase Excel where possible ([[A5]]). However, it's not just a translation of Excel to a different visual format. The other half of the product is fixing Excel's many gaps and frustrating aspects - sometimes in the ways only a node-graph can.

* A node-graph is better for a workbook with many small tables or very long and complicated formulas that Excel buries inside single cells. Rearranging a node-graph's calculation order/flow is much easier as well.
* Values keep their types, units and shape (rows, columns, orientation). These are core promises and value adds. 

## Why

Excel's long formulas buried inside cells, no real unit support, and ambiguity with data types are a user-hostile product gap that we can fill & fix. 

## Consequences

- Type safety, units and shape are flagship features. Every function and every node carry them where possible.
