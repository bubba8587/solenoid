## Decision

**MUST:** when a cell is both an error and missing, the error wins and passes on unchanged. It never shows up as text, as not-a-number, or as garbage like "[object Object]".

## Why

Checking for missing first would drop the error or turn it into text, and a real failure would show up as a blank.
