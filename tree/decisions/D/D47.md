## Decision

**MUST:** two amounts in different currencies never combine. Solenoid has no exchange rates, so a currency's code (USD, EUR) is what tells them apart, and every operation that combines two values with units (arithmetic, comparisons, totals) refuses two different currencies with #UNIT!, multiply and divide included. An amount with no code, such as a computed result, takes the other side's code.

## Why

A currency is known by its code ([[C25]] firstClassUnits), and with no exchange rates two codes can't be reconciled, so any operation that combined them would invent a number: even $10 ÷ 5€ = 2 is a made-up exchange rate.

## Consequences

- How it's built: [[unit-flow]] (Currency).
- **Reopen if:** Solenoid gains exchange rates.
