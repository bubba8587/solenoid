## Decision

Amounts in two different currencies never combine: every operation that would mix $ and €, multiply and divide included, answers #UNIT!. A currency is known by its code.

## Why

Solenoid has no exchange rates, so combining two currencies would invent one: even $10 ÷ 5€ = 2 is a made-up rate.

## Consequences

- How it's built: [[unit-flow#Currency: the display code is the identity]].
- **Reopen if:** Solenoid gains exchange rates.
