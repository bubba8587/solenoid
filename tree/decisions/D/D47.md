## Decision

**MUST:**
- Exchange rates are out of scope, so every currency shares one `currency` axis at scale 1: $5 and 5€ store the same base magnitude, and the display code is the real identity.
- Every unit combinator (`arithmeticCell` for all seven ops, `compareUnits`, `forAggregateUnits`) refuses two operands with different explicit codes as a `#UNIT!` ("no exchange rate"). Multiply and divide refuse too: dividing would produce a ratio, and a unitless $/€ number is a made-up exchange rate.
- A currency cell with no code, such as a computed result, adopts the other side's code.
- A new combinator must register in the policy sweep.

## Why

When the check lived in some combinators and not others, the live path answered `$5 + 5€ = $10` and `$10 ÷ 5€ = 2:1`, while the currency-aware copies of the operators sat unused. One implementation (`arithmeticCell`, in `unitValue.ts`) with the guard up front means no op can miss it ([[C8]] declareOnce).
