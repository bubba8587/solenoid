## Decision

Contains, Starts with and Ends with work only on text. On a number, date or true/false they give an error that says to turn it into text first.

## Why

A number turned into text depends on how it happens to be printed, so a format change would look like a data change.

## Consequences

- How it's built: [[frame-verbs#filter and filterMulti]].
- **Reopen if:** people want these tests on numbers and accept one fixed printing for them.
