## Decision

**MUST:** `refreshConnection`, from the manual button or the interval timer, runs its recompute outside `beginGraphRebuild` / `endGraphRebuild`. Bulk topology operations (load, paste, undo and redo, composite create and unpack, sweeps) wrap themselves in rebuild scopes on purpose; a refresh must never be one of them.

## Why

A rebuild scope suppresses edge-triggered effects so a load doesn't replay old alerts ([[C39]] effectsEdgeTriggered). A refresh inside one would swallow a real alert on fresh data: an Alert watching a live feed would simply stop firing.
