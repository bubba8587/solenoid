## Decision

A temperature difference stays a difference, in its own unit: `30 °C - 20 °C` is 10 Δ°C, never 10 K, and a spread over readings, such as STDEV or a GROUPBY spread, is in Δ°C or Δ°F too. A difference converts by scale alone (10 Δ°C is 18 Δ°F), and adding one to a reading gives a reading (`20 °C + 10 Δ°C` is 30 °C).

## Why

A difference shown as K looks exactly like an absolute kelvin reading, so converting it to °C adds the offset back and shows -263.15 °C. Keeping the difference in its own unit means nothing later has to guess which it is.

## Consequences

- How it's built: [[unit-flow]].
- *Unenforced:* subtracting two readings gives K today, and Convert and the Format Controller add the °C offset back to it (`docs/backlog.md` § Leaves the code breaks).
- **Reopen if:** people need differences shown in K by default, as a scientist might.
