## Decision

A chart object keeps its controls wherever it's drawn: a Display showing it gets every control that belongs to the figure, such as paging through records or rotating a 3-D surface.

## Why

A chart is a value, so it shows up in Displays as well as on the node that made it. If its controls stayed behind on that node, the same figure would work in one place and be dead in another.

## Consequences

- How it's built: [[chart-figures#Display]].
- Controls on a node's card that work with no chart drawn stay on the card.
- **Reopen if:** a figure's controls start changing more than the figure itself, so that driving them from a Display surprises people.
