## Decision

Every place that shows a number nobody formatted shows it the same way: a whole number as it is, anything else to the Decimal places setting (4 by default) with trailing zeros dropped, and extreme sizes in scientific notation.

## Why

The same number shown different ways in different places makes you doubt which one is the value.

## Consequences

- *Exception:* the Constant cards, which exist to show a constant's precision, and chart axis ticks, which the chart lays out, keep their own rule. **Removed by:** nothing; each has its own job.
- How it's built: [[format-model#Styles]].
- **Reopen if:** one surface needs a different default from the rest; it then takes a Format Controller, never its own constant.
