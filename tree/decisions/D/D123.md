## Decision

In a criterion slot (SUMIFS, COUNTIFS and the rest), an operator joined to a value, ">="&cutoff, reads as that operator and the value as it is typed, a date staying a date. Criterion text is never read as a date or a date's serial: a date-shaped or serial text criterion against a date column is #TYPE!, and the message shows the joined form. The formulas and the SUMIFS card match criteria with one implementation.

## Why

The author's call in the 1.6 cut (F8, 2026-11-04): Excel's criteria syntax guesses a date out of text, which socketLattice forbids; keeping Excel's spelling but reading the joined value as typed keeps the habit and drops the guess.
