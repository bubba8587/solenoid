## Decision

The schedule engine and the Gantt layout compute on Excel-style day serials with integer arithmetic and never construct a JS `Date`. A serial converts once, at the border (import and display), and serials are never compared as floating-point numbers.

## Why

Every Gantt library surveyed has the daylight-saving off-by-an-hour class of bug, because it schedules with `Date`. Integer serials can't have it. This is [[C44]] dateSerials applied where it matters most.

## Consequences

- Minutes mode works in whole minutes within the day, still on the serial.
- **Reopen if:** a time-zone-aware schedule is wanted. The border would then grow a time zone, and the engine still wouldn't.
