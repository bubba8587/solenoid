## Decision

The Chart Builder's figure picker also chooses the type of a Chart it feeds: it writes `kind=<type>` into its options, and a Chart whose options carry a kind draws that type, its own type picker showing it locked. Figures that are nodes of their own (Histogram, Heatmap, Gantt and the rest) ignore `kind`.

## Why

Picking the figure in the Builder and then picking it again on the Chart is one choice made twice, and as the number of chart types grows the Builder is where a person chooses a figure together with its settings.

## Consequences

- How it's built: [[chart-figures#Chart]] and [[chart-figures#Serialization and the Chart Builder]].
- The key is pandas' `kind`; its values are the Chart's own type names, so `bar` is horizontal as on the card.
- **Reopen if:** a Builder needs to feed charts of different types at once.
