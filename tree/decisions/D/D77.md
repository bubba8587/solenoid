## Decision

The names pi, tau, e and phi always mean the constant, inside a LAMBDA too. A LAMBDA parameter with one of those names is an error.

## Why

Parameters match by name, so a name has to mean one thing wherever it sits. The author's call.

## Consequences

- How it's built: [[formula-language]].
- **Reopen if:** people need short parameter names that clash with a constant.
