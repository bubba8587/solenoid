## Decision

The names `pi`, `tau`, `e` and `phi`, in any letter case, always mean the constant, inside a LAMBDA as everywhere else. A LAMBDA parameter named after one is refused with an error saying the name is a constant, rather than quietly ignored.

## Why

LAMBDA parameters are matched by name ([[C50]] lambdaBindsByName), so a name has to mean one thing wherever it sits: `e` inside a LAMBDA is Euler's number, as in any formula. The author's call.
