## Decision

**MUST:** an effect that reaches outside the graph, such as an alert, fires only at the moment its status changes, never again while the status stays the same. It re-arms when things calm down, and it stays silent while a document is loading. A reading that jumps from LOW to HIGH counts as a change and fires again.

## Why

How often the graph recalculates is meant to be invisible ([[C23]] calcModes), so an alert fires once per real event however many recalculations run. Opening a document is not an event, so it doesn't replay old alerts.

## Consequences

- How the Alert decides: [[alert-node-alerts-hud]], *When it fires*. The other outside effects and how loading silences them: [[compute-pass]] (Rebuild scopes).
