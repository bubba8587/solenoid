## Decision

A note can turn on Knap in its own properties (`knap: true`), and then it reads in Obsidian the way its Note card reads in Solenoid: its body is filled in from its own properties, and a plain `{{ name }}` on a Solenoid-typed property shows that property's chip. Without the switch the plugin leaves the body alone.

## Why

The same note should read the same in Obsidian as on the canvas. The switch is per note because vaults keep template notes whose `{{ }}` tags aren't meant to fill in.

## Consequences

- A chip in the body edits the property; the body text itself is never written.
- How it's built: [[obsidian-plugin#Knap notes]].
- **Reopen if:** Obsidian fills Knap templates in notes itself, or the Note card stops filling its body from its own properties.
