## Decision

A note can switch Knap on in its own properties, and then it reads in Obsidian the way its Note card reads in Solenoid: filled in from its properties, with chips for Solenoid values.

## Why

The same note should read the same in Obsidian as on the canvas. It's per note because vaults keep template notes that shouldn't fill in.

## Consequences

- How it's built: [[obsidian-plugin#Knap notes]].
- **Reopen if:** Obsidian fills Knap templates in notes itself.
