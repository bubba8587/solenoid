## Decision

A placed node is titled with the name you picked in the Add menu; a node with an operation picker is titled by its current operation, live. A title you type always wins.

## Why

A node's title has to say what it computes, and a family name like Math doesn't.

## Consequences

- *Exception (the author's call):* a Conduit's default title is "Conduit N", its number, so every Conduit reads distinctly in the Navigator. **Removed by:** the Navigator telling same-named nodes apart on its own.
- How it's built: [[add-menu#Naming a placed node]].
- **Reopen if:** people want a placed node to keep its family name as its title.
