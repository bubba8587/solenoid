## Decision

**MUST:** a placed node is titled with the name of what you clicked in the Add menu. For a node with an operation picker, the title is the current operation's name and follows it live: a placed ABS reads "ABS", never "Math". A title you type always wins, and every place that names the node shows that same title.

## Why

A node's name is the one name every surface shows, so it has to say what the node computes. A node titled by its family ("Math") or by a sibling operation misreports what it does.

## Consequences

- *Exception (the author's call):* the small hint shown on hover gives the family name ("Series", "Math FX"), not the operation the title and picker already show. **Removed by:** nothing; the hint exists to name the family once.
- *Exception (the author's call):* a Conduit's default title is "Conduit N", its number, so every Conduit reads distinctly in the Navigator. **Removed by:** the Navigator telling same-named nodes apart on its own.
- How it's built: [[add-menu#Naming a placed node]].
- **Reopen if:** people want a placed node to keep its family name as its title.
