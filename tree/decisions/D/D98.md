## Decision

When a computed column's formula needs something the Rust core doesn't carry (an unported function, a reading only the TypeScript's date parser makes, a list where one value goes), the whole column runs on the TypeScript. The core never answers part of a column or approximates a cell.

## Why

A column that mixed engines, or approximated where it couldn't match, would show different numbers depending on which engine ran it, and nobody can see which one did.

## Consequences

- The support check and the bail: [[rust-core]] § Fallback.
