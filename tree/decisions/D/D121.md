## Decision

The Choice Ladder, Gantt (card and Chart Builder target), Schedule and Earned Value cards are hidden from the Add menu and Chart Builder; saved documents still load them. Removed by: each card's own author pass (docs/backlog.md § Hidden until they get their own pass).

## Why

The author's call in the 1.6 cut (2026-11-04): these cards need more love and attention than 1.6 gives them, so a new user shouldn't reach them yet.
