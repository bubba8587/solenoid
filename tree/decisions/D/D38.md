## Decision

**MUST:** true/false is a kind of value of its own, and a test with a blank in it answers unknown rather than false: `TRUE OR blank` is TRUE, and `FALSE OR blank` is blank, never FALSE. Its one crossing to another kind is to numbers and back, TRUE as 1 and FALSE as 0 ([[C10]] socketLattice).

## Why

[[C24]] arraySemantics makes a blank a missing value, not a zero or a false, so answering false for an unknown would invent a result. Excel treats TRUE and FALSE as 1 and 0, which is why that one crossing exists.

- The three-valued truth tables (Kleene logic): [[value-semantics]] and [[error-values]].
