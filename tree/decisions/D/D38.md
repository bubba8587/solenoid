## Decision

**MUST:** logical is a real type with three-valued logic: a null operand gives the Kleene answer (`TRUE OR null` is TRUE, `FALSE OR null` is null), never false. `logical ↔ number` is the one bridge between families ([[D11]] noAutoCross).

## Why

Excel treats TRUE and FALSE as 1 and 0, which is why that one bridge exists. But a null operand is unknown, not false, and answering false would invent a result ([[C24]] arraySemantics).
