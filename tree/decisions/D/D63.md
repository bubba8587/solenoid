## Decision

A position-locked group sits out every layout pass as a fixed obstacle. Global Tidy and Cleanup never re-place it or carry its members, and the new arrangement is pushed clear of it (a pinned box never moves, so its partner yields). Every standoff solve pins it, so the whole correction falls on the other end. The group's own Tidy still works, because the lock holds the box's corner, not its interior.

## Why

A lock that a layout pass could move is not a lock. Treating the group as an obstacle rather than exempting it is what keeps the rest of the layout consistent: an exempt box that the arrangement landed on would leave an overlap that nothing owns.

## Consequences

- `setGroupLocked` (`groupLogic.ts`) is the one way to set the lock, and `withLockedGroupsPinned` feeds every solver call.
- The expand push never moves a locked group, and it is fixed in every no-overlap pass ([[C112]] noOverlapsEver).
- Autofitting a locked group keeps its corner and moves the members to it instead.
- **Reopen if:** the lock should also freeze the members' interior, or users expect a locked group to still take part in Tidy as a movable unit.

## Contest
