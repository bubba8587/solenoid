## Decision

A position-locked group never moves in a layout pass. Tidy, Cleanup, the expand push, standoffs and the no-overlap pass treat it as a fixed obstacle and move everything else around it, instead of leaving it out of the pass. The lock holds the box, not its inside: the group's own Tidy still works, and autofitting it moves the members to its corner.

## Why

[[C112]] noOverlapsEver ends every layout action with nothing overlapping, and a lock that a layout pass could move is not a lock. Leaving the locked group out of the pass would let the arrangement land on it and leave an overlap nothing owns; treating it as a fixed obstacle keeps both promises.

## Consequences

- **Reopen if:** the lock should also freeze the members' inside, or users expect a locked group to still take part in Tidy as a movable unit.
- How it's built: the auto-arrange-tidy and group-expand-push specs.

## Contest
