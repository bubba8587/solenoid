## Decision

A group locked in place never moves in a layout action. Tidy, the push when a group expands, standoffs and the overlap cleanup all treat it as a fixed obstacle and move everything else around it. The lock holds the box, not its contents: the group's own Tidy still works.

## Why

A lock that a layout could move isn't a lock. And since every layout action ends with nothing overlapping, the locked group can't just sit the action out: everything else has to move around it.

## Consequences

- How it's built: [[auto-arrange-tidy#Position-locked groups]] and [[group-expand-push]].
- **Reopen if:** the lock should also freeze the group's contents, or people expect a locked group to still move in a Tidy.

## Contest

### Contest 2026-09-24 by Claude Opus 5.5: keep wins

Opposite (a locked group joins layout as a movable unit) makes the lock meaningless; deletion leaves C84 tidyTranslatesOnly silent on locks. Keep; applied now to expand push, collapse and autofit as well as Tidy.
