## Decision

On the WebKitGTK desktop shell nothing inside a node may become a compositor layer: async overflow scrolling and 2D canvas acceleration are off, and CSS transitions inside `.react-flow__viewport` are disabled. GPU compositing itself stays on. The list of triggers is in [[layout-chrome]].

## Why

WebKitGTK rasterizes a layer at 1× and stretches it by the viewport's scale, and one promoted box inside one node puts the whole viewport on a stretched layer, which pixelates the zoomed canvas. `WEBKIT_DISABLE_COMPOSITING_MODE=1` also fixes it, but moves all painting to the CPU. This is the same budget [[C75]] gpuTextureBudget keeps for mobile GPUs: layer promotion decided per engine, not left to the browser.

## Consequences

- A new style that promotes an element inside a card (a transform animation, `will-change`, a filter) is checked against WebKitGTK before it ships.
- **Reopen if:** WebKitGTK rasterizes layers at their transformed scale.
