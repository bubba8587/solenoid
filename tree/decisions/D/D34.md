## Decision

**MUST:** there is one kind of error, never two. ISERROR, IFERROR, ISNA and IFNA always agree on what counts as an error. A calculation that comes out as not-a-number is not an error by itself: the node that produced it turns it into a proper error or value first ([[D48]] classifyNonFinite).

## Why

With two notions of error, ISERROR and IFERROR disagree about the same cell. A not-a-number that counts as an error in one place and as a number in another is a wrong answer nobody sees ([[C24]] arraySemantics).
