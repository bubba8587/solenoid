## Decision

**MUST:** every failure flows as a tagged `SolError`. ISERROR and IFERROR agree on what an error is, the `#N/A` test lives in one place (`isNaError`), and a bare `NaN` is not an error (the producer classifies it first, [[D48]] classifyNonFinite).

## Why

With two notions of error, ISERROR and IFERROR disagree about the same cell. A bare NaN that counts as an error in one place and as a number in another is a wrong answer nobody sees ([[C24]] arraySemantics).
