## Decision

Wherever text becomes a value of a declared type, it is read one way, as a Frame Input cell reads it. Text the type can't read is NaN, never a silent blank.

## Why

A blank hides that anything was typed, and one reader means one rule to learn.

## Consequences

- How it's built: [[frame-verbs#Type inference]].
- **Reopen if:** Frame Input's cell reading changes; everything here follows it.
