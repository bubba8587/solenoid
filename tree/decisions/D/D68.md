## Decision

When an imported plan has something Solenoid's schedule can't express (a working-time exception, a must-finish-on constraint), the import still brings the plan in and names what it left out on the node ("Not carried over: ..."). Nothing is dropped silently, and nothing becomes a new column.

## Why

A difference you can see gets looked at; a hidden one is a wrong schedule that looks right.

## Consequences

- How it's built, and the list of what an import must name: [[schedule-and-gantt#Import at the border]].
- **Reopen if:** something left out turns out common enough to earn a column; that is a change to the four-column rule, not to an importer.
