## Decision

A computed column with no authored unit takes the unit its formula works out from its input columns' units and its wired values' units, as Expression does: `@price * @qty` over a $ column is $, `@dist / @time` over km and h is km/h, `@hi - @lo` over °C readings is K, `(@lo + @hi) / 2` is °C. An authored unit still wins, and a clash with it is `#UNIT!`.

## Why

A column holds one unit, so working out the formula's unit takes one pass per column, not one per row. Without it, a unit would silently drop at the one place a Frame does arithmetic. Units are a flagship of the product ([[A6]]), and this is the rule Expression already follows.

## Consequences

- How it's built: [[computed-columns]] and [[unit-flow]].
- **Reopen if:** a column's cells stop sharing one unit, or working out the unit costs more than computing the column.
