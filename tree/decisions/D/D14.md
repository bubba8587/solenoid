## Decision

**MUST:** a new socket type is added by extending the family × rank product. Cross-type dimensional edges are explicit in `accepts()` and swept exhaustively by test. A new type never needs its pairs written by hand.

## Why

Hand-listing pairs grows with the square of the variant count, and a hand-kept list drifts from the rule it copies.
