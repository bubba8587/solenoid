## Decision

Over no values, an aggregate answers its operation's identity where one exists (SUM and COUNT 0, PRODUCT 1, GCD 0, LCM 1, CONCAT and TEXTJOIN empty text), and blank where none does (MAX, MIN, AVERAGE, MEDIAN and the other statistics), in formulas and on the cards. AND, OR and XOR of nothing stay #VALUE!.

## Why

The largest of nothing isn't 0, and a made-up 0 is the confident wrong number blanks exist to prevent; the identity is the one answer that can't change a total it joins (mathlib's empty product is 1 and its max of nothing is a new bottom, never a number).

## Consequences

- MAX, MIN and PRODUCT of nothing, and GCD and LCM of nothing, answer otherwise than Excel; each is in the Excel divergence list.
- AND of nothing would be TRUE by the same rule; it stays an error because a vacuous TRUE reads as a bug to a user.
- Reopen if: a common Excel workbook leans on MAX of an empty range being 0.
