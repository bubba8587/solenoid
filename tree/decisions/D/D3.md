## Decision

**MUST:** when a derived value can't be computed for every case, the exception is declared as a field on the same declaration (the `fx` field on an `OP_META` table), never as a separate lookup map keyed by the same identity.

## Why

A separate map is the failure [[C8]] declareOnce forbids: a second place that must be kept in step, and one that sits away from the thing it modifies. [[C51]] formulaNaming derives a formula name by removing the spaces from the op label, which works only while the label despaces to the function's name. The bare labels of the Sets and Fill ops ("Union", "Constant") despace to UNION and CONSTANT rather than SETUNION and FILLVALUE, so `fx` sits on `SET_OP_META`, `SET_RELATION_META` and `FILL_OP_META`.
