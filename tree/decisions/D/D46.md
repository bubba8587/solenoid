## Decision

**MUST:** a node that draws random numbers keeps its draw, and draws again only when the user recalculates (F9 or one of its equivalents).

## Why

Otherwise it would draw again on every recompute. Any unrelated edit would then change the value, F9 would stop being what controls a new draw ([[C23]] calcModes), and a Monte Carlo built on it couldn't be reproduced. How it's built: the compute-pass spec (Volatile nodes).
