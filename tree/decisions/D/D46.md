## Decision

**MUST:** a node whose `data()` draws random numbers caches the draw and re-rolls only when `getRecalcGen()` changes.

## Why

A bare `Math.random()` in `data()` re-rolls on every recompute pass. Any unrelated edit then changes the value, F9 stops being what controls re-rolling ([[C23]] calcModes), and a Monte Carlo built on it can't be reproduced.
