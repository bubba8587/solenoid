## Decision
For now a Chart refuses a plain list of dates, or a table whose only column is dates, with #TYPE!, and says to put the dates in a table's first column, where they run along the x axis. Drawing dates as values, on a date y axis, is held for later.
## Why
A plain list plots each item as a height, and a date has none: drawn as it is, it plots its serial ([[C137]] datesNeverSerials). Refusing is the honest answer until a chart can draw a date axis for values.
## Consequences
- Dates as a table's first column, and as a scatter or XY chart's x column, still draw, on a date axis.
- **Reopen if:** a chart is wanted that plots dates as values (each task's due date down a list), which needs a date y axis.
