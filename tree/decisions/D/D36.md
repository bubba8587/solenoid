## Decision

**MUST:** a blank (null) is a real missing value at every size, from one value to a whole table. Totals and averages skip it, Filter drops it, and cell-by-cell math passes it on. Nothing turns it into 0.

## Why

Treating a missing value as 0 changes sums, means and counts with nothing on the node to show it. Skipping it in totals and passing it on in cell-by-cell math keeps "missing" distinct from "zero" ([[C24]] arraySemantics).

## Consequences

- *Exception:* Fill (and its Coalesce op) exists to turn a null into something on purpose. **Removed by:** nothing; that is the node's whole job.
