## Decision

A blank is a real missing value, in a single value and in every cell. Totals skip it, filters drop it, cell-by-cell math passes it on, and nothing turns it into zero.

## Why

A blank is missing data, not a zero, and treating it as 0 would quietly change sums, averages and counts.

## Consequences

- *Exception:* Fill exists to turn a blank into something on purpose. **Removed by:** nothing; that is the node's whole job.
