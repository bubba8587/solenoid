## Decision

**MUST:** a blank is a real missing value, in a single value and in every cell of a list or table. Totals and averages skip it, a filter drops it, and cell-by-cell math passes it on. Nothing turns it into 0.

## Why

A blank is missing data, not a zero ([[C24]] arraySemantics). Treating it as 0 would change sums, averages and counts with nothing on screen to show it.

## Consequences

- *Exception:* Fill (and its Coalesce op) exists to turn a blank into something on purpose. **Removed by:** nothing; that is the node's whole job.
- How it's built: [[value-semantics]] (Propagation, by context).
