## Decision

A schedule works in whole working days or in working minutes, chosen on the node. The choice changes how the plan is computed, not only how it's shown.

## Why

What you see should be what was computed; rounding only the display makes two tasks look like they overlap.

## Consequences

- How it's built: [[schedule-and-gantt#Dates and precision]].
- **Reopen if:** a third precision is asked for.
