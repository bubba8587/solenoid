## Decision

A schedule works in one of two precisions, chosen on the node: Days, in whole working days, or Minutes, in working hours, so the next task can start the same afternoon. The choice changes how the plan is computed, never only how it is shown.

## Why

Rounding only what is shown puts two tasks on the same day and reads as an overlap. A real mode keeps what you see and what is computed the same.

## Consequences

- This is a mode of the whole schedule, not one of the per-task modes the four-column rule leaves out.
- How it's built: [[schedule-and-gantt#Dates and precision]].
- **Reopen if:** a third precision is asked for; it would be a third mode.
