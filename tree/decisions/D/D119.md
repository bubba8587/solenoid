## Decision

A date before 1 January 1900 is a date like any other: every function, card, display and sort takes it, where Excel, which has no dates before 1900, refuses it.

## Why

The author's call (2026-11-04): history and birthdays come before 1900, and a date that works in one place and is refused in the next reads as a bug. Excel's refusal is a limit of its serial numbers, which Solenoid doesn't show.
