## Decision

**MUST:** a number a calculation can't represent never leaves the node that computed it as a bare not-a-number or infinity. That node turns it into a named error: not-a-number becomes #DOMAIN!, and infinity from ordinary inputs becomes #OVERFLOW!. An infinity that came in as infinity passes on, since the Constant node's ∞ is a real value.

## Why

Errors are kept apart from numbers so a wrong answer never looks right ([[C24]] arraySemantics). A bare not-a-number looks like an empty cell and computes onward as more of the same, and only the node that produced it knows why.

## Consequences

- When rows are grouped, deduplicated or split into windows, plus infinity, minus infinity and not-a-number are three keys of their own, and none of them ever matches in a Join. One shared bucket would merge values every other surface tells apart, and two rows that both overflowed are not the same thing.
- Which calculations run the check, and the functions with a convention of their own: [[error-values]].
