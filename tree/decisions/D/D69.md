## Decision

A Convert whose from- and to-units measure different things answers `#N/A` for the whole output, rather than a `#UNIT!` in each cell: a bad dropdown pick is the node's problem, not the data's.

## Why

[[C25]] firstClassUnits makes a unit clash an error, and here the clash comes from the node's own settings. `#N/A` is what IFNA and ISNA catch and what Excel's CONVERT answers. A `#UNIT!` in each cell would match how arithmetic reports mismatched units, but the functions users reach for couldn't catch it, and it would blame the data for the pick.

## Consequences

- Every other dimension clash stays `#UNIT!` at the cell ([[C25]] firstClassUnits).
- **Reopen if:** Convert grows per-cell units (a mixed-unit list), when the clash becomes the cell's.
