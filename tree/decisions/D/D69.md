## Decision

A Convert whose from-unit and to-unit measure different things answers #N/A for its whole output, rather than #UNIT! in each cell: a bad dropdown pick is the node's problem, not the data's.

## Why

A unit clash is an error ([[C25]] firstClassUnits), but here the clash comes from the node's own settings. #N/A is what Excel's CONVERT answers and what IFNA and ISNA catch, while a #UNIT! in every cell would blame the data for the pick.

## Consequences

- Every other unit clash stays #UNIT! at the cell.
- **Reopen if:** Convert grows per-cell units (a mixed-unit list), when the clash becomes the cell's.
