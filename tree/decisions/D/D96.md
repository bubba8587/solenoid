## Decision

A Cube column whose cells hold lists or grids can be a PIVOTBY value field: each group pools every item of its rows' cells, so a one-row group gives what `SUM(@name)`, `AVERAGE(@name)` or `COUNT(@name)` gives in a cube formula. Such a column can't be a row, column or filter field, and a nested table stays out.

## Why

A Cube's list columns are where its records keep their history, and a pivot that can't see them would force an Unnest first; reading them the way a formula does means the same column gives the same total on both surfaces.

## Consequences

- How it's built: [[frame-verbs#pivot (oracle only)]].
- **Reopen if:** a pivot over list cells should instead total each row first and then aggregate the row totals (an average of averages).
