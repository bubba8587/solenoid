## Decision

On the desktop, the Navigator's tree is the Documents folder on disk: its folders are folders there, its documents are files there, and renaming or moving a row in the Navigator renames or moves the file. On the web, where there is no folder to read, the browser's document library carries the same tree.

## Why

The author wants the tree to read straight from a plain file tree, so what people see in the app and in their file manager should be the same thing.

## Consequences

- A file changed outside the app shows up in the tree; the open copy reloads when it has no unsaved edits and asks otherwise.
- **Reopen if:** keeping the tree and the disk in step costs more than it gives, for example on synced folders that rename files on their own.
