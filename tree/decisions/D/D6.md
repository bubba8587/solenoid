## Decision

**MUST:** the Add-menu row for a hidden op is built from the host leaf plus the op's own declaration (`opEntry` spreading `...host`), never as a second hand-written catalog entry. What the row must not inherit is named at that call site, citing this leaf, which carries the reason:
- the host's `keywords`, because family words would make every sibling op match the same queries and the rows would stop telling the ops apart;
- its `hiddenOps` and its ops mark, because a row that is one op has nothing folded inside it.

## Why

An op row is a view of its leaf, so every property the leaf owns (label stem, pack, accent, description) has to follow the leaf automatically, or the menu and the card drift apart ([[C8]] declareOnce). The properties it does not inherit are the exception list, and they belong in the same function rather than a parallel table ([[D3]] overrideInPlace). Blocking every `keywords` value, the op's own included, is what once pushed per-op Excel spellings into the visible label.
