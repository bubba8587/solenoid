## Decision

A List is one row, like a line of a CSV file, everywhere, and a Frame column read whole is a column, as an Excel table column is. Functions whose answer depends on shape follow Excel.

## Why

We want Excel's answers, and many of Excel's answers depend on which way the values run.

## Consequences

- How it's built: [[formula-language]] (*Lists, columns and tables*).
- **Reopen if:** SEQUENCE's direction starts to matter to formulas people write.
