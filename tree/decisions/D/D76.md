## Decision

In GROUPBY, PIVOTBY and Cube Rollup, the min and max of a text column give its first and last text in sort order, where Excel gives a blank.

## Why

Excel's blank answers nothing, and a blank should mean a missing value. The author's call.

## Consequences

- How it's built: [[frame-verbs#groupBy]].
- **Reopen if:** a blank result for text is wanted back for Excel parity.
