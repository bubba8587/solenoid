## Decision

In GROUPBY, PIVOTBY and Cube Rollup, the min and max of a text column give the group's first and last text in sort order, where Excel gives a blank. The MIN and MAX formulas over a list keep Excel's answer.

## Why

A blank should mean a missing value ([[C24]] arraySemantics), and Excel's blank here answers nothing. The first and last text is a useful answer, in the order Sort uses ([[C59]] byteStringOrder). The author's call.

- How it's built: [[frame-verbs#groupBy]].
- **Reopen if:** a blank result for text is wanted back for Excel parity.
