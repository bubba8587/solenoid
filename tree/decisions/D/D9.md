## Decision

**MUST:** a claim of the form "this node supports X" over a set of names is checked with `every`. `some` is allowed only where partial support is the deliberate, documented contract.

## Why

`some` reports a node as covered while most of its names still fail. It hid ten names (the seven B-suffixed text functions, `ERF.PRECISE`, `ERFC.PRECISE` and `VALUETOTEXT`) that were each declared against a real node while the formula surface answered `#NAME?`.
