## Decision

Semantics live in shared helpers (the broadcasters, `forAggregate`) and are never re-derived per surface. There is one sanctioned difference between a node and a formula: a reduction skips nulls, and an element-wise operation propagates them with Kleene logic. So the formula `AND(range)` and the Aggregate family skip blanks, as Excel's range functions do, while the Boolean Op node, IF, the operators and the Frame Filter's conditions work cell by cell and treat a null as unknown.

## Why

A helper re-derived per surface answers differently per surface ([[C17]] shareImpl, applied to meaning rather than code). The one split isn't drift: it follows Excel's own range semantics for reductions and three-valued logic for element-wise work ([[C24]] arraySemantics, [[D36]] nullSkippedNotZero, [[D38]] kleeneLogic).

## Consequences

- Any other disagreement between a node and its formula is a bug against this node.
