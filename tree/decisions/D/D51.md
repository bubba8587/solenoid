## Decision

A function that reduces a range to one answer skips blanks, as Excel's range functions do, while cell-by-cell work treats a blank as unknown. The split follows the kind of work, never the surface.

## Why

A total over a column with gaps should still total, but one unknown cell added in should give an unknown answer, not a guess.

## Consequences

- How it's built: [[formula-language#Range functions]] and [[value-semantics]].
- **Reopen if:** Excel's range functions stop skipping blanks.
