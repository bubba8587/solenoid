## Decision

A node and the formula of the same name always give the same answer, never a different one per surface. There is one deliberate difference, and it is between two kinds of work rather than two surfaces: a function that reduces a range to one answer skips blanks, and an operation that works cell by cell treats a blank as unknown. So the formula `AND(range)` and the Aggregate family skip blanks, as Excel's range functions do, while the Boolean Op node, IF, the operators and the Frame Filter's conditions treat a blank as unknown.

## Why

A meaning worked out separately for each surface answers differently on each ([[C17]] shareImpl, applied to meaning rather than code). The one split isn't drift: it follows Excel's own range behavior for reductions and three-valued logic for cell-by-cell work ([[C24]] arraySemantics, [[D36]] nullSkippedNotZero, [[D38]] kleeneLogic).

## Consequences

- Any other disagreement between a node and its formula is a bug against this leaf.
