## Decision

A kernel answers `null` for an undefined-but-not-wrong result (fewer points than the statistic needs, a reversed DATEDIF range, a solver that stalled) and a `SolError` only for a domain failure. Excel answers `#DIV/0!` or `#NUM!` for both, and this is a deliberate departure.

## Why

"Not enough data yet" is the normal state of a growing table; erroring it turns every partial column red and hides the one cell that is actually wrong. Null is first-class and skipped by everything downstream ([[D36]] nullSkippedNotZero), so the quiet answer composes.

## Consequences

- A kernel's doc states which of its answers are the quiet null; the divergence is recorded per name in [[formulajs-divergences]] where Excel would error.
- **Reopen if:** a user needs Excel's exact error for a formula that mixes the two (an `IFERROR` guard that must fire on short data).
