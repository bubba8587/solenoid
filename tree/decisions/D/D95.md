## Decision

The neutral swatch's three shades (white, gray, dark) are drawn from the active palette's chrome: its text, muted text and strong border colors. A palette whose chrome lacks one tints the default shade with its background's hue, and a palette with no chrome keeps the plain shades. The app accent's gray takes the chrome's muted gray; a gray node or socket keeps the palette's own gray slot.

## Why

[[C62]] paletteAllOrNone makes a palette govern every color on screen. Fixed neutrals ignored it, so on a tinted palette like Blueprint a white or dark accent sat on navy chrome as plain gray and looked foreign. Drawing them from the chrome the palette already tuned keeps them its neutrals.

## Consequences

- The resolver is `neutralHex` (`palette.ts`); `resolveColor` routes the white and dark shades through it, and `resolveAccent` the accent's gray.
- Groups, notes and cables colored with a neutral shade follow the palette the same way.
- **Reopen if:** a palette's chrome text or border color reads badly as an accent; that palette then names its neutrals itself.
