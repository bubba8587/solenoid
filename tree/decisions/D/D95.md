## Decision

The neutral swatch's three shades (white, gray and dark) take their color from the active palette's own workbench, so on a tinted palette they are tinted too. A palette with no workbench colors keeps the plain shades, and a gray node or socket keeps the palette's own gray.

## Why

A palette sets the look of the whole workbench, so its neutrals should belong to it too; a plain gray on a navy workbench looks out of place.

## Consequences

- Groups, notes and cables colored with a neutral shade follow the palette the same way.
- How it's built: [[palette-and-theme#The gray swatch's neutral cycle]].
- **Reopen if:** a palette's workbench text or border color reads badly as an accent; that palette then names its neutrals itself.
