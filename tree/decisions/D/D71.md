## Decision

Discrete zoom steps (a wheel notch, the zoom pill, keys and fit) land on a 10% lattice (`ZOOM_SNAP`). The pinch, a continuous gesture, never snaps mid-drag, and the next discrete step rounds back onto the lattice.

## Why

Snapping mid-drag moves the canvas in 10% jumps under the fingers, which the author rejected. A lattice for discrete steps keeps the zoom readout tidy and the steps reproducible. Two touch contacts are the only gate for a pinch ([[C92]] pinchUnvetoable), so a touchscreen laptop pinches smoothly too.

## Consequences

- The wheel carries the unsnapped zoom from one event to the next, so a trackpad glide made of tiny deltas still climbs to the next step.
- **Reopen if:** the lattice step should change with zoom level (a logarithmic lattice). That is a change to the curve, not to the gating.
