## Decision

Discrete zoom steps (a wheel notch, the zoom pill, keys and fit) land on steps of 10%. The pinch, a continuous gesture, never snaps while the fingers move, and the next discrete step rounds back onto a 10% step.

## Why

[[C92]] pinchUnvetoable makes the pinch the one gesture nothing interrupts, and it also has to move smoothly: snapping mid-pinch would move the canvas in 10% jumps under the fingers, the author's call against it. A discrete zoom has no such motion, and fixed steps keep the zoom readout tidy and the steps repeatable.

## Consequences

- **Reopen if:** the step size should change with zoom level. That is a change to the zoom curve, not to when snapping happens. How it's built: the pointer-gestures spec.
