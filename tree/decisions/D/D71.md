## Decision

Zooming in steps (a wheel notch, the zoom buttons, keys, fit) lands on steps of 10%. A pinch zooms smoothly and never snaps while the fingers move; the next stepped zoom rounds back onto a 10% step.

## Why

A pinch should follow your fingers, and snapping mid-pinch would jump the canvas under them; the author's call. Fixed steps everywhere else keep the zoom readout tidy and the steps repeatable.

## Consequences

- How it's built: [[pointer-gestures]].
- **Reopen if:** the step size should change with the zoom level.
