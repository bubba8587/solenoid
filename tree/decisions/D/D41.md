## Decision

**MUST:** a value's display format (decimals, percent, date style and the like) carries on downstream through a step that keeps the value's meaning, and stops at a step that doesn't: adding two percentages is still a percentage, multiplying them isn't. A nearer Format Controller always overrides what it inherits. The unit goes wherever the value goes ([[C25]] firstClassUnits), so multiplying $10.00 by 3 keeps the $ and drops the two decimals.

## Why

A value's format belongs to the value ([[C118]] formatTravelsWithValue), so it follows the value as far as it still describes it and no further: NPV of a 5% rate is not a percent, and one date minus another is a span, not a date.
