## Decision

A composite in a heavy run mode, any mode with a Solve button, sits blank until you press Solve or Refresh and never solves on its own. Single mode stays fully live.

## Why

The author's call: people expect it to sit blank so they can watch it compute when they press Solve.

## Consequences

- How it's built: [[composite-nodes#The heavy-mode hold]].
- **Reopen if:** the author wants a mode to compute on load again.
