## Decision

A composite in a heavy run mode, meaning any mode with a Solve button (Manual or Query, Goal Seek, Monte Carlo, Scenarios, Data Table, By-Row, Simulation), sits blank until you click Solve or Refresh. It never solves on load, paste, create or a switch into a heavy mode. The plain Single mode stays fully live.

## Why

Heavy work waits for the user, the way a manual recalc does for a whole document, and a heavy composite is that heavy work inside one node. The author's call: people expect it to sit blank so they can watch it compute when they press Solve.

## Consequences

- By-Row runs at most 500 rows and warns loudly when it cuts a table short.
- How it's built: [[composite-nodes#The heavy-mode hold]].
- **Reopen if:** the author wants a mode to compute on load again.
