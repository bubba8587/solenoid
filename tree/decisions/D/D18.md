## Decision

**MUST:** every Frame or 2-D input whose label lists the columns it expects ([[C13]] frameLabelGrammar) shows a matching example table: its columns are the label's columns, in order, with a standard initialism spelled out (`OHLC` is Open, High, Low, Close). Every example, whether or not its label lists columns, is 3 to 5 full rows whose cells match the declared column types.

## Why

[[C13]] frameLabelGrammar makes the label the one place the expected columns can be read before wiring, and the example is that label worked through, so the two are one promise. A labeled input with no example leaves the user guessing, and an example whose columns drift from the label shows a confidently wrong table, which is worse than none. That is why it is enforced by a test, the author's call, rather than left to care.
