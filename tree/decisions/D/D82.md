## Decision

The formula `SPARKLINE(range, [type])` answers a small SVG picture as `data:image/svg+xml` text: a line, columns or a win/loss strip, the Sparkline node's three types. A range longer than 40 points is averaged down to 40 buckets, and the picture is one or two paths with fixed colors.

## Why

A computed column's cell holds a number, text, TRUE/FALSE, a blank or (in a Cube) a list, never a chart value ([[C54]] noPerCellFormulas keeps the one definition on the header), so a chart per row has to travel as text. [[C100]] chartIsAValue still holds for the Sparkline node, whose output is the full chart. A sparkline column runs once per row over a whole table, so the picture's size must not grow with the data: a fixed point cap and a fixed shape keep each cell a few hundred bytes, the same spirit as [[C21]] matchNodeLimits.

## Consequences

- The colors are fixed (the Default palette's blue, green and vermilion), since a picture in an image can't follow the theme.
- Any text cell holding a `data:image` picture shows as the picture ([[D83]] imageTextCells).
