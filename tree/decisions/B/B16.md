## Decision

Formulas and nodes share one set of functions, measured against the Excel people use today: a function current Excel has replaced, like VLOOKUP or MATCH, has no node and no formula, and typing one gets a short error naming the one to use instead. Where an Excel quirk would make the two disagree, they stay consistent and the difference is written down.

## Why

We want Excel's names and answers, and what a user learns in the formula bar should work the same on the canvas, and still be right in Excel.

## Consequences

- How it's built, and the written-down differences: [[formula-language#Deliberate differences from Excel]], [[formula-language#Blocked and wrong-surface names]] and [[formulajs-divergences]].
- **Reopen if:** a surface is wanted that deliberately offers fewer functions, such as a simple mode, or a real Excel file import ships, where rewriting old lookups beats an error.
