## Decision

Formulas and nodes share one set of functions: a function has the same name and gives the same answer on both, rather than each surface keeping its own. That set is measured against the Excel people use today. Where matching an Excel quirk would make the two surfaces disagree, they stay consistent and the difference is written down. Nodes no formula can express (Frame verbs, figures, inputs) aren't functions and sit outside this.

## Why

We want Excel's names and Excel's answers, so there should be one copy of them, and what a user learns in the formula bar works the same on the canvas.

## Consequences

- A new function lands on both surfaces in one change, or on neither.
- The written-down differences: [[formula-language#Deliberate differences from Excel]], and the Formula.js overrides in [[formulajs-divergences]].
- **Reopen if:** a surface is wanted that deliberately offers fewer functions, such as a simple mode. It would be a filter over the one set, never a second set.
