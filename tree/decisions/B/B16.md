## Decision

Formulas and nodes share one set of functions, measured against the Excel people use today. Where an Excel quirk would make the two disagree, they stay consistent and the difference is written down.

## Why

We want Excel's names and answers, and what a user learns in the formula bar should work the same on the canvas.

## Consequences

- How it's built, and the written-down differences: [[formula-language#Deliberate differences from Excel]] and [[formulajs-divergences]].
- **Reopen if:** a surface is wanted that deliberately offers fewer functions, such as a simple mode.
