## Decision

Every npm package and every crate moves to its newest release, majors included, and transitive dependencies with them. A failing check is reported; an agent fixes what it can in its own commit, and nothing is pinned to an older version to make a check pass.

## Why

The point of the branch is to meet each breakage while it is small: a pinned version hides exactly what the branch exists to show.
