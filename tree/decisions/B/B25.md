## Decision

Each finding on the bleeding-edge branch, a platform feature or a dependency fix, is marked ready or not ready to port to a work branch. A platform feature is ready when every engine Solenoid ships into supports it, or when an engine without it behaves exactly as the work branches do today. A dependency fix is ready together with the upgrade that needs it. Anything else is not ready, and the finding names what it waits for.

## Why

The author judges the branch by porting from it, so each finding has to say whether porting it would break anyone today; a list of what works in one browser doesn't.
