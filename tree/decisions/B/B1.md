## Decision

Solenoid positions itself as the computation layer for an Obsidian vault, rather than a standalone calculator people feed by hand: it reads notes, their frontmatter and TaskNotes data, and writes computed values back. This is its main route to adoption.

Writing a note's body (a filled template, a Report, a direct write) matters as much as writing its properties, and more. Tools for managing Obsidian properties already exist, and Solenoid adds little there; its value is computed content landing in the note itself.

## Why

Obsidian has a large and established userbase which is focused on knowledge work. They like to use plugins. Besides Solenoid, there is not a clean route between Obsidian note data and spreadsheet work. By building that route, we can attract an audience.

Also, the project author is an Obsidian & Excel user and would simply like it for themself.
