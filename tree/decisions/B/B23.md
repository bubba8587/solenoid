## Decision

The newest CSS and HTML features replace what the app does by hand (a measurement in script, a ladder of rules, a workaround) wherever the look and behavior stay the same or get clearly better. Each is used for real, with no fallback or polyfill, so an engine that lacks it shows the breakage. A feature that would change the look is listed for the author instead of built.

## Why

The branch exists to show what the platform can already do for the app and which engines hold it back; a fallback would hide both. Changes of look are taste, which is the author's.
