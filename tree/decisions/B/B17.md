## Decision

A value knows what kind of thing it is: its kind, its shape, its unit, or that it is a blank or an error. Nothing changes that behind the user's back; a change takes a node the user can see.

## Why

Solenoid parts from Excel where Excel lets types and units slide, so a value carries what it is along with what it holds.

## Consequences

- How it's built: [[value-semantics]] and [[socket-lattice]].
- **Reopen if:** Solenoid stops treating types and units as first-class.
