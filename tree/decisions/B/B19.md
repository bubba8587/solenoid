## Decision

Where Excel users have a working habit that doesn't conflict with the graph, Solenoid keeps it: answers recalculate on their own after every edit, heavy work can switch to recalculating by hand with F9, and a typed value takes effect when you press Enter or click away, the way a cell does.

## Why

[[A5]] promises people arriving from Excel no learning curve, and a habit is part of that promise as much as a function name is. [[A1]] is a calculator, and a calculator whose answers go stale until you ask, or change under you as you type, doesn't feel like one. The alternative, a run button for the whole graph like a batch tool, would make every edit a two-step act.

## Consequences

- Automatic and manual recalculation ([[C23]] calcModes).
- Edits commit on Enter or click-away, never per keystroke ([[C95]] commitOnEnter).
- **Reopen if:** a habit turns out to fight the graph (then the graph wins, and the difference is written down under [[A6]] divergeByDesign).
