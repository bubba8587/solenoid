## Decision

Anyone can open a Solenoid document someone else made without risk. Opening it never writes outside the app, never sends anything anywhere, and never carries a secret of the person who made it. Anything that reaches outside the app happens only when the person who opened the document asks for it.

## Why

A calculator people build on together ([[A1]] visualGraphCalculator) only works if documents can be passed around: shared, attached, committed to a vault, opened from the web. The moment opening a file could write to a disk, call a web address or leak a key, every shared document becomes a risk and people stop sharing them. The opposite, trusting documents by default, is simpler to build and is how many tools start; it holds only until the first harmful file.

## Consequences

- Writes outside the app wait for a Run press and a document opens disarmed ([[C38]] sinkRunButtonOnly).
- Content crossing into or out of the app is checked in one place per kind ([[C103]] untrustedContentSeams).
- An AI provider key stays on the device and never enters a document ([[C105]] apiKeysStayLocal).
- **Reopen if:** never; loosening it would make every shared document suspect.
