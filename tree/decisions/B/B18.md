## Decision

Opening a document someone else made is always safe: it never writes outside the app, sends anything anywhere, or carries its maker's secrets. Anything that reaches outside waits for the person who opened it to ask.

## Why

Documents are meant to be passed around, and people would stop sharing them if opening one could write to a disk, call out or leak a key.

## Consequences

- *Unenforced:* a foreign document's Script nodes run without asking, and its web images load before the network gate (`docs/backlog.md` § Leaves the code breaks).
- **Reopen if:** never; loosening it would make every shared document suspect.
