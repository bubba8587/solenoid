## Decision

Solenoid is built to work well on phones and tablets as well as on a desktop with a mouse. Touch, narrow screens and the on-screen keyboard are designed for everywhere the app appears: the canvas, the popups, the web app and the Obsidian plugin.

## Why

People reach for the device in their hand to read an answer or change a number away from their desk. An app that only works with a mouse on a wide screen loses those moments, and with them a large part of its reach.

## Consequences

- **Reopen if:** Solenoid becomes desktop-only.
