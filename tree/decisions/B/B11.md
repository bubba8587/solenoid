## Decision

When several nodes do variations of the same job, they are one node with an option that picks the variation: one Distribution node with a distribution picker, not a node per distribution. A merge goes all the way; it never stops at joining two of them.

## Why

Every extra node is one more name to find in the Add menu, learn, and keep in step with its formula. A merge that stops partway leaves most of that cost in place.

## Consequences

- How it's built: [[node-classes]].
- **Reopen if:** a family's variants take genuinely different inputs; then separate nodes are more honest than one node whose sockets change shape.
