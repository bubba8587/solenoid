## Decision

The website & marketing pages use real instances of the app and its node function cards, not mockups.

## Why

1. The project author is very particular about the look and feel.
2. Visual divergences are really really obvious given how carefully crafted the node cards are. Every pixel matters. 
3. We don't want to surprise users by having content that drifts from the app.
