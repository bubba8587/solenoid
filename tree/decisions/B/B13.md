## Decision

An AI assistant that edits the document is in scope. It proposes an edit, the app checks it the same way it checks an opened file, and the user sees the change and approves it before it lands. The assistant uses Anthropic's models. AI is a capability, not the headline: the pitch stays the files and the canvas.

## Why

Building a graph by hand takes time, and an assistant that can draw one for you shortens that. Checking and approving every edit keeps the user in charge of their document.

## Consequences

- The assistant ships switched off, the author's call, until its checking is finished; while it is off, every trace of it is hidden.
- How it's built: [[command-palette#AI mode]].
- **Reopen if:** the assistant can't be made trustworthy, or it pulls the product's focus on Excel users toward an AI-first pitch.

## Alternatives considered

### Contest 2026-09-15 by Claude Opus 5: keep wins

Rubric: [[A1]].
- keep: AI edits in scope, caged behind validation and approval. Cost: the AI service, the whole-document rewrite gate and diff.
- opposite: AI stays out. Forgoes AI-assisted graph building for a calculator whose canvas is already a text form an AI can write.
- deletion: [[A1]] alone does not decide whether an AI may edit the document; each AI feature would re-open the question.
- variant: AI as a separate assistant that never edits the document. Loses the edit loop; the cage already bounds the risk.
Verdict: keep.
