## Decision

An AI assistant that edits the document is in scope, on Anthropic's models. Every edit it proposes is checked like an opened file, and the user approves it before it lands.

## Why

An assistant that draws a graph for you saves the time of building it by hand, and approving every edit keeps the user in charge.

## Consequences

- *Exception:* the assistant ships switched off, with every trace of it hidden, the author's call. **Removed by:** its checking being finished.
- How it's built: [[command-palette#AI mode]].
- **Reopen if:** the assistant can't be made trustworthy, or it pulls the pitch toward AI first.

## Alternatives considered

### Contest 2026-09-15 by Claude Opus 5: keep wins

Rubric: [[A1]].
- keep: AI edits in scope, caged behind validation and approval. Cost: the AI service, the whole-document rewrite gate and diff.
- opposite: AI stays out. Forgoes AI-assisted graph building for a calculator whose canvas is already a text form an AI can write.
- deletion: [[A1]] alone does not decide whether an AI may edit the document; each AI feature would re-open the question.
- variant: AI as a separate assistant that never edits the document. Loses the edit loop; the cage already bounds the risk.
Verdict: keep.
