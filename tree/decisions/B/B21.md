## Decision

The branch is persistent. Its own commits (dependency bumps, fixes, experiments) are rebased onto the most recently pushed work branch whenever it is kept, so no bump or experiment is ever redone. A commit that conflicts with new work is dropped from the branch, named in its report, and kept where it can be redone.

## Why

Rebuilding the branch from its base every day redid every bump and erased anything built there, so nothing could live on it but the bumps.

## Consequences

- How it's built: [[branch#The generator]].
- **Reopen if:** rebasing costs more than rebuilding, such as when most carried commits conflict every day.
