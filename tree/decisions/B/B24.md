## Decision

An agent working on the bleeding-edge branch implements, commits and pushes what is within reason without asking the author to pick, approve or order the work. The author judges afterward, by porting what's worth keeping.

## Why

The branch is for trying everything; a question per experiment turns it back into the author's to-do list, which is what it exists to avoid.
