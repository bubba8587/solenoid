## Decision

Answers recalculate on their own after every edit, with a manual mode (F9) for heavy documents, and an edit recalculates only what depends on it. However a recalculation is started and however much of the graph it touches, the answers on screen are the ones a full, clean recalculation would give.

## Why

We keep Excel's habit of answers that update on their own ([[B19]] spreadsheetHabits). Recalculating only what an edit touches keeps that fast enough to leave on, and a shortcut is only acceptable if nobody can tell it was taken.

## Consequences

- Sketch mode is the user's own pick of a quicker answer on very large tables: it works on a sample and marks its answers approximate, and F9 always computes exactly.
- How it's built: [[compute-pass]].
- **Reopen if:** a document grows too big for a recalculation that matches a full one to keep up.
