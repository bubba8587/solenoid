## Decision

Answers recalculate on their own after every edit, touching only what the edit affects, with a manual mode (F9) for heavy documents. However much is recalculated, the answers match a full, clean recalculation.

## Why

We keep Excel's habit of answers that update themselves, and a shortcut is only fine if nobody can tell it was taken.

## Consequences

- *Exception:* Sketch mode, the user's own pick, works on a sample of very large tables and marks its answers approximate; F9 always computes exactly. **Removed by:** exact answers on any table being fast enough.
- How it's built: [[compute-pass]].
- **Reopen if:** a document grows too big for a recalculation that matches a full one to keep up.
