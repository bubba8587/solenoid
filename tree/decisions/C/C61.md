## Decision

Every probability distribution lives on one Distribution node, never a node per distribution or per function. One picker chooses the distribution and a second chooses what to work out (CDF, PDF, an inverse, a sample). Switching distribution keeps the second choice by its meaning instead of resetting it.

## Why

The author's call: fourteen distributions times their forms would otherwise be dozens of nodes to find and learn.

## Consequences

- *Exception:* Binomial Range stays its own node, because it needs a second x input. **Removed by:** the Distribution node taking a second x input.
- How it's built: [[formula-language]] § Distribution table.
- **Reopen if:** a distribution arrives that doesn't fit this shape (it needs a second x input, or a parameter that isn't a number).
