## Decision

Every probability distribution lives on one Distribution node, never a node per distribution or per function. One picker chooses the distribution (14 of them, from Normal to Negative Binomial), and a second chooses what to work out (CDF, PDF, an inverse, Sample and so on). Switching distribution keeps that second choice by its meaning rather than resetting it: PDF becomes PMF across the line between continuous and discrete, and any inverse becomes plain Inverse. Inputs that go away on a switch take their cables with them ([[input-cable-pruning#The ordering rule]]). Binomial Range is the one exception and stays its own node.

## Why

The author's call ([[B11]] maximalMerge): fourteen distributions times their forms would otherwise be dozens of nodes to find and learn. Binomial Range stays separate because it needs a second x input, which the one-first-input shape can't give it.

## Consequences

- How the distributions are described and switched: [[formula-language]] § Distribution table.
- **Reopen if:** a distribution arrives that doesn't fit this shape (it needs a second x input, or a parameter that isn't a number).
