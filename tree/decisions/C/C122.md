## Decision

The Choice Ladder node picks a set of options from a Decision Matrix ranking within limits like a budget. It walks the ranking one rung at a time: a clear rung takes the best-ranked option that belongs in the best plan, and a rung whose best plan without that pick is within the weight wiggle forks into both. Its main line is always the best possible set.

## Why

Taking options straight down a ranking wastes the limits when the top pick is expensive, so the ladder skips an option that would crowd out more than it brings and says so; and where the weights can't tell two plans apart, showing both is more honest than picking one.
