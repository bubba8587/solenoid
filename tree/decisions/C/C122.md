## Decision

The Choice Ladder node picks a set of options from a Decision Matrix ranking within limits like a budget, walking the ranking one rung at a time toward the best plan. A rung is a toss-up only when a rival can't share a plan with the rung's pick and the weight wiggle can't tell their plans apart; the user may take the rival, the node remembers it, and the pick it displaced stays in the pool. With no picks made, the plan is the best possible set.

## Why

Taking options straight down a ranking wastes the limits when the top pick is expensive, so the ladder skips an option that would crowd out more than it brings and says so. Where the weights can't tell two plans apart the person should decide, and two options that fit together are never a choice between them.
