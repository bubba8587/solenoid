## Decision

A Pivot's or a Describe's popup opens on a view laid out for reading, while the node's output stays the plain Frame every node downstream reads. A Pivot's opens on a pivot table laid out the way Excel lays one out, with grouped labels that fold, and the columns in its Details field aren't summarized but open each row group's own source rows under it. Describe's opens on a Dashboard: one tile per column of the data it read, with how full it is, its key numbers and a small chart of what it holds.

## Why

Excel users read a pivot as a pivot table, with grouped labels and merged headers, not as the flat sheet PIVOTBY spills, and they check a summary by looking at the rows behind it. A Frame of means and quartiles says what a column holds only to someone who reads statistics; a histogram and the top values say it at a glance, on the one node for "what's in this data". The output itself has to stay a plain Frame, so every other node reads it the way it reads any Frame.

## Consequences

- *Exception:* the ribbon's Subtotals and Grand Totals are the Pivot's own totals setting, so changing them changes the Frame, as Configure Fields does. **Removed by:** totals becoming a setting of the view alone, so the Frame never carries them.
- The Grid, Cards and CSV views stay, showing the Frame as it is; Copy and Export copy the Frame.
- How it's built: [[table-popup#The Pivot view]] (with Details) and [[table-popup#The Dashboard view]].
- **Reopen if:** people want the laid-out view itself to flow to other nodes, the detail rows as their own Frame (a Filter's job), or the dashboard on the canvas as a chart value another node can take.
