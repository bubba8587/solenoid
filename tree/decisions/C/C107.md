## Decision

Solenoid ships an Obsidian plugin, Solenoid Properties, that shows Solenoid's kinds of value in a note's properties as the app's own chips, opening the app's own editors, while the note keeps plain YAML. Anything that needs a graph (computed columns, units, saved formats) is left out rather than faked.

## Why

Solenoid writes tables and cubes into notes, and in Obsidian those read as raw YAML, so the values Solenoid writes are the ones a reader can't see. Using the app's real chips and editors keeps a value looking and behaving the same in the note as on the canvas.

## Consequences

- How it's built, every difference from the full app, and the author's rulings on its details: [[obsidian-plugin]].
- **Reopen if:** Obsidian removes the way plugins add property types, or a chip would have to be redrawn to work there.
