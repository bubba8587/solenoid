## Decision

The app never uses the browser's own alert, confirm or prompt boxes. Its own notices and its own confirm dialog are the only interruptions.

## Why

The browser's boxes are unreliable in the desktop app and unstyled on the web, and the app should behave the same in both.

## Consequences

- How it's built: [[stores#Notices and confirmations]].
- **Reopen if:** a platform requires a native dialog for a permission.
