## Decision

The app never pops up the browser's own alert, confirm or prompt boxes. Its own notices and its own confirm dialog are the only interruptions: every new "are you sure" uses that confirm dialog, and every new message is a notice.

## Why

The browser's boxes are unreliable in the desktop app and unstyled on the web, and [[B2]] webTryDesktopFull needs both to behave the same. The app's own dialog also handles the keyboard like every other dialog and follows DESIGN.md. How it's built: the stores floor spec.

## Consequences

- **Reopen if:** a platform requires a native dialog for a permission. File access prompts belong to the operating system, not to the app, and do not count.
