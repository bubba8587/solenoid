## Decision

Every way of showing a table one record at a time (the Card, Gallery, Board and List views, and any future one) is a choice on the one Record node, never a separate node. Each view still has its own row in the Add menu ("Record: Gallery"), and you switch views on the node the way you switch a chart's type.

Entering data one record at a time is the Table popup's Form view: the same record look made editable, arranged by the same layout text. That layout is written on the Frame Input node, never in the popup. How it works: [[table-popup]] § The Form view.

## Why

The author's call ([[B11]] maximalMerge): a view is a way of presenting one figure, so it belongs on the one node. Each view still gets its own Add-menu row, like a chart type, because people search for a drawing by name, and typing "gallery" has to land on a row that says Gallery.

## Consequences

- The views have no formula names; figures never do.
- The Record node doesn't draw the figure on itself, where it would be squashed to the node's width. It shows a small Chart chip instead, and the drawn figure appears wherever its output goes (a Display, the popup, a Report). Record boxes touch and are square.
- **Reopen if:** a record view needs behavior a simple view choice can't give, or formulas ever need to name a record view.
