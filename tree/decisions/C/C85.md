## Decision

Expanding a group nudges its neighbors just far enough aside, and collapsing it slides them back exactly. A node you've moved yourself since then stays where you put it.

## Why

Where you put a node is deliberate, so opening a group should disturb as little as possible and closing it should undo that.

## Consequences

- How it's built: [[group-expand-push]].
- **Reopen if:** people want a whole-canvas re-layout on expand.

## Contest

### Contest 2026-09-24 by Claude Opus 5.5: keep wins

Opposite (ELK or a global re-layout on expand) cannot invert exactly and moves cards the expansion never touched; deletion leaves B10 reactFlowView unable to say why expand must be undoable. Keep: a deterministic push over plain boxes is headless per B10 and reverses exactly.
