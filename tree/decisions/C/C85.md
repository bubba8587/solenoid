## Decision

Expanding a collapsed group nudges its neighbors just far enough aside, the same way every time, and collapsing it again slides them back exactly where they were. The rest of the canvas is never re-laid out. A node you have moved yourself since the push stays where you put it.

## Why

Where you put a node is deliberate, so opening a group should disturb as little as possible and closing it should undo that completely. Your own move is the one placement you chose, so it wins.

## Consequences

- How it's built: [[group-expand-push]].
- **Reopen if:** people want a whole-canvas re-layout on expand rather than the least disturbance.

## Contest
