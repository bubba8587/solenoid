## Decision

When matching an Excel quirk would make the JS engine and Polars disagree, the quirk loses and the deviation is documented with `parity:false`. `0^0` is the standing example: Excel answers `#NUM!`, and everything else answers 1.

## Why

The two engines ([[B2]] webTryDesktopFull) must give one answer, and a documented deviation from Excel costs less than a document whose numbers change between web and desktop. [[A5]] already requires every divergence to be documented, so the cost is one note.
