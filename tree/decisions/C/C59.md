## Decision

When data is sorted, or two pieces of text are compared with `<` or `>`, text is ordered by its character codes, not by the rules of a language. The order is case- and accent-sensitive: "B" comes before "a", and "é" comes after "z". Lists in the app's own interface (menus, the Navigator) keep a friendly alphabetical order.

## Why

Language-aware sorting depends on settings of the computer it runs on, so the same document would sort differently from one machine to another. [[B2]] webTryDesktopFull also needs the desktop and web engines to sort identically, and character-code order is the one both give. The exact order, and the one rare gap between the two engines (some emoji), is [[frame-verbs]] § String order.

## Consequences

- **Reopen if:** users ask for language-aware sorting of their data. That needs a language fixed per document, recorded as a new decision.
