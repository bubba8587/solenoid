## Decision

The Equation node holds a relation, not a one-way formula: every variable is both an input and an output, so it solves for whichever one is left unwired, and a Check output says whether the relation holds. It uses its own small solver, never an outside algebra library. A quadratic gives every real root, smallest first. TVM, Compound Growth and Effective Rate are built the same way; on those, a setting is anything that changes the relation itself.

## Why

[[B11]] maximalMerge: one Equation node covers every relation that can be solved for any of its variables, instead of one solver node per formula. Owning the solver keeps it small and free of dependencies.

## Consequences

- **Deliberately not converted, so no one converts them later:** Depreciation; IPMT, PPMT, CUMIPMT and ISPMT (derived quantities, not relations); DOLLARDE and DOLLARFR; bonds and T-bills; and the DIST/INV pairs (no closed-form formula to invert).
- **Reopen if:** there is demand for cubic or higher roots (a cubic formula or a bundled algebra system).
- How the solver works: [[equation-solver]].
