## Decision

The Equation node is the acausal sibling of Expression, with fixed sockets: every variable is both an input and an output, and a logical `Check` output reports whether the relation holds. It solves by isolating the unknown in its own syntax tree, falling back to bracketing and bisection, with no computer-algebra dependency. A quadratic residual returns every real root in ascending order. TVM, Compound Growth and Effective Rate are built on the same framework; what counts as a configuration there is anything that changes the relation itself.

## Why

[[B11]] maximalMerge: one acausal card covers every relation that can be solved for any of its variables, instead of one solver node per formula. Owning the solver keeps it small and free of dependencies.

## Consequences

- **Deliberately not converted (relapse guard):** Depreciation; IPMT, PPMT, CUMIPMT and ISPMT (derived quantities, not relations); DOLLARDE and DOLLARFR; bonds and T-bills; and the DIST/INV pairs (no closed-form CDFs).
- **Reopen if:** there is demand for cubic or higher roots (Cardano's formula or a vendored computer-algebra system).
