## Decision

Every selection surface skips members of collapsed groups and nodes that isolate has receded, so a selection never holds something the user can't see, and deleting a collapsed group never deletes its members.

## Why

An action on something the user can't see is an action they didn't choose, and its result reads as data loss: an invisible selection turns into a mysterious delete.

## Consequences

- **Reopen if:** a real workflow needs to select hidden nodes by gesture. The Navigator and where-used already reach them.
