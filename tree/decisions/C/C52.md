## Decision

Selecting never picks up anything the user can't see: every way of selecting skips the members of a collapsed group and the nodes Isolate has faded out, rather than selecting them out of sight. So deleting a collapsed group never deletes its members.

## Why

[[B14]] oneDesignSystem holds the canvas to showing what there is. An action on something the user can't see is an action they didn't choose, and its result reads as data loss: an invisible selection turns into a mysterious delete.

## Consequences

- **Reopen if:** a real workflow needs to select hidden nodes by gesture. The Navigator and where-used already reach them.
