## Decision

Selecting only ever picks up what you can see. Every way of selecting skips the nodes inside a collapsed group and the nodes Isolate has faded out, so deleting a collapsed group never deletes its members.

## Why

An action on something you can't see is one you didn't choose. Deleting a collapsed group shouldn't quietly take its contents with it.

## Consequences

- How it's built: the lasso in [[pointer-gestures]], grouping in [[group-expand-push]].
- **Reopen if:** a real workflow needs to select hidden nodes by gesture. The Navigator and where-used already reach them.
