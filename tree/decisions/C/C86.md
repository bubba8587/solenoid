## Decision

A node joins or leaves a group only when you put it there or take it out: by dragging it in or out, grouping a selection, or resizing the box over it by hand. Fitting a group's box to its contents never changes who is in it, a collapsed group never gains a member, and a node belongs to at most one group.

## Why

Being in a group should be something you did, not an accident of where boxes happen to sit. Otherwise a resized box would swallow bystanders, and a node dropped on a small collapsed card would vanish into it.
