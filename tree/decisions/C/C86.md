## Decision

A node joins or leaves a group only when you put it there or take it out. Fitting a group's box never changes who is in it, and a collapsed group never gains a member.

## Why

Being in a group should be something you did, not an accident of where boxes happen to sit.

## Consequences

- How it's built: [[group-expand-push#Who is a member]].
- **Reopen if:** an action to absorb everything under a box is wanted.
