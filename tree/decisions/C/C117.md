## Decision

Text read as a number (VALUE, Cast, a typed cell, a pasted value) is read in the US form only: `.` is the decimal point and `,` groups thousands. A European form such as `1.234,5` is refused as not a number, never reread as a different one.

## Why

Solenoid reads numbers the way US Excel does, and one fixed reading makes the same text the same number on every machine. Guessing a locale would let `1.234` mean a thousand in one place and one point two in another, and a refusal is visible where a silent misreading is not.

## Consequences

- How each reader applies it: [[formulajs-divergences]] (*Text and number parsing*) and [[value-semantics]].
- **Reopen if:** users outside the US need their own number form, which would mean a per-document setting rather than a guess per value.
