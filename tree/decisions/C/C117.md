## Decision

Text read as a number is read in the US form only: `.` is the decimal point and `,` groups thousands. A European form such as `1.234,5` is refused, never reread as a different number.

## Why

One fixed reading makes the same text the same number on every machine, and a refusal is visible where a misreading is not.

## Consequences

- How it's built: [[formulajs-divergences]] (*Text and number parsing*) and [[value-semantics]].
- **Reopen if:** users outside the US need their own number form, as a per-document setting.
