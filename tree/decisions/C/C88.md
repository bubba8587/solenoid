## Decision

A group can collapse into a small card that shows only its results: the values that leave the group and the answers that go nowhere. The wiring decides which results show.

## Why

It's nice to have groups that collapse to take up a lot less space.

## Consequences

- How members hide, and which rows show, is in [[group-collapse]].
- **Reopen if:** people want to choose a group's readouts themselves.
