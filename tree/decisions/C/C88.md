## Decision

Collapsing a group turns it into one small card that reads like a single node: it shows what the group produces and hides the working. Its rows are the group's results, meaning every value that leaves the group and every answer that goes nowhere. A Display inside the group stands in for the value it shows. Cables coming in and going out stay connected and land on the card's edges. The rows follow from the wiring, so there is nothing to pick or keep up to date. Nothing inside stops computing.

## Why

A group is usually a piece of working toward a few answers. Once that working is trusted, the steps inside are clutter, and what belongs on the canvas is what the piece produces, the way a function hides its body. Reading the rows off the wiring keeps them right as the group changes and asks nothing of the user. An answer that goes nowhere counts because it is usually the thing the group was built to find, and a Display wins because someone put it there to be read.

## Consequences

- Going to a node inside a collapsed group frames the collapsed card. A pinned group shows the same rows.
- Which rows appear, and how the members hide without losing their layout, is in [[group-collapse]].
- **Reopen if:** people want to choose a group's readouts themselves, or want a collapsed group to show its inputs as well as its results.
