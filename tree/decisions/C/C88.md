## Decision

Collapsing a group changes nothing about computation: members stay wired and keep computing. Hiding is a `visibility` rule applied through a class on the React Flow node, never `display` and never the wrapper's inline style. What the collapsed card shows is derived, not chosen: exactly the members whose output leaves the group or goes nowhere, each as a readout row.

## Why

A hidden member must stay laid out, or its measured size and socket positions collapse to zero, which is why the rule uses `visibility` and not `display`. React Flow owns the wrapper's inline visibility and writes `visible` after measuring, so an earlier sweep that set each element's style was silently undone (seeds loaded with their members showing); a class rule survives. The rows are what the group means from outside, its terminals, so a value that goes nowhere counts as a terminal too.

## Consequences

- The full retain rule (the hop from a Display to its FC, the leaf-member case, no double counting) is in [[group-collapse]]. `groupReadouts()` and `recomputeGroupCollapse()` keep it identical; the first serves a pinned group and the second a collapsed one.
- Any "go to this node" path first resolves the nearest visible ancestor, and frames a collapsed group by its rendered size rather than its stored expanded one.
- **Reopen if:** collapse should also pause computation, or a member's readout should be chosen by the user rather than derived.
