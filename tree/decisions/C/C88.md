## Decision

Collapsing a group only changes what you see: its members stay wired and keep computing. The collapsed group shows a readout row for each member whose result leaves the group or goes nowhere, and nothing else. Those rows are worked out from the wiring, not picked by the user.

## Why

The rows are what the group means from outside, its end results, so a value that goes nowhere counts as an end result too. Hiding the members without pausing them means a collapsed group gives the same answers as an expanded one.

## Consequences

- The exact rule for which members get a row, and how hiding is done without losing the hidden members' layout, is in [[group-collapse]]. A pinned group shows the same rows.
- Going to a node inside a collapsed group shows the collapsed group, framed at its collapsed size.
- **Reopen if:** collapse should also pause computation, or a member's readout should be chosen by the user rather than derived.
