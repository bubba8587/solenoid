## Decision

A rule's exceptions are listed under that rule, never in a separate file, and each exception states the condition that would remove it.

## Why

An exception written beside its rule is seen every time the rule is read, so the list stays counted and the pressure on it is to shrink. An exception kept somewhere else goes unseen by the rule's readers and accumulates. An exception that no condition could ever remove is a sign the rule itself was written wrong, and the fix is to rewrite the rule ([[B9]] rulesWithoutMemory).
