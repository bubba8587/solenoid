## Decision

Extending the Pivot view ([[C127]] pivotView), a Pivot has a Details field beside Rows, Columns and Values. The columns put in it aren't summarized: in the Pivot view every row group gets a fold button that opens the group's own source rows under it, showing those columns. The Pivot's output Frame doesn't change, and with Details empty the Pivot behaves as it did before.

## Why

People check a summary by looking at the rows behind it, which Excel offers as Show Details on a new sheet. Seeing them in place, under their group, answers "which rows made this number" without leaving the pivot table, and keeping it out of the Frame keeps every downstream node reading the same cross-tab.

## Consequences

- Details opens leaf groups only, the rows of one full row tuple; a subtotal or the grand total opens nothing. A Pivot with no row fields has no group to open.
- What a group has open is the popup's state, never saved: a saved document opens with every group closed.
- A group shows at most 100 of its rows, with a line saying how many more it has.
- How it's built: [[table-popup#The Pivot view]].
- **Reopen if:** people want the detail rows as their own Frame, which is a Filter's job, or want a subtotal's whole group listed.
