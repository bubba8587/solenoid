## Decision

A column has one definition, on its header. No surface accepts a formula typed into a single cell.

## Why

[[A6]]: Excel lets every cell in a column hold a different formula, so reading the header tells you nothing about what the column does. That freedom is the source of the errors this surface removes. Ruled out permanently, like [[C14]] currentExcelParity.
