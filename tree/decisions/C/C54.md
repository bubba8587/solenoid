## Decision

A column has one definition, on its header, rather than a formula in each cell. No surface accepts a formula typed into a single cell.

## Why

[[B17]] typedValueModel puts a formula on a column, not on each cell. Excel lets every cell in a column hold a different formula, so reading the header tells you nothing about what the column does, and that freedom is the source of the errors this surface removes ([[A6]] divergeByDesign). Ruled out permanently, like [[C14]] currentExcelParity.
