## Decision

**MUST:** an outward effect such as an alert fires only on the edge where its status changes, re-arms when the status calms, and stays silent while `isGraphRebuilding()` is true. Status is compared by `statusKey`, so a range that moves from LOW to HIGH fires again, and in Boolean mode the condition is met when the value `=== 1`.

## Why

Ungated effects misfired both ways. A Connection node's auto-refresh interval inside a closed composite kept firing full recomputes forever, and switching into a document whose alert condition was already met fired the alert as if it had just happened. The post-load recompute runs inside the rebuild scope, so an effect that ignored the rebuild flag would replay its whole backlog on every document open, switch and rollback.
