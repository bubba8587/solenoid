## Decision

**MUST:** an effect that reaches outside the graph, such as an alert, fires only at the moment its status changes, never again while the status merely stays the same. It re-arms when things calm down, and it stays silent while a document is loading. A range reading that jumps from LOW to HIGH counts as a change and fires again.

## Why

Effects with no such gate misfired both ways. A Connection node's auto-refresh inside a closed composite kept triggering full recalculations forever, and switching to a document whose alert condition was already met fired the alert as if it had just happened. The recalculation right after a load counts as part of loading, so an effect that ignored loading would replay its whole backlog every time a document is opened, switched to or rolled back.
