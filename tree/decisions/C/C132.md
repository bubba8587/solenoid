## Decision

Several documents and several canvases are organized as one tree in the Navigator, not as a strip of sheet tabs: folders hold documents and other folders, and each document holds its own tree of pages. Values flowing between separate documents are a later decision; inside a document, its pages are one graph.

## Why

A tree lets people group their graphs however they like, folders inside folders, where a tab strip only ever gives one flat row; and a tree of folders and files is something people already know how to read.

## Consequences

- The Excel habit kept is a workbook with many sheets ([[B19]] spreadsheetHabits); the tab strip that shows them is what changes.
- A document with a single page shows no page row, so a one-canvas document looks as it does today.
- **Reopen if:** people working across many pages want them in view at once more than a tree can show.
