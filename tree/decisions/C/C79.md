## Decision

Switching a pack off hides its nodes from the Add menu and its functions from formula autocomplete. It doesn't remove them: a saved document that uses a switched-off pack still opens and computes, and its formulas still work.

## Why

A clean first look shouldn't cost anyone their work, so switching a pack off only hides it.

## Consequences

- A switched-off pack still ships with the app; leaving it out of the build is deliberately not done, and letting other people's packs load is a safety project of its own.
- Before the first third-party pack ships, a document needs to record which packs it uses and offer to switch them on when it opens; that is parked with pack distribution (`docs/deferrals.md`).
- A node from a pack this build doesn't have at all opens as a stand-in ([[C35]] unknownViaPlaceholder).
