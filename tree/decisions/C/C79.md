## Decision

Switching a pack off hides its nodes from the Add menu and its function names from formula autocomplete. It does not remove them: a saved document that uses a switched-off pack's node still opens and computes, and its formulas still work.

## Why

What the product needs now is a clean first look with most things dormant. A pack that, once switched off, turned a saved document's nodes into Placeholders or its formulas into `#NAME?` would lose the user's work ([[B12]] losslessSaves). The line between pack and core is drawn so that making each pack fully self-contained is a later tidy-up. Letting other people's packs load into the app is a safety project of its own, not a matter of tidying.

## Consequences

- A switched-off pack still ships with the app; leaving it out entirely is deliberately not built. Before the first third-party pack ships, a document needs to record which packs it uses and offer to switch them on when it opens; that is parked with pack distribution (`docs/deferrals.md`).
- A node from a pack that this build doesn't have at all opens as a Placeholder ([[C35]] unknownViaPlaceholder).
