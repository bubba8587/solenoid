## Decision

Switching a pack off hides its nodes from the Add menu and its formula names from autocomplete, but every pack's constructors stay registered and its formula functions keep resolving, so a saved graph that uses an inactive pack's node still loads and computes. This is isolation level 1.

## Why

Level 1, a clean core with most things dormant, is what the product needs now, and a pack that turned saved documents into Placeholders or `#NAME?` when switched off would be data loss ([[B12]] losslessSaves). The wall between pack and core is drawn so that level 2 (each pack self-contained, enforced by structure) is a later tidy-up. Level 3 (third-party packs loaded at runtime) is a safety project of its own, not a matter of code organization.

## Consequences

- "Code not loaded when off" is deliberately not built. A record of required packs and versions, with an offer to enable them on open, is parked with pack distribution (`docs/deferrals.md`) and must land before the first third-party or code pack ships.
- A pack type that no build registers at all loads through Placeholder ([[C35]] unknownViaPlaceholder).
