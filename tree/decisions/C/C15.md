## Decision

Formulas take matrices, as Excel's dynamic arrays do, and complex numbers through the IM functions. Frames and Cubes stay out of formulas; they belong to the Frame nodes.

## Why

Dynamic arrays are current Excel. Excel has no formula behavior for a whole table, and the Frame nodes already do that job.

## Consequences

- How it's built: [[formula-language]].
- **Reopen if:** broadcasting diverges from Excel in ways users hit.

## Contest

### Contest 2026-09-26 by claude: keep wins

keep: matrices are current Excel's dynamic arrays, so B16 wants them; Frames and Cubes have no Excel formula model to measure against. opposite (Frames in formulas) invents a surface B16 cannot measure and duplicates the Frame nodes. deletion leaves B16 silent on whether a table may enter a formula. Keep.
