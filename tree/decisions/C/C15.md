## Decision

Formulas take matrices, with Excel's dynamic-array behavior, and complex numbers through the IM* functions. Nothing wider than a matrix enters a formula: Frames and Cubes belong to the Frame nodes.

## Why

Dynamic arrays are current Excel, so matrices belong in formulas. Frames and Cubes stay out because Excel has no formula behavior for them to copy, they would compete with the Frame nodes, and handing a whole table to a formula would force loading data a large table otherwise leaves on disk.

## Consequences

- Per-row math on a Frame is a computed column ([[C22]] rowFormulaRefs), which sees one row's cells and whole columns, never the Frame itself.
- How it's built (containment, broadcasting, padding): [[formula-language]].
- **Reopen if:** broadcasting diverges from Excel in ways users hit. Nothing at the formula level reopens Frames or Cubes.

## Contest

### Contest 2026-09-26 by claude: keep wins

Keep.
