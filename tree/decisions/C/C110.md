## Decision

Range includes its Stop value: Start 1, Stop 5 gives 1 through 5, rather than stopping before 5 as Python's range does.

## Why

To most people "1 to 5" means 1 through 5, and LinSpace already includes its End. Excel has no RANGE to copy, so it's the author's call.

## Consequences

- How it's built: [[formula-language]] (*List kernels*).
- **Reopen if:** Excel ships a RANGE-style function with an exclusive stop.
