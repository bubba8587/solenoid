## Decision

Range includes its Stop value: Start 1, Stop 5, Step 1 gives 1, 2, 3, 4, 5, rather than stopping just before 5 as numpy's `arange` and Python's `range` do. The Series node's Range op and the RANGE formula agree.

## Why

"1 to 5" means 1 through 5 to most people, and LinSpace already includes its End, so the two Series ops that take an endpoint treat it the same way. Excel has no RANGE to copy, so this is the author's call.

## Consequences

- The Range description says it differs from numpy and Python, so someone porting code knows to adjust.
- How it's built: [[formula-language]] (*List kernels*).
- **Reopen if:** Excel ships a RANGE-style function with an exclusive stop.
