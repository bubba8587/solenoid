## Decision

A schedule is always recomputed from the task table, and four visible columns (Start, Finish, Deadline, Manual) carry one rule each. There are no hidden constraint types, per-task modes or project-wide switches.

## Why

The worst thing a scheduling tool can do is move your dates because of something you can't see.

## Consequences

- How it's built: [[schedule-and-gantt#The one rule]].
- **Reopen if:** imported plans keep needing a constraint the four columns can't express.
