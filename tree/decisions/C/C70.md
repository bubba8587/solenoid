## Decision

A schedule is always recomputed from the task table, and four visible columns carry one rule each: Start is the earliest a task may begin, Finish is a cap that shows as negative float when it can't be met, Deadline flags a task without moving it, and Manual pins a task in place. A gap between two tasks is a lag on the link between them. There are no constraint types, per-task scheduling modes or project-wide switches.

## Why

The worst thing a scheduling tool can do is move your dates because of something you can't see. Four visible columns with one rule each leave nothing hidden.

## Consequences

- How it's built: [[schedule-and-gantt#The one rule]].
- **Reopen if:** imported plans keep needing a constraint the four columns can't express. Even then it is named on import, never added as a fifth column.
