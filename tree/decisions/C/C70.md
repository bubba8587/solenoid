## Decision

A schedule is always recomputed from the plan, the Frame of tasks, and four visible columns (Start, Finish, Deadline, Manual) carry one rule each. There are no hidden constraint types, per-task modes or project-wide switches. The schedule works in whole working days or in working minutes, chosen on its node, and the choice changes how the plan is computed, not only how it's shown.

## Why

The worst thing a scheduling tool can do is move your dates because of something you can't see. What you see should be what was computed; rounding only the display makes two tasks look like they overlap.

## Consequences

- How it's built: [[schedule-and-gantt#The one rule]] and [[schedule-and-gantt#Dates and precision]].
- **Reopen if:** imported plans keep needing a constraint the four columns can't express, or a third precision is asked for.
