## Decision

Query, Solenoid's answer to Power Query, is an ordinary composite node in a pre-set form, never a new kind of node with its own step types or its own way of saving. Its steps are the usual table nodes placed inside it, and it runs only when you press Refresh, not on every change.

## Why

[[B11]] maximalMerge: a query is a composite that runs on demand, so it is a setting of the existing node rather than a new kind of node with its own way of saving.

## Consequences

- How the preset is built: [[composite-nodes]] § Manual refresh and Query.
- **Reopen if:** people need to preview or hold the result of each step, or need something a composite's inputs and outputs can't express.
