## Decision

Query, Solenoid's answer to Power Query, is an ordinary composite set up in advance: its steps are the usual table nodes inside it, and it runs when you press Refresh.

## Why

A query is just a composite that runs when you ask, so it doesn't need a kind of node of its own.

## Consequences

- How it's built: [[composite-nodes#Manual refresh and Query]].
- **Reopen if:** people need to preview or hold each step's result, or need something a composite's inputs and outputs can't express.
