## Decision

A gesture follows what is touching the screen (a finger, a mouse or a pen), not what kind of device it is. With a finger, dragging an unselected node or group pans the canvas and dragging a selected one moves it: tap, then drag. A mouse or a pen selects and drags in one motion. A pen counts as a precise pointer, and there is no palm rejection.

## Why

A tablet running the desktop layout should feel just like a phone under a finger, so the touch decides, not the device. A busy graph leaves no blank space to pan from, so an unselected node has to be somewhere a finger can pan from. Solenoid is a precise editor, not a drawing surface, so nobody rests a palm on it; these are the author's calls.

## Consequences

- Text fields don't stop a finger pan: a tap focuses one, a drag pans.
- How it's built: [[touch-gestures]] and [[pointer-gestures]].
- **Reopen if:** a drawing-style surface lands in the app; it would want the shape and pressure of each touch, not just whether a pen is in use.
