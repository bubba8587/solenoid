## Decision

**MUST:** a Frame or 2-D input's label says which columns the input expects, in one pattern, never a mix of patterns:
- **Expects specific columns:** the column roles in the expected order, joined by a plus, each a Title-case word: `From + To + Value`, `Label + Value`, `Date + Value`. A standard group of columns may shorten to its standard initialism (`Date + OHLC`), never spaced-out single letters.
- **No specific columns** (any Frame, or columns that are all alike): one plain Title-case noun (`Frame`, `Data`, `Series`, `Scores`).
- **Never the shape in brackets**, like `(2-D)` or `(list)`. The socket's shape and the Socket Legend already say it.

Exception: the λ-table nodes' inputs carry the λ argument's name and whether it is optional (`Values (value)`, `value2 (optional)`). That is a hint about binding, not a shape.

## Why

[[B14]] oneDesignSystem gives every string one voice. Parallel columns arrive as one Frame input, so the label is the only place the expected columns can be read before wiring, and two patterns side by side (`From + To + Value` beside `Series (2-D)`) would make that one important hint impossible to learn.
