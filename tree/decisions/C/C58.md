## Decision

What Table Input saves is the text you typed, exactly. No editor tidies or converts it; only the table worked out from it does (where a blank cell reads as empty). A blank line you typed stays wherever it is: at the start, in the middle or at the end.

## Why

Saving from the popup once rebuilt the text from the parsed table, and silently deleted rows ([[B12]] losslessSaves). When the typed text is what's saved, and the table is worked out from it, no way of editing can drop what the user typed.
