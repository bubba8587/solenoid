## Decision

What Table Input saves is the text you typed, exactly. No editor tidies or converts it; only the table worked out from it does (where a blank cell reads as empty). A blank line you typed stays wherever it is: at the start, in the middle or at the end.

## Why

[[B12]] losslessSaves: if the saved text were rebuilt from the table worked out of it, any row the reading step drops would be dropped from the document too, silently. Saving the typed text, and working the table out from it each time, means no way of editing can lose what the user typed.
