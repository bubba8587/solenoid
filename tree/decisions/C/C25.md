## Decision

A unit is part of a value, so 5 m and 5 km are different values. Every function and node carries units wherever its answer's kind follows from its inputs: a sum of dollars is dollars, a payment on a dollar loan is dollars, a distance over a time is a speed, and a computed column takes the unit its formula works out. Only Convert, or a calculation whose answer is a different kind of quantity (a rate, a count), changes a unit, and a Format Controller can't relabel one. A calculation refuses (`#UNIT!`) only where its inputs disagree or its answer's unit is genuinely ambiguous, and says which: two currencies never combine, and two temperature units never mix until one is converted.

## Why

A number's unit is part of what it is, and units are a headline reason to use Solenoid over Excel ([[A6]]). A unit a dropdown could relabel, or one a calculation quietly drops, makes every number with a unit untrustworthy; one a calculation refuses without a stated reason makes units a cost instead of a help. Solenoid has no exchange rates, so combining two currencies would invent one (even $10 ÷ 5 € = 2 is a made-up rate), and kelvin names both a temperature and a change in one, so mixing it with °C would be a guess.

## Consequences

- A unit sits exactly where values are sure to agree: on each item of a list, on each column of a Frame, on a matrix as a whole; never one per matrix cell or one for a whole list ([[unit-flow#One idea, a carrier per rank]]).
- A computed column with no authored unit takes the unit its formula works out from its columns and wired values, as Expression does: `@price * @qty` over a $ column is $, `@dist / @time` over km and h is km/h. An authored unit still wins, and a clash with it is `#UNIT!` ([[computed-columns]]).
- A finance function's money arguments share one currency, and a money result comes out in it; rates and period counts are plain numbers in and out, and a plain number where money goes reads in the money's currency. A rate or period count with a time unit (5%/yr, 30 yr) is `#UNIT!`, since turning an annual rate into a period rate takes a convention (nominal or effective) the inputs don't state.
- A currency is known by its code, and every operation that would mix two, multiply and divide included, is `#UNIT!` ([[unit-flow#Currency: the display code is the identity]]).
- A temperature difference stays a difference in its own unit: `30 °C - 20 °C` is 10 Δ°C, never 10 K, a spread such as STDEV is in Δ°C or Δ°F too, a difference converts by scale alone, and adding one to a reading gives a reading. A calculation that mixes two temperature units (`20 °C + 5 K`) is `#UNIT!` for now; `20 °C + 5` works as before.
- *Unenforced:* subtracting two readings gives K today, which Convert and the Format Controller then shift by the °C offset, and the Arithmetic card reads a K value as a change (`docs/backlog.md` § Leaves the code breaks).
- A function or node that doesn't carry units yet is a gap to close, tracked in the backlog, not a rule. Until it is closed, an unclassified formula function refuses a united argument, and a card that isn't unit-aware reads its inputs as the numbers they show (the fallback at the unit boundary in [[compute-pass]]).
- How it's built: [[unit-flow]].
- **Reopen if:** Solenoid gains exchange rates or a convention for rates per unit of time; people need to mix temperature units (K as a reading or as a change, with a rule for a difference added back) or want differences in K by default; per-column units on unnamed matrices are wanted (that is what a Frame is for); or a column's cells stop sharing one unit. A unit that could be relabeled or dropped is never a reason: it is a wrong number.

## Contest

### Contest 2026-10-22 by Claude Opus 5.5: keep wins

Rubric: [[B17]].
- keep: every calculation carries units where its answer's kind follows from its inputs, only Convert changes one, a refusal names its ambiguity. Cost: each function and card declares how its units flow; about 290 cards still strip at the boundary and are tracked as gaps.
- opposite: units are labels a calculation may drop; only arithmetic carries them. B17 says a value's unit is never dropped for convenience, so this undoes the parent.
- deletion: B17 names units but not where they change or what a function owes them; without this leaf each function and card re-decides between carrying, refusing and stripping, which is how the finance functions came to answer three ways.
- variant: refuse by default (an unclassified function answers #UNIT!) instead of carrying. Honest, but it leaves a united value with no way into the function (Convert can't make a plain number), so units become a cost; B17 keeps units to make values trustworthy, not to block them.
Verdict: keep.
