## Decision

A unit is part of a value, so 5 m and 5 km are different values. Only Convert, or a calculation whose result is a different kind of quantity, changes a unit; a Format Controller can't relabel one.

## Why

A number's unit is part of what it is. A unit a dropdown could relabel would make every number with a unit untrustworthy.

## Consequences

- How it's built: [[unit-flow]].
- **Reopen if:** nothing; a unit that could be relabeled is a wrong number.
