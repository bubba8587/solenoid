## Decision

Every scene on the marketing pages is the real app, real nodes computing a real graph, never a picture of it. Only one scene per page is live, so the visitor can work with it, popups such as the report and table views included; every other scene is locked.

## Why

[[B3]] sameNodeEverywhere requires the real product. The app can run only one fully working canvas per page, with its popups and overlays, so at most one scene can be live and the rest compute once and hold still.

## Consequences

- How it's built: the React Flow surface spec, *Marketing-page scenes*.
