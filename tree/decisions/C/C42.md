## Decision

While a big graph pans or zooms, an optional setting can draw snapshots of the cards in place of the live cards; at rest the live cards are back. The live cards are the default; the setting is offered only in browsers that support it.

## Why

Panning a big graph can stutter, and pictures of the cards move much more smoothly. It rests on an experimental browser feature, so it stays an extra and never a requirement. Keeping it is the author's call.

## Consequences

- How it's built, and every tuning knob: [[html-in-canvas]].
- **Reopen if:** the browser feature ships to everyone and the live-card path becomes the one that costs more to keep.
