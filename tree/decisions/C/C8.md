## Decision

**MUST:** every user-visible fact (a label, a name, an op's identity, an arity) has exactly one declaration, and every other surface derives from it. Copying a value into a second place is a defect even while the two copies agree.

## Why

Nothing keeps two copies equal, so they are already wrong; nobody has looked yet. The IS.TEST card once read `ISBOOLEAN` while Add-menu search offered `ISLOGICAL`, so you could read a name off a card and fail to find it in the menu. The cause was two `OPS` arrays, one in the component and one in `nodeOps.ts`.
