## Decision

A date is a real calendar day, without the 29 February 1900 that Excel's date system invents. Dates show as DD-MMM-YYYY (03-Jun-2026) by default.

## Why

A date should be a real date, never a wrong one, so Solenoid doesn't carry Excel's wrong calendar for compatibility.

## Consequences

- How it's built: [[formula-language#Dates and times]].
- **Reopen if:** documents must swap date numbers with Excel for days before March 1900.
