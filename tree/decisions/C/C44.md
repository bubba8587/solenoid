## Decision

A date is a real calendar day, counted in days, without Excel's 1900 date system and the 29 February 1900 it invents. Dates show as DD-MMM-YYYY (03-Jun-2026) by default, and ISO and other styles can be picked.

## Why

We want a date to be a real date, never a plain number and never a wrong one ([[B17]] typedValueModel), so Solenoid doesn't carry Excel's wrong calendar for compatibility.

## Consequences

- A year is always read as written: DATE(26, 1, 1) is the year 26, never 1926, and date text needs a four-digit year. One reading of a year everywhere beats matching Excel's two-digit guess.
- An imported column becomes dates only when every filled cell is an unambiguous ISO date; one cell that isn't keeps the whole column as text. Declining to guess is always safe, and a wrong date never is.
- How it's built: [[formula-language]] (dates).
- **Reopen if:** documents must swap date numbers with Excel for days before March 1900.
