## Decision

A date is a real day serial with no Excel 1900 date system. The default display is `DD-MMM-YYYY`, and ISO is a selectable style.

## Why

[[A6]]: Excel's 1900 date system reproduces Lotus 1-2-3's leap-year bug, a 29 February 1900 that never existed. Solenoid doesn't carry a wrong calendar for compatibility.

## Consequences

- Don't reintroduce 1900 compatibility to match an Excel serial.
- There is no two-digit-year century pivot anywhere. DATE's year is literal (DATE(26, 1, 1) is the year 26, never 1926, a named deviation from Excel), and date text needs a four-digit year to parse. One reading of a year on every surface beats matching Excel's pivot.
- Import inference turns text into dates only for unambiguous ISO forms, and one non-conforming cell keeps the whole column text. Declining to infer is always safe and a wrong serial never is; Get Column's read-as converts other date text explicitly.
