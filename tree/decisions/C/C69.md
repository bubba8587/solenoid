## Decision

The scheduling engine and the Gantt chart are built here, as three separate packages with their own licenses, rather than adopted from another project or folded into the app's own code. No GPL, EPL or commercial code enters them, and each package records what was studied while building it. How the app consumes them is in [[schedule-and-gantt]].

## Why

Scheduling serves the task data that [[B1]] obsidianBet brings in from TaskNotes, and no permissively licensed project has a real engine to adopt (`docs/v2.0/25-gantt.md` § 0), so the engine is built here. Keeping it in separate packages holds its license boundary and leaves a future publish open without changing how the app builds.

## Consequences

- **Reopen if:** a second consumer appears (then publish them), or the source-scan tests and the shared fixture directory stop reaching them.
