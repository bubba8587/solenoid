## Decision

The scheduling engine and the Gantt chart are built here, as separate packages with their own licenses, and no copyleft or commercial code goes into them.

## Why

No permissively licensed project had a real scheduling engine to adopt, and separate packages keep the license clean and free to publish later.

## Consequences

- How it's built: [[schedule-and-gantt]].
- **Reopen if:** a second project wants to use them; then they get published.
