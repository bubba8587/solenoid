## Decision

Each filter node does one job: List Filter keeps a list's items by their own values, and Frame Filter keeps a table's rows. Totalling or counting the matches is the job of SUMIFS and its siblings, never a filter's.

## Why

A second job isn't a variant: if a filter could do two jobs, you couldn't tell from the node which one it was doing.

## Consequences

- How it's built: [[formula-language]] and [[frame-verbs]].
- **Reopen if:** filtering one list by another, without totalling, turns out to be common enough to be worth the confusion.
