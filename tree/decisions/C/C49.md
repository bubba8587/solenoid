## Decision

Each filter node has one job. List Filter tests a list's items on their own values only, and never filters one list by another. Filtering a table is the Frame Filter's job, and totalling or counting the rows that match is the job of SUMIFS and its siblings, never a filter's.

## Why

We fold variants of one job into one node, but a second job isn't a variant. If a filter could do two jobs, you couldn't tell from the node which one it was doing.

## Consequences

- A grid is filtered by flattening it first (TOCOL, then Filter); no filter works cell by cell on a grid.
- **Reopen if:** filtering one list by another, without totalling, turns out to be common enough to be worth the confusion.
