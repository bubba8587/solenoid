## Decision

**MUST:** every function's answers are checked against one record for that function, which lists its cases with Excel's answer beside Solenoid's and says why any two differ. Excel's answer in a record is only ever what Excel computed when it opened the case; an answer copied from a document, remembered, or taken from another program is not Excel's. Every function is asked the same edge cases, drawn from what each of its arguments can receive, and a case without an answer shows as a gap.

## Why

Excel parity means Excel's real answers ([[A5]]), and they kept being assumed or missed while each test kept its own expected values in its own place. One record per function, with Excel's side filled by Excel itself, puts every answer, every difference and every gap where they can be seen together.

## Consequences

- Solenoid's own past answers (the frozen fixtures) tell whether an answer changed, never whether it is right, so they never stand in for Excel's.
- A difference from Excel is accepted only as the same under a written rule, a deliberate difference citing the leaf that decided it, or a known bug with its backlog line.
- A function Excel doesn't have still gets a record: its cases are answered by Solenoid's own rules or another reference, and say so.
- How it's built: [[function-records]].
- *Unenforced:* the records, the importer that alone writes Excel's answers, and the readiness check are not built yet.
- **Reopen if:** Excel stops being the reference the people using Solenoid know, or the records can no longer be opened in Excel.
