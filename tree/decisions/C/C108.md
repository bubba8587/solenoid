## Decision

When an audit finds behavior that is defensible but worse than an available alternative, the default outcome is to fix it, not to record it as acceptable. Leaving it takes a stated reason.

## Why

A finding filed as acceptable settles into the record as a decision nobody made, and the next session reads it as intended. [[B9]] rulesWithoutMemory: a known-worse behavior that isn't fixed now is forgotten, not deferred.
