## Decision

Checking whether two texts are equal ignores case, as Excel's `=` does. Telling rows apart, as joins and grouping do, respects case.

## Why

Excel's `=` ignores case, but merging "Apple" and "apple" into one group would quietly destroy a distinction in the data.

## Consequences

- How it's built: [[formula-language#Scalar operators]] and [[frame-verbs]].
- **Reopen if:** people more often want "Apple" and "apple" grouped together than kept apart.
