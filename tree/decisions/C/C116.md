## Decision

Scatter, XY Line and Bubble stay three separate types on the Chart node, grouped as one XY family apart from the charts that plot against labels. The option not taken was folding them into one Scatter type with settings. There is no combined bars-and-lines type: that figure is Merge Plots over a Column chart and a Line chart.

## Why

Connected scatter and bubble are the names people look for, and as settings on one type they would only be found by someone who already knew them; a family of their own says they are one figure without hiding the names. A fixed bars-and-lines type would be a second, narrower way to draw what Merge Plots already draws. The author's call.

## Consequences

- How it's built: [[chart-figures#Chart]].
- **Reopen if:** the XY types' defaults converge so their names no longer mean different starting points, or charts with bars and lines from one table turn out common.
