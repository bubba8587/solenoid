## Decision

Scatter, XY Line and Bubble stay separate types on the Chart node, grouped under one XY family apart from the Cartesian charts that plot against labels. The Chart node has no combined bars-and-lines type; that figure is Merge Plots over a Column chart and a Line chart.

## Why

The three draw on the same numeric x/y plane and differ only in their starting settings, so folding them into one type would be the cleanest menu, but connected scatter and bubble are the names people look for, and as options they would only be found by someone who already knew them. A family of their own says they are one figure without hiding the names. Composed was a fixed bars-then-lines overlay that Merge Plots already draws with any mix of marks, so it was a second, narrower route to one figure.

## Consequences

- The Chart card's families are Cartesian, XY and Categorical; the Chart Builder lists Merge Plots under Cartesian.
- A table whose bars and line sit in one frame is split into two charts before Merge Plots.
- **Reopen if:** the XY types' defaults converge so their names no longer mean different starting points, or charts with bars and lines from one table turn out common.
