## Decision

A blank and an error are values in their own right, in a single value and in every cell of a list or table. A blank is a missing value that totals skip; an error in one cell spreads to whatever uses that cell and leaves the other cells alone. True/false is its own kind of value, and a test on a blank answers unknown rather than false.

## Why

We want a value to say what it is ([[B17]] typedValueModel), and a missing value, an error and a zero are three different facts. A calculator that mixes them up answers with believable wrong numbers.
