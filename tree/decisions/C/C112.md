## Decision

Every layout action (expanding or collapsing a group, Tidy, fitting a group, grouping) ends by pulling apart anything that overlaps, anywhere on the canvas, including overlaps that were there before. Only a group locked in place never moves. Your own moves (a drag, Align, paste) are not layout actions: things stay where you put them.

## Why

Overlapping nodes look broken, and nobody overlaps them on purpose. The promise only holds if nothing is exempt.

## Consequences

- A node that grows with its content can cover a neighbor until the next layout action; whether growth should push is open with the author.
- How it's built: [[group-expand-push]].
- **Reopen if:** the author wants a kind of overlap kept, such as a Note deliberately laid over a node.

## Contest

### Contest 2026-09-24 by Claude Opus 5.5: keep wins

Opposite (overlaps allowed, or best-effort) is what B14 oneDesignSystem rules out: an overlap reads as a broken canvas. Deletion leaves no node owning the final pass, which is how two exemptions crept back in. Variant (exempt overlaps the user made before the op) was tried and broke the guarantee twice. Keep.
