## Decision

Every layout action ends by pulling apart anything that overlaps, anywhere on the canvas. Your own moves (a drag, Align, paste) aren't layout actions: things stay where you put them.

## Why

Overlapping nodes look broken, and nobody overlaps them on purpose.

## Consequences

- How it's built: [[group-expand-push#What is not a layout op]].
- **Reopen if:** the author wants a kind of overlap kept, such as a Note laid over a node.

## Contest

### Contest 2026-09-24 by Claude Opus 5.5: keep wins

Opposite (overlaps allowed, or best-effort) is what B14 oneDesignSystem rules out: an overlap reads as a broken canvas. Deletion leaves no node owning the final pass, which is how two exemptions crept back in. Variant (exempt overlaps the user made before the op) was tried and broke the guarantee twice. Keep.
