## Decision

Content entering or leaving the app is made safe at the one place it crosses, so every new way in or out gets the guard.

## Why

Opening someone else's document should never act or reach out on its own, so no path in or out can be allowed to forget the guard.

## Consequences

- How it's built: [[reports-and-notes#What is sanitized, and where]], with the network gate in [[live-connections#The network gate]].
- **Reopen if:** something needs to run untrusted script on purpose.

## Contest

### Contest 2026-09-26 by claude: keep wins

keep: B18 says opening a document never acts or reaches out; one guard per seam is the cheapest way to make every new path inherit the guard. opposite (guard per feature) is how a feature forgets. deletion leaves B18 with no enforcement shape. Keep.
