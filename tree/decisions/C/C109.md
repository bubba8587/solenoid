## Decision

On Linux the desktop app draws its own minimize, maximize and close buttons and its own window edges, sitting in the menu bar like on Windows. Windows keeps the ready-made buttons, which bring its layout menu on hovering maximize.

## Why

The ready-made buttons don't work on Linux, and the desktop is the full product, so its window buttons have to work.

## Consequences

- How it's built: [[layout-chrome#The desktop window frame (the Tauri shell)]].
- **Reopen if:** the ready-made buttons work on Linux, or Windows moves to the app's own buttons too.
