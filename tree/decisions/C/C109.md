## Decision

On Linux the desktop shell draws its own window controls (`WindowControls.tsx`) and resize grips on an undecorated window. Windows keeps the decorum plugin's overlay controls. The mechanics are in [[layout-chrome]].

## Why

decorum 1.1.1 on Linux leaves the native title bar in place, injects its control group twice (once per page-load event, with no guard), and builds its buttons from GNOME's `button-layout`, where `:minimize` gets no click handler. The desktop is the full product ([[B2]] webTryDesktopFull), so its window chrome has to work.

## Consequences

- **Reopen if:** decorum fixes all three problems, or the app moves Windows onto `WindowControls` too, which gives up the Snap Layouts hover.
