## Decision

The `.sol-landing` wrapper overrides the four accent variables (`--accent`, `--accent-soft`, `--accent-mid`, `--accent-ink`) with the brand coil gold. Only chrome that reads them changes color; the node and socket colors on the scene canvases use their own variables and are untouched.

## Why

The marketing pages are Solenoid's storefront, not the visitor's workbench ([[B3]] sameNodeEverywhere), so they show the brand accent whatever accent the visitor picked in the app.
