## Decision

The marketing pages always use the brand gold as their accent, whatever accent the visitor picked in the app. Only the page chrome changes; the node and socket colors in the scenes are the app's own and are untouched.

## Why

[[B3]] sameNodeEverywhere keeps the scenes the app's own, node and socket colors included. The page around them is not the app: it is Solenoid's storefront, not the visitor's workbench, so its chrome carries the brand rather than whichever accent the visitor happens to use in the app.

## Consequences

- How it's built: the palette-and-theme spec, *The theme*.
