## Decision

The marketing pages always wear the brand gold, whatever accent the visitor picked in the app. The nodes in their scenes keep the app's own colors.

## Why

The marketing pages are Solenoid's storefront, so they should look like the brand every time.

## Consequences

- How it's built: [[palette-and-theme#The theme]].
- **Reopen if:** the site should preview the visitor's own accent.
