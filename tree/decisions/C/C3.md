## Decision

The marketing pages always use the brand gold accent and the Default palette, in light or dark mode, whatever palette and accent the visitor set in the live app. A live app scene on a marketing page uses the Default palette, the page's light or dark mode and the app's true node colors; the gold accent doesn't override them.

## Why

There's not much in the app to market with. Besides the Frame, the socket designs aren't really marketable, and every icon is Lucide. The things the brand can reinforce and protect are the SOLENOID wordmark with its icon, and its color, so the marketing has to hold onto those tightly.

## Consequences

- How it's built: [[palette-and-theme#The theme]].
- *Unenforced:* only the four accent variables are pinned to gold; the visitor's own palette still reaches the site's chrome and the scenes' node colors (`docs/backlog.md` § Leaves the code breaks).
- **Reopen if:** the site should preview the visitor's own palette or accent, or the app gains a marketable look of its own beyond the wordmark and its color.
