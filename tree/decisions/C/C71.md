## Decision

The Gantt chart is for looking at, and you can't drag its bars to change the plan. Every edit happens in the task table, through the Cube Input popup.

## Why

The chart is drawn by a different node from the one that holds the tasks, often after a filter or a join, so a chart that wrote back would be a second, hidden way to edit the table. The author's call. Why it is also the only rule that can be built: [[schedule-and-gantt]] § The figure never writes.

## Consequences

- **Reopen if:** the author asks. It would then come back the way the Record node picks a record (the chart only picks a task, and another node follows that pick by name), written as a reopening, and never as the chart editing the table.
