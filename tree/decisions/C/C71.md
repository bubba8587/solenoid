## Decision

The Gantt chart is for looking at: every edit to the plan happens in the plan itself, the Frame of tasks, never by dragging bars.

## Why

The Frame of tasks is where the plan lives, so a chart that also edited would be a second, hidden way to change it.

## Consequences

- How it's built: [[schedule-and-gantt#The figure never writes]].
- **Reopen if:** editing on the chart is wanted.
