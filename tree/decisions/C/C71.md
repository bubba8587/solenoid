## Decision

The Gantt chart is for looking at: every edit to the plan happens in the task table, never by dragging bars.

## Why

The task table is where the plan lives, so a chart that also edited would be a second, hidden way to change it. The author's call.

## Consequences

- How it's built: [[schedule-and-gantt#The figure never writes]].
- **Reopen if:** the author asks for editing on the chart.
