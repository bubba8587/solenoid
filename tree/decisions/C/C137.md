## Decision

Wherever a date goes (a card's output, a column, a list, a popup, a readout, an export, a text built from it), it stays a date and shows as one. The only way to get its serial number is to ask for it with Cast to Number. A step that would leave a date's type unknown has to keep it, and one that would mix dates with another kind in one value refuses with #TYPE! and says to Cast one side, rather than handing on a mix of dates and numbers.

## Why

A date shown as 46037 is wrong in a way the user can't trace: nothing on the canvas says a conversion happened. A value's type is part of what it is ([[B17]] typedValueModel), and kinds never mix on their own ([[C10]] socketLattice), so the one conversion a user asked for, Cast, is the only one that may happen (the author, 2026-11-03: 'never, ever').

## Consequences

- Results that are still dates stay dates: the min, max, median or mean of dates, a lookup of a date, a branch that is a date, a shifted or rounded date ([[C136]] typesKnownAfterCalc, [[C118]] formatTravelsWithValue).
- A card that would put dates and another kind in one output (joining lists, an IF over a list of conditions, a lookup across columns of different kinds) answers #TYPE! instead.
- Real numbers made from dates are not serials and are fine: YEAR, a span between two dates, a count.
- **Reopen if:** a surface needs the raw serial for a reason Cast can't serve.
