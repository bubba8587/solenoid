## Decision

When a formula is committed with more `(` than `)`, the missing `)` are added at its end, rather than closing parentheses as you type.

## Why

Leaving off closing parentheses is the commonest slip in a long formula, and it has one obvious fix, so an error only costs a trip back.

## Consequences

- How it's built: [[formula-language#Syntax hints]].
- **Reopen if:** a formula surface commits on every keystroke.
