## Decision

When a formula is committed (the formula popup closes, or an Fx column's formula row is left) with more `(` than `)`, the missing `)` are added at its end. It is a count and nothing more: parentheses inside quoted text don't count, and a formula with too many `)` is left alone.

## Why

[[B16]] oneFormulaSurface measures against current Excel, which offers the same repair when a formula is entered short of its closing parentheses. Leaving them off is the most common slip in a long nested formula like an IFS over QUARTILEs, and it has one obvious fix, so a syntax error for it only costs a trip back into the editor.

## Consequences

- The repair happens on commit, never while typing, so the text doesn't change under the cursor.
- *Rejected:* inserting `)` as `(` is typed. It fights typing over the closer, and the author asked for the count only.
- **Reopen if:** a formula surface commits per keystroke, where the count would add parentheses mid-typing.
