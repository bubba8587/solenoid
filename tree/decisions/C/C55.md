## Decision

When the AI edits a document, it writes the whole document out again. The app checks it like an opened file and shows you the difference to apply or cancel.

## Why

A whole document is exactly what the open-file check reads, while piecemeal edits would need checks of their own and could half apply.

## Consequences

- How it's built: [[command-palette#AI mode]].
- **Reopen if:** documents grow too big to rewrite in one go, or outside AI agents get a tool interface of their own.
