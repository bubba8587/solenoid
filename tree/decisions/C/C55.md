## Decision

An AI edit is a whole-document rewrite of the text form: the model emits a full replacement, the strict validator gates the apply, and the approval step shows the diff from old to new. There is no edit-operation layer.

## Why

[[B13]] aiInScope requires an AI edit to take the same validated, approved path as a human's, and the text form already is that path. An edit-op layer would be a second grammar and a second validator, with partly applied states to handle, and at the size of document a model works on it would be pure cost.

## Consequences

- **Reopen if:** documents outgrow reliable whole-document regeneration, or the MCP port ships (its typed tools would be the op layer).
