## Decision

When the AI edits a document, it writes the whole document out again in its plain-text form. The app checks that text as strictly as it checks a file being opened, and then shows you the difference between old and new for you to apply or cancel. The AI never makes small piecemeal edits.

## Why

[[B13]] aiInScope requires an AI edit to take the same checked, approved path as a human's, and opening a document from its text form already is that path. A separate language of small edits would need its own checks and would have to cope with edits that half apply. At the size of document an AI works on, it would cost a lot and give nothing.

## Consequences

- The AI can't change the document on its own: its rewrite only ever reaches the approval step. How the rewrite is checked, repaired and shown is [[command-palette]] § AI mode.
- **Reopen if:** documents grow too big to rewrite reliably in one go, or Solenoid gains a tool interface for outside AI agents, whose tools would then be the piecemeal edits.
