## Decision

When the AI edits a document, it writes the whole document out again. The app checks that as strictly as a file being opened, then shows you the difference for you to apply or cancel. The AI never makes small piecemeal edits.

## Why

An AI edit should be checked the same way as an opened file, and a whole document is exactly what that check reads. Piecemeal edits would need checks of their own and could half apply.

## Consequences

- How it's built: [[command-palette#AI mode]].
- **Reopen if:** documents grow too big to rewrite reliably in one go, or Solenoid gains a tool interface for outside AI agents, whose tools would then be the piecemeal edits.
