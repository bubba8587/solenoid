## Decision

A node's color is its family's color, and placing a node in a family is how its color is chosen. A node no family lists falls back to math blue, unless it has exactly one output that isn't a number: then it wears that output's color.

## Why

A node's color tells you what it makes. The blue fallback only exists so every node has a color, and a text or date result on a blue node would read as a number.

## Consequences

- How it's built: [[palette-and-theme#Card colors]].
- **Reopen if:** the nodes no family lists get a family of their own.
