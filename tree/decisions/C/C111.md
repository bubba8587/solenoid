## Decision

A card's accent is its family's color: the node kinds in `nodeKindOf` (input, math, logic, text, date, complex, list, table, frame, chart, document and the rest) are the families, and a card listed under one keeps that color. A card that no family lists falls back to math, and when such a card has exactly one output whose type isn't a number or a wildcard, it wears that output socket's color instead (`nodeAccent`, `explicitKindOf`). Numeric fallbacks stay math blue, since a card that makes a number is math in all but name.

## Why

The fallback exists so every card has a color, not to say the card is math. A text or date result on a blue card reads as a number; its output color already says what it makes, and families stay as they are because they were chosen (the author, 2026-09-23).

## Consequences

- Placing a card in a family is still the way to pick its color; the rule only reaches cards nobody placed.
- A wildcard output (`any`, `anycombo`, `trueany`) carries no family, so such a card keeps the fallback.
- **Reopen if:** the numeric fallbacks get a family of their own, or a card's color should follow a changing output type, as the socket-driven cards (List Input, Table Input, Sets, the Format Controller) already do.
