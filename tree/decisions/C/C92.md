## Decision

A two-finger pinch always zooms the canvas, whatever it starts on: no node or control can stop it, and it never selects anything. Panning and dragging a node, by contrast, give way to a control that wants the touch for itself, such as a slider, a dial or a text field.

## Why

On a phone or tablet the pinch is how you get around, so it has to work wherever your fingers land. A pinch that something under the fingers could swallow, or that died halfway, would make the canvas feel broken.

## Consequences

- How it's built: [[pointer-gestures#The ordering rule]].
- **Reopen if:** a control turns up that truly needs two fingers for itself.
