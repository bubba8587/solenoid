## Decision

When a LAMBDA is wired into MAP, BYROW, BYCOL, REDUCE, SCAN or MAKEARRAY, its parameters are matched to that function's values by name, in any order, rather than by position as in Excel. A name the function doesn't offer is an error. The option not taken, filling those names in automatically whatever is wired, would leave inputs that do nothing.

## Why

On a canvas you can't see the order of the wires, so matching by position would quietly swap arguments. Matching by name turns a mismatch into an error you can see.
