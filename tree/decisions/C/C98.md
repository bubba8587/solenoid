## Decision

The Command Palette is the menubar in searchable form, plus the canvas and selection actions that have no menu. Node types stay out: the Add menu is the one place to browse them. A setting that is greyed out on this device can't be flipped from the palette either.

## Why

Two separate lists of the same actions would drift apart, so every menu item is a palette command automatically. One place to find nodes keeps the catalog easy to learn, and the palette shouldn't be a back door around a setting this device has switched off.

## Consequences

- How it's built: [[command-palette]].
- **Reopen if:** the Add menu and the palette merge into one launcher.
