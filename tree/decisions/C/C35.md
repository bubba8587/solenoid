## Decision

A node the app doesn't know, say from a switched-off pack, opens as a stand-in that keeps its settings, values and connections, and saves back exactly as it was. Its outputs show #REF!, never a blank.

## Why

Switching a pack off has to be safe, so its nodes come back intact when it's on again, and the error keeps the gap visible meanwhile.
