## Decision

**MUST:** when a document holds a node the app doesn't know (say, from a pack that is switched off), the node opens as a stand-in that keeps its settings, its typed values and its connections, and saving writes it back exactly as it was. Its outputs show `#REF!`, never a silent blank.

## Why

Switching a pack off has to be safe: open a document with the pack off, save it, and the pack's nodes must come back intact when the pack is on again. The error keeps the gap visible meanwhile.
