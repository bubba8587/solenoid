## Decision

The first round of node merges produced these cards:
- **Hypothesis Test:** Z, three T variants, F and chi-square as six flat ops. The two-sample ops share input keys, so switching between them keeps cables.
- **Rank & Percentile:** LARGE, SMALL, RANK.EQ, RANK.AVG, PERCENTILE, QUARTILE and PERCENTRANK, with the INC and EXC forms as ops.
- **Series:** Range, SEQUENCE and LinSpace. Geometric, Fibonacci, Repeat and RANDARRAY stay separate (the author's call).
- **NPV and IRR** absorb XNPV and XIRR: a Periodic/Dated toggle reveals the Dates input.
- **Workdays:** WORKDAY and NETWORKDAYS as inverse forms; the op retypes the output in place.
- **Depreciation** absorbs VDB, through a per-op spec table.
- **List Take/Drop**, matching its table sibling.
- **Surface** absorbs Contour, with a 3-D/Flat view toggle.

## Why

The author approved the batch ([[B11]] maximalMerge). Flat ops were preferred over a second picker wherever the number of combinations is small, because per-op hover descriptions keep working and no new saved field is needed.

## Consequences

- Every merge keeps its old Add-menu leaf types, so `NODE_EXCEL` and the Function Reference are untouched. It prunes departing sockets' cables first ([[D10]] onePrunePath), and an old save of a retired type loads as a Placeholder ([[B7]] preAlphaBreakFreely).
- Later merges followed the same pattern: TREND into FORECAST.LINEAR and GROWTH, LINEST into LOGEST, PHI and GAUSS into Distribution, Select and Drop Columns into Columns, and Text Filter into List Filter.
- **Parked pending the author's review:** the paired-list aggregate (SUMPRODUCT, the SUMX functions, CORREL, COVARIANCE and a weighted average as one two-list Aggregate; the author said to wait), and the remaining smaller pairs.
- **Reopen if:** a merged family needs per-op behavior the spec table can't express, or an op needs its own formula-name treatment.
