## Decision

Isolate, Isolate chain and Where used are switched off: no menu item, command, shortcut or gesture enters an isolate view. The code that draws one stays in place, behind one switch, so the call can flip back.

## Why

The author wants them out of the way for now and may bring them back, so they are switched off rather than removed.

## Consequences

- The I key does nothing, and the node menu and command palette list no isolate items.
- **Reopen if:** the author wants an isolate view back; turning the switch on restores every entry point.
