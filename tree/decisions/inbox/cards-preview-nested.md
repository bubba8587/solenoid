## Decision

A cube card shows a preview of a nested list, table, Frame or Cube (first names, a mini table, or counts) instead of only its drill chip.

## Why

The author asked for it (2026-10-05); the layout is a design call, and table-popup § The Cards view needs the rules first.

## What ratifying means

- **Ratify:** example layouts are explored and the card-interpreter rules written before building.
- **Reject:** cards keep the drill chip only.
- **Lean:** ratify, starting with counts, which need no layout.
