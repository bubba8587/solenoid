## Decision

Copy and paste carries a card's pins (values lifted onto the always-visible overlay), its comments, and any standoff (a fixed spacing link) between two copied cards. Today, pasting a card with a comment gives a copy with no comment, while a pasted composite keeps its inner cards' comments. After, both keep them.

## Why

Plain cards and composites disagree. These are notes about one spot on the canvas, so a copy may or may not want them.

## What ratifying means

- **Lean:** Ratify. A copy that looks like the original but loses its comments is the surprise, and one rule for both is simpler.
- **Ratify:** Pasting brings pins, comments and standoffs; pasting a card twice gives two pinned copies with the same comments.
- **Reject:** Pasting leaves all three behind, and a pasted composite's inner cards drop them too. A middle way carries comments and standoffs but not pins, since a pin marks the one card you are watching.
