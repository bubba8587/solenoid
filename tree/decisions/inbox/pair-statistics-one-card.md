## Decision

Three cards become one with nine choices: CORREL, RSQ, SPEARMAN, KENDALL, COVARIANCE.P, COVARIANCE.S, SLOPE, INTERCEPT and STEYX. Today the Add menu shows three cards. After, one, and switching it from CORREL to SLOPE keeps both cables attached. Every formula name and answer stays the same. Smaller merges, each a separate yes or no: FIXED with DOLLAR; REDUCE with SCAN; Append with Bind Columns; Web Source, Import HTML and Import XML as one fetcher; and Group Lists, retired for GROUPBY or kept.

## Why

The three cards read two paired lists the same way and differ only in the math, which the merge rule says makes them one card.

## What ratifying means

- **Lean:** Ratify the main merge. Of the smaller ones, take REDUCE/SCAN and Append/Bind Columns, and keep Group Lists as the quick one-dimensional version.
- **Ratify:** One card in place of three; saves using the old cards break (pre-alpha).
- **Reject:** The three cards stay separate.
