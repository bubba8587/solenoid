## Decision

A pasted card, or one added from the Add menu, lands at the nearest spot to the pointer that overlaps nothing. Today, pasting with the pointer over a card drops the new card on top of it. After, the new card lands a short hop away.

## Why

A review treated placing at the pointer as manual placement, which the no-overlap rule exempts.

## What ratifying means

- **Lean:** Ratify. You didn't pick the exact pixel, so shifting it costs nothing, and no overlaps ever is your rule.
- **Ratify:** New cards never land on top of another card.
- **Reject:** A new card lands exactly at the pointer and can cover a card, and the exemption stays.
