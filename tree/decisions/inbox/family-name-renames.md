## Decision

`IFErrorNode` → "If Error", `MatDetNode` → a family name for MDETERM/MINVERSE/TRACE/NORM/MATRIXRANK ("Matrix"?), `MRoundNode` → a name for MROUND/CEILING/FLOOR; and `nodeTypeName` learns acronyms and hyphens ("URL Encode", "E-Series") or those stay "Url Encode"/"ESeries".

## Why

D22 titles a card with its class-derived name; these read awkwardly.

## What ratifying means

- **Ratify:** the author picks the two names; the classes rename and seeds update.
- **Reject:** the names stay.
- **Lean:** ratify "If Error" and the acronym rule; pick the other two.
