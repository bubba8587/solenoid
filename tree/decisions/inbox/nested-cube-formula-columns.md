## Decision

Fx columns stay top-level on Cube Input; nested levels get none.

## Why

Types reach every level (D90); formula columns on nested levels were left "if wanted".

## What ratifying means

- **Ratify:** no change.
- **Reject:** nested levels get Fx columns.
- **Lean:** ratify until a plan needs one.
