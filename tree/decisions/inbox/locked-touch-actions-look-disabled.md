## Decision

On a locked canvas, the phone's ➕ button and the tablet's edit buttons are drawn dimmed, as disabled controls are. Today they look live but do nothing while locked. After, they stay in place, dimmed.

## Why

They give no sign that the lock is why nothing happens.

## What ratifying means

- **Lean:** Ratify. The bar keeps its layout and you can see why nothing happens.
- **Ratify:** The buttons stay in place and look disabled while locked.
- **Reject:** They look live and do nothing, as today. The other option hides them while locked, which changes the bar's shape.
