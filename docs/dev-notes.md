### SESSION DIGEST (2026-10-22: units in popup cells and unit fixes from the solenoid/tsrs port; cloud session)
- **A popup cell writes its number's unit** (`100 km`, `2 hr`), Table and Cube popups and their footers; the overflow menu's Hide units in cells is the app-wide setting `tablePopupCellUnits`, on by default (author). Copy and Export stay unit-free; the CSV view is the grid's text. Spec: table-popup § Units in cells.
- **A computed column infers its unit from its formula** ([[D100]], unratified), Frames and Cubes; an indeterminate one (`POWER(@d, 2)` over km) keeps its shown numbers. REDUCE over a plain list gives only `acc` the initial's unit.
- **Convert and the FC share one unit id space**; Convert applies its own format to a value with a unit; a lookup reads a united key column in its display unit.
- **An error value is the answer** of a filter's value and of a Decision Matrix's Weight cell, not a blank.
- Open: Convert units the FC doesn't list have no label (backlog).

### SESSION DIGEST (2026-10-21d: TS fixes from the solenoid/tsrs port; cloud session)
- **Found while porting the formula core to Rust** (branch `solenoid/tsrs`), fixed here too:
  - **Formats:** a lazy node's own column format never reached downstream; it now rides the plan as a `formats` step ([[D41]] formatFlowsDownstream).
  - **Split Column** splits each cell's display text, so a date splits as its date.
  - **Shapers memoized:** FILTER, UNIQUE, SORT, SORTBY, TAKE and DROP over a whole column keep their answer like a range call (2.8 s to 22 ms on 3,000 rows).
  - **Correlation:** Kendall's tau counts in O(n log n), and the matrix computes each pair once.
  - **Loops bounded by a typed number now answer an error at once** ([[C120]] linearWork): BINOM.INV (it now searches the CDF), the cumulative hypergeometric (it sums only where its terms are nonzero, to the bit), the exact tests, PADTEXT, VDB, CUMIPMT, CUMPRINC, DB, DDB, the Bessel functions, WRAPROWS, WRAPCOLS, NTILE and GAUSSIANSMOOTH.
  - **Formula.js overrides** ([[formulajs-divergences]]):
    - FACT, COMBIN, PERMUT and MULTINOMIAL multiply without recursing (`COMBIN(30, 15)` is exact);
    - BINOM.DIST.RANGE checks its range;
    - CLEAN reads a number as text and keeps DEL;
    - HASH refuses inherited keys.
  - **Throws:** a function that throws answers `#VALUE!` at its call, so IFERROR reads it.
- **How they were found:** a sweep that calls every function with extreme and ordinary arguments, timing each call and catching throws.

### SESSION DIGEST (2026-10-21b: hangs and a SUM limit found by the `solenoid/tsrs` Rust port; cloud session)
- **SUM is owned** (`sumLikeFx`, [[formulajs-divergences]]): Formula.js's SUM spreads a list into `apply`, which throws past about 125k values, so `SUM(column)` over a long table threw in every row and re-summed per row. Pinned in `formulaDivergence.test.ts`.
- **Four hangs now answer `#DOMAIN!`:** NETWORKDAYS with an end past the years 1 to 9999 (an infinite date never left the day loop), WORKDAY with such a start (past 2^53 a step doesn't move the day, so a start that's also a holiday looped), BINOM.INV with an infinite trial count and HYPGEOM.DIST's cumulative form with an infinite count (both summed NaN forever). Pinned in `datetimeParity.test.ts` and `distributionFormula.test.ts`.
- **Open:** TEXT with a date code writes Formula.js's ISO date whatever the code (`TEXT(d, "mmm yyyy")` is `2026-03-15`), where the divergences spec says it formats through the UTC getters. Needs a call: route date codes through `formatDateSerial`, or correct the spec.

### SESSION DIGEST (2026-10-16: the Choice Ladder and the decision domain; cloud session)
- **Choice Ladder node** ([[C122]] choiceLadder, unratified; mechanics [[frame-verbs#Choice Ladder]], figure [[chart-figures]] § The ladder figure): orders a Decision Matrix's options into the sequence to take them in when you can't do everything. Limits take an At most, an At least (a floor, such as two vegetarian places) or neither (a rough cost such as political capital, ordered by value for what it costs). Requires, Excludes, Must and Never columns link and force options. Close places fork, the flow splitting by score; a pick at a fork is saved on the node (`choices`) and the passed side stays dimmed and clickable. The Plan carries running totals.
- **The figure is a Sankey of the value served**: cards take their slice, stroked ribbons keep their thickness sideways, a merge that forks again meets in one knot, lanes never cross (`ladderLayout.test.ts`).
- **The Decision Matrix family grew** (the author's "chained decisions and choice multi-select" loop): a blank scores as the criterion's median and every guess is named in Filled in ([[C123]] decisionBlanks); At least / At most deal-breakers rule options out before weighing, a limit-only row being a screen ([[C124]] dealBreakers); Range normalize; Sensitivity's Flip points mode ([[C125]] flipPoints); a Cube from Nest Join ranks as a chain of choices ([[C126]] chainedDecisions); the ladder gained floors, Must / Never / One of and running totals. Seeds: `apartment-hunt.json`, `hiring-team.json`.
- **How it was checked:** seven critic-agent review rounds until one returned END STATE; a 64-scenario intuition battery written apart from the engine (`decisionIntuition.test.ts`, misses the math is right about carry a `ruling`), which also chose the default scale (÷Max missed 3, Range 9, Rank 10); and property tests holding the ladder's plans and forks to exhaustive search on random problems (`ladderProperties.test.ts`).
- **Ruled out:** sequencing by period (C122's reopen condition), pairwise (AHP) weights, Monte Carlo weight jiggling (flip points answer the same question).
- **dev-up.mjs** stops and restarts the dev server by PID (`--stop`, `--restart`); `pkill -f` matched the caller's own shell.

### SESSION DIGEST (2026-10-12: motion graphics from the app's own geometry; cloud session)
- **Motion clips rebuild the graph from what the app draws** ([[C121]] motionFromAppGeometry, under [[B3]]; the `motion` skill, `scripts/motion/`). `capture.mjs` reads every card off the live page as paint-ordered pieces (boxes, border rings, SVG frames/sockets/icons, mask and background SVGs, glyphs at Chrome's pen positions, select and input values), plus cables and one reference shot per state; `export.py` triangulates them (shapely, the app's own woff2 fonts) and matches pieces across states; a three.js player renders in headless Chromium (software GL, ~1 s a 1080p frame at 2 samples), straight into ffmpeg. Every clip ends at rest and `render.mjs --check` holds that frame against the app's screenshot (~1% of pixels off by more than 16 levels, all anti-aliasing).
- **Direction from the author:** the graph is the product, so shoot it straight on and assemble cards quietly; causes come before effects (`kit.causal`: one beat of lag per cable hop, a pulse landing as the next card updates); cards start blank; inputs are typed, wired, picked or edited, not slid; big type carries the story. Comparison clips do one task in a generic sheet (`sheet.py`, its own state timeline) and then in Solenoid (`kit.compare`): `formula`, `switch`, `add-row` (the real table popup), `mistake`; plus `typing` and `table`. Recordings, not final renders, for now; `reel.mjs` cuts them.
- **Gotchas found:** hiding the canvas backing changes how Chrome antialiases card text, so plates are never transparent screenshots; CSS paint order (positioned after flow, auto-z children join the parent stack) and per-state paint order both matter; Blender/Cycles was ~15 s a frame and is gone. Charts draw to `<canvas>` and can't be read.
