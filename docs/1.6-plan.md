## What 1.6 already is

1.6 is the engine release: everything a document computes runs on Solenoid's own Rust core, in
WebAssembly ([[C130]] rustCore). Built and on `develop` since the v1.5.0 tag:

- **One engine, in Rust.** The TypeScript engine, Polars and rete are gone; the graph runs on
  netcore-rs; card bodies run on the core; exact sums; linear work at 800k rows; cards held to
  their formulas and Excel answers checked in the author's Excel.
- **Dates are a value kind** ([[D114]] dateIsAKind, [[C137]] datesNeverSerials), InlineCast
  ([[D113]] inlineCast), wildcards typed by their values ([[D117]], [[C136]] typesKnownAfterCalc).
- **New cards and views:** Pivot view and Details, Dashboard (phone too), Cards, Record's figure
  in the card, XLookup with Filter's modes, XY annotations.
- **The Navigator as a workspace tree** ([[C132]] workspaceTree), composite drill-in on a phone.
- **One file and folder picker**; **safe to share** ([[B18]] safeToShare); Write to Obsidian
  writes Solenoid Properties types ([[D116]]); the headless CLI builds (unpublished).

## Round 1.5 (decisions from the author first)

- **F8** COUNTIFS, SUMIFS and the other criterion functions take an operator plus a typed value
  in place of criterion text ([[D123]] typedCriteria), and the Comparison card's ops are functions
  in the Sets pack ([[D124]] comparisonFunctions). **Done 2026-11-04.**
- **F8's follow-ons**: the Comparison and Between cards take any value; a value keyed into a
  card's wildcard slot is read as the kind its type button shows, never guessed; one field per
  unwired input, decided by the core's table, with saves pruned to it
  ([[inline-literal-maps#One field per input]]); Value Input's type button rides its field, and
  Value Input, Constant, Physics Constant and TODAY/NOW collapse to their value cell. **Done
  2026-11-05.**
- **F12** The Navigator: the author's five calls in bundle 26. **Decided 2026-11-06** ([[C132]] workspaceTree);
  the one to build, a new document written at once on desktop, waits for the desktop session.
- **P7** Card vs formula on blank, `""` and NaN, and the text leftovers (backlog § Value
  handling, the author's walk, tracked in [`p7-walk.md`](p7-walk.md)). Built 2026-11-05: every empty card
  field declares its kind and the formula's empty slot agrees with it ([[D96]] emptySlotShowsItsValue), empty

  cards, PTP, IQR, MAD, SEM, CV and RMS refusing text in formulas as on cards. Next: a cabled blank, a formula
